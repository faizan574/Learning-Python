## 📘 Subject 1: Indian Constitution & Polity
### 🎯 Local Government: 73rd & 74th Amendments; Telangana Panchayat Raj

The **73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendment Acts (1992)** gave **constitutional status** to rural and urban local self-government — the "third tier" of Indian federalism.

### 📖 Concept

**73rd Amendment (1992) — Panchayati Raj.** Added **Part IX (Art 243–243O)** and the **Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects)**.
- **Three-tier system** (village, intermediate, district) — intermediate tier optional for states with population < 20 lakh.
- **Gram Sabha** as the foundation (`Art 243A`).
- **Elections every 5 years**; if dissolved early, elections within **6 months**. Conducted by the **State Election Commission (Art 243K)**.
- **Reservations:** for **SC/ST (proportional to population)** and **at least 1/3 for women** (many states, incl. Telangana, made it **50%**).
- **State Finance Commission (Art 243I)** every 5 years for local-body finances.
- **Compulsory (mandatory)** vs **voluntary** provisions.

**74th Amendment (1992) — Municipalities.** Added **Part IXA (Art 243P–243ZG)** and the **Twelfth Schedule (18 subjects)**.
- Three types: **Nagar Panchayat** (transitional area), **Municipal Council** (smaller urban), **Municipal Corporation** (larger urban).
- **Ward Committees**, reservations (SC/ST/women), **5-year term**, **District & Metropolitan Planning Committees**.

**Telangana Panchayat Raj Act, 2018.** The state's own law updating PR institutions — three tiers (**Gram Panchayat, Mandal Praja Parishad, Zilla Praja Parishad**), regular elections, and provisions on functions/finances. *(Verify the latest amendments/structure and reservation levels — e.g., 33% vs 50% women, BC reservation — before the exam.)*

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — The **73rd Amendment (Part IX, 11th Schedule, 29 subjects)** = **Panchayati Raj**; the **74th Amendment (Part IXA, 12th Schedule, 18 subjects)** = **Municipalities**. Both mandate **5-year terms, SC/ST + 1/3 women reservation, State Election Commission & State Finance Commission**.

💡 **Example:** If a Gram Panchayat is dissolved, a fresh election **must** be held within **6 months** — local democracy can't be indefinitely suspended, unlike the older discretionary system. That is the "constitutional status" the 73rd Amendment guarantees.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **73rd = Panchayats = Part IX = 11th Schedule = 29 subjects**; **74th = Municipalities = Part IXA = 12th Schedule = 18 subjects**.
- **State Election Commission (243K)**; **State Finance Commission (243I)**; **5-yr term, 6-month re-election rule**.
- **1/3 women reservation** minimum (Telangana **50%**); **SC/ST proportional**.
- **Three urban bodies:** Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**73 = Rural (Part IX, 11th Sch, 29 subjects); 74 = Urban (Part IXA, 12th Sch, 18 subjects).** Both: 5-yr term, ⅓ women, State EC + State FC."

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Don't swap the schedules: **11th = Panchayats (29), 12th = Municipalities (18)**. Local-body **elections are conducted by the STATE Election Commission**, not the central ECI.

**✅ Quick self-check:** Which amendment/schedule governs Panchayats, and who conducts local-body elections?
*(73rd Amendment, Part IX, 11th Schedule (29 subjects); the State Election Commission.)*

**Gruha Jyothi — free electricity guarantee.**
- **200 units of free electricity per month** to eligible domestic households (verify eligibility criteria — consumption threshold, category).

## 🏰 Subject 3: Telangana History
### 🎯 The Statehood Movement 2001–2014 (TRS, JACs, Agitations, Sri Krishna Committee)

The final push: from the **founding of the TRS (2001)** to the **formation of Telangana (2 June 2014)** — a decade of agitations, committees and political manoeuvre.

### 📖 Concept

**Founding & early phase.**
- **Telangana Rashtra Samithi (TRS)** founded **27 April 2001** by **K. Chandrashekar Rao (KCR)**, who resigned from the TDP, making **separate statehood** the single agenda. (Later renamed **BRS**.)
- The **Telangana Development Forum**, intellectuals, students, employees and cultural figures (**Gaddar**, professors) built a mass base; the demand entered mainstream politics.

**Key agitations & milestones.**
- **2004:** TRS allied with Congress (UPA); Telangana included in the **Common Minimum Programme**; a **Second SRC** demand.
- **2009 (Nov–Dec):** **KCR's fast-unto-death** (Nov 2009) forced the Centre's hand; on **9 December 2009**, Home Minister **P. Chidambaram announced** the process for Telangana's formation — but it was **paused (23 Dec 2009)** after Andhra backlash.
- **Joint Action Committees (JACs)** — the **Telangana JAC (Prof. Kodandaram)** coordinated cross-party agitations: **Sakala Janula Samme** (2011 general strike, ~42 days), **Million March, Sagara Haram, rail/road rokos**.
- **Osmania University** was a movement epicentre; several **student self-immolations** marked the period.

**Sri Krishna Committee (2010).** The Centre appointed the **Justice B. N. Srikrishna Committee** (Feb 2010) to examine the demand; its **report (Dec 2010)** gave **six options** (ranging from status quo, to a united AP with safeguards, to bifurcation with Hyderabad as a Union Territory or joint capital, to separate Telangana). It **favoured** keeping the state united with a statutory Telangana Regional Council as a "workable option," but listed **separation** as the option that best met aspirations — inconclusive, keeping agitation alive.

**Formation.**
- **30 July 2013:** the **Congress Working Committee/UPA** resolved to create Telangana.
- **February 2014:** Parliament passed the **Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014**.
- **2 June 2014:** **Telangana** became India's **29th state**, with **KCR** as the first Chief Minister and **Hyderabad** as a **shared capital** (with AP) for up to **10 years**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **TRS founded 2001 (KCR)** → **KCR fast + Chidambaram's 9 Dec 2009 announcement** → **JAC (Kodandaram) agitations (Sakala Janula Samme 2011)** → **Sri Krishna Committee (2010, six options)** → **AP Reorganisation Act 2014** → **Telangana formed 2 June 2014 (29th state, KCR CM)**.

💡 **Example:** The **9 December 2009** announcement and its **pause on 23 December** is the movement's turning point — the reversal triggered the **JAC-led mass agitations** (Sakala Janula Samme etc.) that ultimately made statehood irreversible.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **TRS — 27 April 2001 — KCR**.
- **9 Dec 2009** Chidambaram announcement; **KCR fast**.
- **Telangana JAC — Prof. Kodandaram**; **Sakala Janula Samme (2011)**.
- **Sri Krishna Committee (2010) — six options**.
- **Formation: 2 June 2014, 29th state, AP Reorganisation Act 2014, Hyderabad shared capital (10 yrs)**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**TRS 2001 → KCR fast + 9 Dec 2009 → JAC (Kodandaram) & Sakala Janula Samme (2011) → Sri Krishna 6 options (2010) → AP Reorg Act 2014 → 2 June 2014, 29th state.**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Telangana formed on 2 June 2014** (29th state), via the **AP Reorganisation Act 2014** — not in 2009 (that was only an announcement, later paused). The **Sri Krishna Committee** gave **six options** and did **not** unambiguously recommend a separate state.

**✅ Quick self-check:** When was the TRS founded and when did Telangana form, and who chaired the committee examining the demand?
*(TRS: 27 April 2001; Telangana formed 2 June 2014; Justice B. N. Srikrishna chaired the committee (2010).)*

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4: Indian National Movement (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision: The Revolt of 1857

Beginning the **INM revision pass**, today reconsolidates the **Revolt of 1857** (originally Day 1) — the "First War of Independence."

### 📖 Concept

**Causes** — political (Doctrine of Lapse, annexations — Awadh 1856), economic (peasant/artisan ruin, drain of wealth), social-religious (fear for religion, reforms), military (grievances of sepoys), and the immediate spark: the **greased cartridges** (Enfield rifle, cow/pig fat) of the new rifle.

**Outbreak & spread.**
- **Mangal Pandey** (Barrackpore, **29 March 1857**) — early flashpoint.
- **Meerut, 10 May 1857** — the real outbreak; sepoys marched to **Delhi** and proclaimed **Bahadur Shah Zafar** the symbolic leader.

**Centres & leaders:**

| Centre | Leader |
|---|---|
| Delhi | Bahadur Shah Zafar (nominal); Bakht Khan (real) |
| Kanpur | Nana Sahib; Tantia Tope |
| Lucknow (Awadh) | Begum Hazrat Mahal |
| Jhansi | Rani Lakshmibai |
| Bihar (Jagdishpur) | Kunwar Singh |
| Bareilly | Khan Bahadur Khan |
| Faizabad | Maulvi Ahmadullah |

**Suppression & results.**
- Crushed by mid-1858; **Bahadur Shah Zafar** exiled to **Rangoon**.
- **Government of India Act 1858:** ended **Company rule**; **Crown** took over; the **Board of Control + Court of Directors** replaced by a **Secretary of State for India** + India Council; Governor-General became **Viceroy**.
- **Queen's Proclamation (1858)**; end of **Doctrine of Lapse**; reorganisation of the **army** (more British troops, divide-and-rule).

**Nature (debated):** called a mere "sepoy mutiny" by some, a "first war of independence" (**V. D. Savarkar**) by others; largely **north-Indian, feudal-led**, lacking pan-Indian unity and modern leadership.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — The **Revolt of 1857** began at **Meerut (10 May 1857)**, sparked by **greased cartridges**; led (region-wise) by **Nana Sahib/Tantia Tope (Kanpur), Rani Lakshmibai (Jhansi), Begum Hazrat Mahal (Lucknow), Kunwar Singh (Bihar)**. It ended **Company rule** via the **Government of India Act 1858** (Crown rule, Viceroy, Secretary of State).

💡 **Example:** The revolt's immediate cause (**greased cartridges**) crystallised deep grievances; its biggest consequence was **structural** — the **1858 Act** transferred India from the **Company to the Crown**, creating the Viceroy and Secretary of State.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Meerut, 10 May 1857**; Mangal Pandey (29 March, Barrackpore).
- **Leader-centre pairs** (Jhansi–Lakshmibai, Kanpur–Nana Sahib/Tantia Tope, Lucknow–Hazrat Mahal, Bihar–Kunwar Singh).
- **GoI Act 1858:** Crown rule, **Viceroy**, **Secretary of State for India**.
- **Savarkar** called it the "First War of Independence."

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Cartridges → Meerut (10 May 1857) → Delhi (Bahadur Shah) → Jhansi/Kanpur/Lucknow/Bihar leaders → crushed 1858 → Crown rule (Viceroy + Secretary of State).**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The revolt's outbreak was **Meerut, 10 May 1857** (Mangal Pandey at Barrackpore on 29 March was the precursor). The **Government of India Act 1858** (not 1857) transferred power to the **Crown**.

**✅ Quick self-check:** Where and when did the 1857 revolt break out, and what was its biggest constitutional consequence?
*(Meerut, 10 May 1857; the Government of India Act 1858 ended Company rule and began Crown rule.)*

## 🗺️ Subject 5: Telangana Geography (with Map)
### 🎯 Area Focus: Medak–Sangareddy–Siddipet

The **Medak–Sangareddy–Siddipet** belt of north-central Telangana blends **pharma/industry (Patancheru)**, the **Kaleshwaram** reservoirs, and Kakatiya-era temple heritage.

**🗺️ Reference map — Telangana regions (Medak-Sangareddy-Siddipet, north-central):**

![Telangana regions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/faizan574/Learning-Python/main/tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_regions.svg)

*(If the map doesn't display in email, open the lesson on GitHub — the SVG is at `tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_regions.svg`.)*

### 📖 Concept

**Location & administration.** The undivided Medak district (north-central, adjoining Hyderabad's northwest) was reorganised (2016) into **Medak, Sangareddy, and Siddipet** districts (`verify`). The belt borders **Karnataka/Maharashtra** to the west and the **Hyderabad** urban region to the southeast.

**Physiography & geology.**
- **Deccan plateau, granite–gneiss** with **black and red soils**; drained by the **Manjira** (a Godavari tributary) and the **Godavari's** Kaleshwaram-linked reservoirs.
- **Nizam Sagar** (on the Manjira, Kamareddy/Medak side) and the huge **Mallanna Sagar** reservoir (Kaleshwaram, Siddipet) lie in/near this belt.
- **Singur dam** on the Manjira (Sangareddy) — a key drinking-water source for Hyderabad.

**Industry & economy (the identity).**
- **Patancheru–Bollaram (Sangareddy)** — a major **pharmaceutical & chemical industrial** cluster near Hyderabad (also an **industrial-pollution** concern — Nakkavagu/Peddavagu).
- **Medak** — historically an agricultural district; **Siddipet** — paddy under Kaleshwaram; **Sangareddy** — industry + education (**IIT Hyderabad is at Kandi, Sangareddy**).
- **Gollabhama sarees** — the famous **Siddipet handloom** (GI) depicting the "Gollabhama" (milkmaid) motif.

**Heritage & sites (cultural reference):**
- **Medak Cathedral** — one of Asia's largest churches (Gothic, built by British missionaries, early 20th c.) — a major landmark.
- **Medak Fort** (Kakatiya-era hill fort).
- **Kondapur (Sangareddy)** — a **Satavahana-era** archaeological site & museum.
- **Edupayala** (Medak) — Vana Durga temple (jatara); **Komuravelli Mallanna** temple (Siddipet) — a major folk pilgrimage.
- **Pocharam** wildlife sanctuary & lake (Medak).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Medak-Sangareddy-Siddipet = **Patancheru pharma cluster + IIT Hyderabad (Kandi)** on the **Manjira/Kaleshwaram** waters (Singur, Mallanna Sagar), with **Siddipet Gollabhama sarees**, **Medak Cathedral** (one of Asia's largest churches), **Medak Fort** and **Kondapur (Satavahana)** heritage.

💡 **Example:** "Geology + culture of Medak" → **Manjira/Kaleshwaram irrigation + Patancheru pharma industry** (geology/economy) with **Medak Cathedral + Gollabhama sarees + Komuravelli Mallanna** (culture) — the area-focus pairing.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Patancheru–Bollaram = pharma/chemical cluster** (+ pollution issue).
- **IIT Hyderabad = Kandi, Sangareddy**.
- **Siddipet Gollabhama sarees (GI)**; **Medak Cathedral** (Gothic, Asia's largest churches).
- **Singur (Manjira) & Mallanna Sagar (Kaleshwaram)** reservoirs; **Kondapur (Satavahana)**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Medak belt = Pharma (Patancheru) + IIT (Kandi) + Gollabhama sarees (Siddipet) + Medak Cathedral & Fort; watered by Manjira (Singur) & Kaleshwaram (Mallanna Sagar).**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Medak Cathedral** is a **church** (Gothic, among Asia's largest), distinct from **Medak Fort**. **IIT Hyderabad is at Kandi (Sangareddy)**. **Gollabhama sarees = Siddipet**. Verify the reorganised district list.
