## 🏛️ Polity: DPSP (Art 36-51) & Fundamental Duties (51A)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP), Part IV, Art `36-51`** — borrowed from the **Irish Constitution** — are **non-justiciable** (not enforceable in court, Art 37) but **fundamental in governance**; the State should apply them in making laws. They aim at a **welfare state** and **socio-economic justice**.

**Classification (by content):**
- **Socialistic** — Art 38 (welfare, minimise inequalities), 39 (adequate means of livelihood, equal pay, wealth not concentrated), **39A** (equal justice & free legal aid), 41 (right to work, education, public assistance), 42 (just conditions of work, maternity relief), 43 (living wage), **43A** (workers' participation in management), 47 (nutrition, standard of living, prohibition).
- **Gandhian** — Art 40 (**village panchayats**), 43 (cottage industries), **43B** (co-operative societies), 46 (SC/ST/weaker sections), 47 (prohibition of intoxicants), 48 (organise agriculture & animal husbandry, cow protection).
- **Liberal-Intellectual** — Art 44 (**Uniform Civil Code**), 45 (early childhood care <6, post-86th Amdt), 48 (modern agriculture), **48A** (environment, forests, wildlife), 49 (protection of monuments), 50 (**separation of judiciary from executive**), 51 (international peace).

**FR vs DPSP:** In conflicts, **Minerva Mills (1980)** held the **balance** between FRs and DPSP is part of the basic structure; Art 31C (via 25th Amdt) protects laws giving effect to Art 39(b)/(c) even if they violate Art 14/19.

**Fundamental Duties (Art 51A, Part IVA)** — added by the **42nd Amendment, 1976** on the **Swaran Singh Committee** recommendation (originally **10**; the **11th** — duty of parents/guardians to provide education to children 6-14 — added by the **86th Amendment, 2002**). They are **non-justiciable** moral obligations of citizens (respect the Constitution/flag/anthem, cherish the freedom struggle, protect sovereignty, defend the country, promote harmony, preserve heritage, protect environment, develop scientific temper, safeguard public property, strive for excellence, provide education).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — DPSP = **Part IV, Art 36-51**, from **Ireland**, **non-justiciable** (Art 37). Art 40 (panchayats), 44 (UCC), 48A (environment), 50 (judiciary-executive separation). **Fundamental Duties = Art 51A**, added by **42nd Amdt (1976)**, now **11** (11th by 86th Amdt).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — DPSP and Fundamental Duties are both **non-justiciable**. Fundamental Duties are in **Part IVA (Art 51A)**, added by the **42nd Amendment** (Swaran Singh Committee); the **11th** duty came via the **86th Amendment (2002)**.

## 🎁 Telangana Schemes: Musi Rejuvenation, HYDRAA & Urban Governance

### 📖 Deep Dive

Two flagship **urban governance** initiatives in Hyderabad:

1. **Musi Riverfront Development / Rejuvenation** — a major project to clean and redevelop the heavily polluted **Musi river** (a Krishna tributary flowing through Hyderabad). It aims at **sewage treatment (interception & diversion), riverfront beautification, resettlement of encroachments, and STPs**, inspired by riverfront models (e.g. Sabarmati). It involves relocation of families in the buffer/river zone (rehabilitation is a sensitive issue) — *verify latest project cost, phases and rehabilitation status before the exam.*

2. **HYDRAA (Hyderabad Disaster Response and Assets Protection Agency)** — a state agency (formed **2024**) to protect **government/public assets, lakes (FTL — Full Tank Level), parks and nala buffer zones** from encroachment, and to strengthen **urban disaster response** (flooding). HYDRAA has undertaken high-profile **demolitions of encroachments on lake beds/buffer zones** — a significant and debated governance measure (*verify latest jurisdiction/legal status*).

Both connect to broader urban challenges: unplanned growth, lake encroachment, flooding (the 2020 Hyderabad floods spurred such measures), and the ORR/RRR expansion.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Musi rejuvenation** = clean/redevelop the polluted **Musi** river (STPs, riverfront, resettlement). **HYDRAA (2024)** = Hyderabad Disaster Response and Assets Protection Agency — protects **lakes (FTL), parks, buffer zones** and handles urban disaster/flood response; known for encroachment **demolitions**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **HYDRAA** = Hyderabad **Disaster Response and Assets Protection Agency** (asset protection + disaster response), not a welfare-cash scheme. **Musi** is a **Krishna** tributary. Rehabilitation of displaced families is the sensitive angle.

## 🏰 Telangana History: The Satavahanas (Round 3)

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **Satavahanas** (also **Andhras/Andhrabhrityas**) were the first major indigenous dynasty of the Deccan (c. **2nd century BCE – 3rd century CE**), rising after the Mauryas. Telangana was a core region — **Kotilingala (Karimnagar/Jagtial)** is considered an early capital/mint site; other capitals: **Pratishthana (Paithan)** and **Dhanyakataka/Amaravati**.

**Founder & key rulers:**
- **Simuka** — founder.
- **Satakarni I** — early expansionist (performed Ashvamedha; mentioned with the **Naneghat** inscription of his queen **Nayanika**).
- **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (c. 1st-2nd c. CE) — the **greatest** ruler; defeated the **Western Kshatrapa (Shaka) Nahapana**, restruck his coins; described in the **Nasik Prashasti** by his mother **Gautami Balashri** as destroyer of Shakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas, and upholder of **varna** order.
- **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** — successor; **Amaravati** stupa flourished.
- **Yajna Sri Satakarni** — last great ruler; ship-motif coins (maritime trade).

**Administration & economy:** matronymic names (Gautamiputra, Vasishthiputra) though **patriarchal** succession; land grants (**agrahara**) to Brahmins (early instance of tax-free religious grants); flourishing **Roman trade** (coins, ports); guilds (**shreni**).

**Culture:** patronised **Prakrit** (official language, **Gatha Saptasati** attributed to **Hala**) and Buddhism (Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda art) as well as Brahmanism (Vedic sacrifices). The **Amaravati school** of art flourished.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Satavahanas: founder **Simuka**; greatest **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (defeated **Nahapana**; **Nasik Prashasti** by Gautami Balashri). Early site **Kotilingala** (Telangana). **Prakrit** language, **Hala's Gatha Saptasati**, Amaravati art, Roman trade, matronymic names.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Satavahanas used **matronymic** names (mother's name prefix) but succession was **patrilineal**. **Gautamiputra Satakarni** defeated **Nahapana** (Shaka); the eulogy is the **Nasik Prashasti** (by his mother Gautami Balashri).

### 💡 Example

*Q: Who composed the Nasik Prashasti eulogising Gautamiputra Satakarni?* → His mother **Gautami Balashri**.

## 🇮🇳 Indian National Movement: Integration of Princely States (incl. Hyderabad) & Making of the Republic

### 📖 Deep Dive

At independence, **~565 princely states** had to accede to India or Pakistan (or theoretically stay independent). **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** (the "Iron Man") with **V. P. Menon** (Secretary, States Department) achieved the integration through the **Instrument of Accession** and persuasion, invoking geography and public sentiment. Most acceded by **15 August 1947** on three subjects (defence, external affairs, communications).

**Difficult cases:**
- **Junagadh** — a Muslim Nawab acceded to Pakistan despite a Hindu-majority population; a **plebiscite (1948)** confirmed accession to India.
- **Kashmir** — Maharaja Hari Singh signed the **Instrument of Accession (26 Oct 1947)** after a Pakistani tribal invasion; India sent troops.
- **Hyderabad** — the **Nizam (Mir Osman Ali Khan)** sought independence; after a **Standstill Agreement (1947)** broke down and **Razakar** violence (Kasim Razvi) grew, India launched **"Operation Polo" / Police Action (13-17 September 1948)**, and Hyderabad was integrated (**Major General J. N. Chaudhuri** led; **17 Sep 1948** — now observed as Telangana Praja Palana Dinam / Liberation Day).

**Making of the Republic:** After integration, states were reorganised. The **Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950**, making India a **Republic** with **Rajendra Prasad** as first President. Later, the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** redrew boundaries on a **linguistic** basis (on **Fazl Ali Commission / SRC** recommendation), forming **Andhra Pradesh** (merging Telangana of Hyderabad State with Andhra) — a decision that later fed the Telangana movement.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Integration by **Sardar Patel + V. P. Menon** (Instrument of Accession, ~565 states). Hard cases: **Junagadh (plebiscite), Kashmir (26 Oct 1947), Hyderabad (Operation Polo, 13-17 Sep 1948, J. N. Chaudhuri)**. Republic **26 Jan 1950**; **SRC 1956** (linguistic) formed Andhra Pradesh.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Hyderabad was integrated by **Operation Polo (Police Action), Sep 1948**, **after** independence (not on 15 Aug 1947). **Patel + V. P. Menon** drove integration. The **SRC (1956)** was linguistic, forming AP (Telangana + Andhra).

### 💡 Example

*Q: By what military action was Hyderabad integrated into India, and when?* → **Operation Polo (Police Action), 13-17 September 1948.**

## 🗺️ Telangana Geography: Area Focus — Mahabubnagar / Palamuru

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Palamuru (Mahabubnagar)** is the southern gateway of Telangana — historically one of the **largest districts** (since reorganised into Mahabubnagar, Nagarkurnool, Wanaparthy, Jogulamba Gadwal, Narayanpet).

**Physiography & geology:** part of the **Deccan plateau**, dominated by **Archaean granites-gneisses**; semi-arid, **drought-prone** (rain-shadow), with the **Krishna** and its tributaries (**Tungabhadra, Bhima, Dindi**) along the south/west. The **Krishna enters Telangana** here (Jurala).

**Rivers & irrigation:** **Priyadarshini Jurala** (first major project on the Krishna in the state), **Koil Sagar**, **RDS (Rajolibanda Diversion Scheme)**, and the mega **Palamuru-Rangareddy Lift Irrigation Scheme** designed to lift Krishna water to the parched uplands. **Srisailam** backwaters lie to the south.

**Culture & heritage:** **Alampur** (Jogulamba Gadwal) — the **"Western Gateway of Srisailam,"** site of the **Navabrahma temples** (9 Badami-Chalukya-era Shiva temples) and the **Jogulamba Shakti Peetha** (one of the 18 Maha Shakti Peethas). **Gadwal** sarees (GI); **Pillalamarri** (a giant ~700-year-old banyan tree near Mahabubnagar town); **Koilkonda & Gadwal forts**.

**Socio-economic:** historically high **out-migration/labour migration** (Palamuru labour) due to drought and low irrigation; the lift schemes aim to reverse this.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Palamuru (Mahabubnagar): granite-gneiss, **drought-prone** rain-shadow; **Krishna enters at Jurala** (first project); **Palamuru-Rangareddy LIS**. **Alampur** = Navabrahma temples + **Jogulamba Shakti Peetha** ("Western Gateway of Srisailam"). **Gadwal** sarees; **Pillalamarri** banyan.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Alampur** (Jogulamba Shakti Peetha, Navabrahma temples) is in this region — a very common question. **Jurala** = first Krishna project in Telangana; Palamuru is the **drought-prone/labour-migration** belt.
