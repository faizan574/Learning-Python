## 🏛️ Polity: President & Vice-President

### 📖 Deep Dive

**President (Art 52-62)** — the constitutional **head of state**; executive power vests in the President (Art 53), exercised on the **aid and advice** of the Council of Ministers (Art 74; advice made **binding** by the 42nd Amendment, with a one-time return option added by the 44th).

**Election (Art 54-55):** elected **indirectly** by an **electoral college** of elected members of both Houses of Parliament + elected members of **State (and UT: Delhi, Puducherry) Legislative Assemblies**, by **proportional representation with a single transferable vote** and **secret ballot**. The value of votes uses population (1971 census, frozen) — ensuring **uniformity among states** and **parity between Union and states**.

- **Term:** 5 years; eligible for re-election. **Qualifications:** citizen, 35+, qualified for Lok Sabha, not holding office of profit.
- **Impeachment (Art 61):** only for **"violation of the Constitution"**; either House can initiate, needs a **special majority (2/3 of total membership)** in both Houses; the other House investigates. (Nominated members can participate — unlike the presidential *election*.)
- **Powers:** executive, legislative (summon/prorogue, dissolve LS, ordinances Art 123, assent to bills), judicial (**pardon — Art 72**), financial, diplomatic, military (supreme commander), emergency (Art 352/356/360).
- **Vacancy:** filled within **6 months**; the **Vice-President acts** meanwhile.

**Vice-President (Art 63-71)** — **ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha**. Elected by an **electoral college of both Houses of Parliament** (elected + **nominated** members) by PR-STV, secret ballot. Term 5 years; qualifications similar but **35+, qualified for Rajya Sabha**. Removed by a **Rajya Sabha resolution (effective majority)** agreed to by the **Lok Sabha** (no formal "impeachment" grounds needed).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — President: elected by an **electoral college (MPs + elected MLAs)**, PR-STV; impeachment for **violation of the Constitution** (2/3 special majority). **VP = ex-officio RS Chairman**, elected by **both Houses (incl. nominated)**; removed by RS effective-majority resolution + LS agreement.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — In the **President's election**, only **elected** members vote (no nominated members, no Legislative Council members). In the **VP's election**, **both Houses of Parliament** vote **including nominated** members (state legislatures don't participate).

### 💡 Example

*Q: Who participates in the Vice-President's election?* → **Both Houses of Parliament** (elected + nominated members) — **not** the state legislatures.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- President: Art 52-62; impeachment Art 61 (violation of Constitution).
- VP: Art 63-71; ex-officio RS Chairman.
- President election: elected MPs + elected MLAs (+ Delhi/Puducherry).
- Pardoning power: Art 72 (President), Art 161 (Governor).

## 🎁 Telangana Schemes: State Budget & Economic Survey Highlights

### 📖 Deep Dive

*(All figures here must be verified against the latest official Telangana Budget & Socio-Economic Outlook before the exam — budget numbers change every year.)*

**Budget basics for TGPSC:**
- The **State Budget** is presented annually by the **Finance Minister** in the Assembly under **Art 202 (Annual Financial Statement)**. It splits into **Revenue** and **Capital** accounts, and shows **Revenue Deficit/Surplus, Fiscal Deficit, and Primary Deficit**.
- **FRBM (Fiscal Responsibility & Budget Management)** norms cap the **fiscal deficit** (generally ~3% of GSDP, with flexibility) and target reducing debt/GSDP.
- **Telangana's economy** is **services-led** (Hyderabad IT/pharma), with a relatively **high per-capita income** (among the higher states — *verify latest*), and significant own-tax revenue (GST, stamps & registration, excise, motor vehicles).

**Socio-Economic Outlook / Economic Survey** — the annual document giving **GSDP growth, sectoral shares (agriculture/industry/services), per-capita income, human development indicators, and scheme outlays**. Recurring themes to remember (verify current data):
- **Services** contribute the **largest share** of GSDP; agriculture's share is smaller but employs many.
- Welfare (Six Guarantees) forms a large share of budget outlay.
- Key revenue sources and the debate on **debt/borrowings** and **committed expenditure** (salaries, pensions, interest).

For the exam, focus on **concepts** (types of deficit, FRBM, Art 202, revenue vs capital) rather than memorising volatile figures — but know Telangana is **services-driven with high per-capita income**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — State Budget = **Annual Financial Statement (Art 202)**; **Revenue vs Capital**; deficits (Revenue/Fiscal/Primary); **FRBM** caps fiscal deficit (~3% GSDP). Telangana = **services-led** economy, **high per-capita income** (verify latest). Economic Survey/Socio-Economic Outlook gives GSDP, sectoral shares.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Do **not** quote budget figures from memory — write "verify latest official figure." Know the **concepts**: Art 202, deficit types, FRBM, revenue vs capital expenditure, and that Telangana's GSDP is **services-dominated**.

## 🏰 Telangana History: Ikshvakus & Vishnukundins

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Ikshvakus (c. 3rd century CE)** — rose after the decline of the Satavahanas in the **Krishna-Guntur** region (their influence extended to parts of Telangana). Capital: **Vijayapuri (Nagarjunakonda)**.
- Great patrons of **Buddhism** — **Nagarjunakonda** and **Amaravati** flourished as major **Mahayana Buddhist** centres; royal women (e.g. **Chamtisri**) endowed monasteries (mahachaityas, viharas, universities).
- Also performed **Vedic sacrifices** (Ashvamedha) — a blend of Brahmanism and Buddhism. Rulers: **Vasishthiputra Chamtamula**, **Virapurushadatta**, **Ehuvala Chamtamula**.
- Nagarjunakonda (now largely submerged under the Nagarjuna Sagar reservoir, with relocated monuments on an island museum) is named after the Buddhist philosopher **Nagarjuna**.

**Vishnukundins (c. 5th-6th century CE)** — a significant dynasty ruling **Telangana and coastal Andhra** after the Ikshvakus/Vakatakas; often considered the **first major dynasty native to the Telangana region** proper.
- Capitals associated: **Indrapalanagara/Indrapuram (Nalgonda area)** and **Amaravati/Denduluru** (locations debated).
- Greatest ruler: **Madhavavarma II** — performed **Ashvamedha and other sacrifices**, expanded the kingdom.
- Patronised **Hinduism (Shaivism/Vaishnavism)** and Buddhism; issued copper-plate grants; associated with early **rock-cut cave temples** (e.g., **Mogalrajapuram, Undavalli** in the wider region) and the growth of Sanskrit/Telugu-region culture.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Ikshvakus** (capital **Vijayapuri/Nagarjunakonda**; Buddhist patrons — Chamtisri, Virapurushadatta). **Vishnukundins** (5th-6th c.; greatest **Madhavavarma II**; Telangana-based; Ashvamedha; cave temples). Nagarjunakonda submerged under **Nagarjuna Sagar**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Nagarjunakonda** = Ikshvaku capital (Vijayapuri), a great **Buddhist** centre, now under the **Nagarjuna Sagar** reservoir. **Madhavavarma II** was the greatest **Vishnukundin** ruler.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Which dynasty had its capital at Vijayapuri (Nagarjunakonda) and patronised Buddhism?* → The **Ikshvakus**.

- Vishnukundins: 5th-6th c.; Madhavavarma II; Ashvamedha.
- Nagarjunakonda under Nagarjuna Sagar reservoir.
- Blend of Buddhism & Brahmanism.

## 🇮🇳 Indian National Movement: Pre-Congress Associations & Socio-Religious Reform

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Pre-Congress political associations** (before 1885) built the ground for organised nationalism:
- **Landholders' Society (1838, Bengal)**, **British India Association (1851)**.
- **East India Association (1866, London)** — by **Dadabhai Naoroji**.
- **Poona Sarvajanik Sabha (1870)** — Ranade, G. V. Joshi.
- **Indian Association (1876, Calcutta)** — by **Surendranath Banerjee & Ananda Mohan Bose** (the most important pre-Congress body; opposed the reduced ICS age limit, organised the Indian National Conference).
- **Madras Mahajana Sabha (1884)**, **Bombay Presidency Association (1885)**.

**Socio-religious reform movements** (19th century):
- **Brahmo Samaj (1828, Raja Ram Mohan Roy)** — monotheism; campaigned against **Sati** (abolished 1829 under Bentinck); later Keshab Chandra Sen, Debendranath Tagore.
- **Prarthana Samaj (1867, Bombay; Atmaram Pandurang, Ranade)** — social reform (widow remarriage, caste).
- **Arya Samaj (1875, Swami Dayananda Saraswati)** — "**Back to the Vedas**," **Shuddhi** movement, education (DAV schools).
- **Ramakrishna Mission (1897, Swami Vivekananda)** — Vedanta, service; Vivekananda at the **1893 Chicago Parliament of Religions**.
- **Theosophical Society (Blavatsky & Olcott; Annie Besant in India)**.
- Muslim reform: **Aligarh Movement (Sir Syed Ahmed Khan)**; **Deoband**.
- **Jyotiba Phule** (Satyashodhak Samaj, 1873) — anti-caste, women's/Dalit education; **Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar** — **widow remarriage (1856)**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Key pre-Congress body: **Indian Association (1876, Surendranath Banerjee)**. Reformers: **Brahmo (Ram Mohan Roy, anti-Sati)**, **Arya Samaj (Dayananda, "back to Vedas")**, **Ramakrishna Mission (Vivekananda)**, **Aligarh (Sir Syed)**, **Phule (Satyashodhak)**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Raja Ram Mohan Roy → Brahmo Samaj (1828)**; **Dayananda → Arya Samaj (1875, "back to the Vedas")**; **Vivekananda → Ramakrishna Mission (1897)**. Don't mix founders. **Indian Association (1876)** > earlier bodies in importance.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Who founded the Arya Samaj and what was its slogan?* → **Swami Dayananda Saraswati (1875)**; "**Back to the Vedas**."

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- Indian Association 1876 — Surendranath Banerjee.
- Brahmo 1828 (Ram Mohan Roy), Prarthana 1867, Arya 1875.
- Ramakrishna Mission 1897 (Vivekananda); Chicago 1893.
- Phule (Satyashodhak 1873); Sir Syed (Aligarh).

## 🗺️ Telangana Geography: Area Focus — Adilabad & the Northern Gond Belt

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Adilabad** (now split into **Adilabad, Kumram Bheem Asifabad, Mancherial, Nirmal**) is Telangana's **northernmost** region — the **"Gateway to Telangana / South"** from Maharashtra.

**Physiography & climate:** hilly, forested (part of the **Satmala/Sahyadri outliers** and the northern plateau); the **wettest** part of Telangana (`1000+ mm`); the **Godavari** forms the southern boundary, with tributaries **Penganga, Pranahita, Kadam, Swarna**. **Kawal Tiger Reserve** and **Pranahita/Shivaram** sanctuaries lie here — the **teak-rich forest** heartland.

**Tribes & culture:** the **Gond** heartland (with **Kolams, Pardhans, Thotis, Naikpods**); festivals **Dandari-Gussadi** and the **Nagoba Jatara (Keslapur, Indervelli)**. **Komaram Bheem** — the Gond freedom-fighter icon ("**Jal, Jangal, Jameen**") who fought the Nizam; **Indervelli** is remembered for the **1981 tribal firing** incident. **Basar/Basara** (Gnana Saraswati temple) is in Nirmal district.

**Economy:** **Singareni coal** (Mancherial, Bellampalli, Ramagundam nearby), **cement** (limestone), **Nirmal toys/paintings** (GI), **teak & bamboo** forest produce, **cotton** (black-soil belt). **Kadam** and **SRSP** irrigation.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Adilabad = northernmost, **wettest**, **teak-forest & Gond heartland**; **Kawal Tiger Reserve**; **Komaram Bheem** (Jal-Jangal-Jameen), **Nagoba Jatara (Keslapur)**, **Gussadi** dance; **Nirmal** toys (GI); Godavier tributaries **Penganga/Pranahita/Kadam**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Adilabad is the **wettest** and most **forested/tribal (Gond)** region — northern, not southern. **Komaram Bheem** and **Nagoba Jatara (Keslapur/Indervelli)** belong here. Basara (Saraswati) is in **Nirmal** (carved from Adilabad).
