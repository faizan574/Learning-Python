## 🏛️ Subject 1 — Polity: Fundamental Rights — Articles 19-22

### Deep Dive

**Article 19 — Six freedoms** (to citizens), subject to **reasonable restrictions**:
- **19(1)(a)** speech & expression, **(b)** assembly (peaceful, unarmed), **(c)** association, **(d)** movement throughout India, **(e)** residence, **(g)** profession/occupation/trade.
- *(The former **(f) right to property** was deleted by the **44th Amendment, 1978** → now a legal right under **Art 300A**.)*

**Article 20 — Protection in respect of conviction:**
- **20(1)** no **ex-post-facto** law (no punishment under a law made after the act),
- **20(2)** no **double jeopardy** (no prosecution/punishment twice for the same offence),
- **20(3)** no **self-incrimination** (an accused can't be compelled to be a witness against himself).

**Article 21 — Protection of life & personal liberty.** "No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to **procedure established by law**." *Maneka Gandhi (1978)* read in **"due process"** (procedure must be **fair, just and reasonable**). Art 21 now houses many derived rights — **privacy** (*Puttaswamy, 2017*), clean environment, livelihood, speedy trial, etc.
- **Art 21A** — **Right to Education** (free & compulsory, ages `6-14`) — **86th Amendment, 2002**.

**Article 22 — Protection against arrest & detention.** For ordinary arrests: right to be **informed of grounds**, to **consult a lawyer**, and to be **produced before a magistrate within 24 hours**. **Preventive detention** has separate safeguards (advisory board, maximum periods).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Art 19: **six** freedoms (property deleted by **44th Amdt**). Art 20: **ex-post-facto, double jeopardy, self-incrimination**. Art 21 + *Maneka Gandhi* → **due process**; **21A** RTE (86th Amdt). Art 22: **24-hour** production rule + preventive-detention safeguards.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Art 20 and Art 21 **cannot be suspended even during an emergency** (44th Amendment). The "right to property" is **no longer a fundamental right** (Art 300A legal right). Art 19 freedoms are for **citizens**, not all persons.

💡 **Example:** *Maneka Gandhi (1978)* transformed Art 21 — a passport impounding must follow a **fair, just and reasonable** procedure, not mere "procedure established by law."

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Art 19: 6 freedoms (a,b,c,d,e,g); property → 300A (44th Amdt).
- Art 20: 3 protections; Art 20 & 21 non-suspendable in emergency.
- Art 21: due process (Maneka); 21A RTE (86th Amdt, 6-14).
- Art 22: 24-hour rule; preventive detention safeguards.

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Which two FRs cannot be suspended even during a National Emergency?* → **Articles 20 and 21** (44th Amendment).

## 🗳️ Subject 2 — Telangana Schemes & Incidents: Central Schemes in Telangana (PM-KISAN, Ayushman & Comparisons)

### Deep Dive

Several **Central (Union) schemes** operate in Telangana **alongside state schemes**, sometimes overlapping — a favourite "compare central vs state" area. *(Verify latest amounts, coverage and convergence status.)*

| Domain | Central scheme | Telangana (state) scheme |
|---|---|---|
| Farmer income support | **PM-KISAN** (`₹6,000/yr`) | **Rythu Bharosa / Rythu Bandhu** |
| Health insurance | **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** | **Rajiv Aarogyasri** |
| Housing | **PMAY** | **Indiramma Indlu / 2BHK** |
| LPG | **PM Ujjwala** | **Mahalakshmi ₹500 cylinder** |
| Piped water | **Jal Jeevan Mission** | **Mission Bhagiratha** |

**Key comparison points:**
- **PM-KISAN** gives `₹6,000/year` in three instalments to eligible farmer families; the **state** support (Rythu Bandhu earlier, Rythu Bharosa now) is **per-acre** and larger — the two can **stack**.
- **Ayushman/PM-JAY** and **Aarogyasri** cover hospitalisation; states may **converge** the two to raise cover (verify current integration).
- Overlaps raise questions of **duplication, targeting and cost-sharing** (many centrally-sponsored schemes are cost-shared, often **60:40** Centre:State).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **PM-KISAN** = `₹6,000/yr` (central) vs per-acre **Rythu Bharosa** (state); **PM-JAY** vs **Aarogyasri** (health); **PMAY** vs **Indiramma/2BHK**. Centrally-sponsored schemes are often **60:40** cost-shared.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Amounts, eligibility and convergence keep changing** — write **"verify latest official figure."** Distinguish **central (PM-KISAN, PM-JAY)** from **state (Rythu Bharosa, Aarogyasri)** schemes; questions test which is which.

💡 **Example:** A farmer may receive **PM-KISAN (`₹6,000/yr`, central)** *and* **Rythu Bharosa (state, per-acre)** — a classic "central + state stacking" illustration.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- PM-KISAN ₹6,000/yr (central); Rythu Bharosa per-acre (state).
- PM-JAY vs Aarogyasri; PMAY vs Indiramma/2BHK; Ujjwala vs Mahalakshmi LPG.
- Centrally-sponsored schemes: cost-shared (often 60:40).
- Always verify amounts/convergence.

## 🏰 Subject 3 — Telangana History: Festivals & Jataras

### Deep Dive

Telangana's cultural calendar is rich in **festivals and tribal jataras**:

- **Bathukamma** — the **state floral festival**, celebrated by women over **nine days** during **Navaratri (Sep-Oct)**, ending with **Saddula Bathukamma**; flowers are stacked in a conical arrangement and immersed. It is the cultural emblem of Telangana.
- **Bonalu** — worship of **Mahankali** in **Ashada masam (Jul-Aug)**; famous at **Golconda, Ujjaini Mahankali (Secunderabad), and Lal Darwaza (Old City)**; women carry **"bonam"** (offering pots).
- **Sammakka-Saralamma (Medaram) Jatara** — a **biennial** tribal fair in **Mulugu** district, honouring the **Koya** tribe's goddesses **Sammakka & Saralamma**; described as **one of the largest tribal congregations in Asia**.
- **Nagoba Jatara** — of the **Mesram clan** of the **Raj Gonds** at **Keslapur, Adilabad**; snake-god (Nagoba) worship, with the **Keslapur durbar**.
- **Peddagattu / Komuravelli** — **Lingamanthula Swamy** jatara (Nalgonda/Suryapet region) and **Komuravelli Mallanna** (Siddipet).
- **Sadar** — the **Yadav** community's buffalo festival in **Hyderabad**, after Diwali.
- **Moharram (Peerla Panduga)** — observed across Telangana with local syncretic traditions.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Bathukamma** = state floral festival (Navaratri, women, Saddula Bathukamma finale). **Bonalu** = Mahankali, Ashada masam. **Medaram (Sammakka-Saralamma)** = biennial **Koya** tribal jatara, Mulugu — among Asia's largest.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Medaram** is **biennial** (once every two years), **Koya** tribe, **Mulugu**. **Nagoba Jatara** is **Gond (Mesram clan), Keslapur/Adilabad** — don't confuse the two tribal jataras.

💡 **Example:** "Which is the biennial tribal jatara at Mulugu?" → **Sammakka-Saralamma (Medaram)** — a very common single-line PYQ.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Bathukamma: state festival, Navaratri, women; Saddula finale.
- Bonalu: Mahankali, Ashada; Golconda/Secunderabad/Lal Darwaza.
- Medaram: biennial, Koya, Mulugu.
- Nagoba: Gond (Mesram), Keslapur, Adilabad; Sadar: Yadavs, Hyderabad.

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Which tribe is central to the Medaram jatara?* → the **Koya** tribe (goddesses Sammakka & Saralamma).

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4 — Indian National Movement: Pre-Congress Associations & Socio-Religious Reform

### Deep Dive

**Pre-Congress political associations** (before the INC's 1885 founding) trained early political opinion:

| Association | Year | Key figure(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Landholders' Society (Bengal) | 1838 | zamindars |
| British Indian Association | 1851 | Bengal |
| East India Association (London) | 1866 | **Dadabhai Naoroji** |
| Indian Association (Calcutta) | 1876 | **Surendranath Banerjee**, Ananda Mohan Bose |
| Madras Mahajana Sabha | 1884 | — |
| Bombay Presidency Association | 1885 | Pherozeshah Mehta, Telang, Tyabji |

**Socio-religious reform movements** reshaped Indian society:
- **Brahmo Samaj (1828)** — **Raja Rammohan Roy**; against sati, idolatry, caste; **Sati abolished (1829)** under Bentinck.
- **Prarthana Samaj** — Maharashtra (Atmaram Pandurang, later M.G. Ranade).
- **Arya Samaj (1875)** — **Dayananda Saraswati**; "Back to the Vedas"; Shuddhi movement.
- **Ramakrishna Mission (1897)** — **Swami Vivekananda** (guru: Ramakrishna Paramahamsa).
- **Aligarh Movement** — **Sir Syed Ahmad Khan** (modern education for Muslims; MAO College 1875).
- **Theosophical Society** — Blavatsky & Olcott (India HQ Adyar); later **Annie Besant**.
- **Satyashodhak Samaj (1873)** — **Jyotiba Phule** (anti-caste, women's & lower-caste education).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Indian Association (1876, Surendranath Banerjee)** and **East India Association (1866, Dadabhai Naoroji)** preceded the INC. Reform: **Brahmo (Rammohan Roy), Arya Samaj (Dayananda), Ramakrishna Mission (Vivekananda), Aligarh (Syed Ahmad Khan), Satyashodhak (Phule)**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Sati was abolished in 1829** (Bentinck, Rammohan Roy's campaign). Don't confuse **Brahmo Samaj (1828, Bengal)** with **Prarthana Samaj (Maharashtra)** or **Arya Samaj (1875, Dayananda)**.

💡 **Example:** Matching **reformer → movement** (Rammohan Roy→Brahmo, Dayananda→Arya, Vivekananda→Ramakrishna Mission, Phule→Satyashodhak) is a very common PYQ.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Pre-Congress: East India Assoc. (1866, Naoroji), Indian Assoc. (1876, S.N. Banerjee).
- Brahmo (1828, Rammohan Roy); Sati abolition 1829.
- Arya Samaj (1875, Dayananda); Ramakrishna Mission (1897, Vivekananda).
- Aligarh (Syed Ahmad Khan); Satyashodhak (Phule).

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Who founded the Indian Association in 1876?* → **Surendranath Banerjee** (with Ananda Mohan Bose).

## 🗺️ Subject 5 — Telangana Geography: Industries & Transport (IT, Pharma, ORR/RRR, Highways, Rail)

### Deep Dive

**Industry.** Telangana — anchored by **Hyderabad** — is a leading **services + high-tech** economy:
- **IT/ITeS** — **HITEC City / Cyberabad / Madhapur-Gachibowli** host global tech firms; Hyderabad is a top Indian IT-export hub (*verify latest export figures*).
- **Pharma & life sciences** — **Genome Valley** (life-sciences cluster) and the proposed **Hyderabad Pharma City**; the region is a major **bulk-drug/vaccine** centre.
- **Defence & aerospace** — **DRDO labs, HAL, BDL, DRDL** around Hyderabad.
- **Textiles** — **Kakatiya Mega Textile Park (Warangal)**.
- **Mining/heavy** — **Singareni (coal)**, cement, granite.

**Transport:**
- **Roads:** the **Outer Ring Road (ORR)** — an ~**`158 km`** expressway ring around Hyderabad — and the planned **Regional Ring Road (RRR)** farther out. National highways (e.g., **NH-44**, the north-south corridor) cross the state.
- **Rail:** **South Central Railway (SCR)** is headquartered at **Secunderabad**; the **Hyderabad Metro Rail** serves the city.
- **Air:** **Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (RGIA), Shamshabad** — the main international gateway.
