## 🏛️ Polity: Parliament — Composition, Sessions, Bills, Budget & Committees

**Parliament (`Art 79`)** = President + **Rajya Sabha (Council of States)** + **Lok Sabha (House of the People)**.

**Composition.**
- **Lok Sabha:** max **550** (530 states + 20 UTs); directly elected (FPTP); term **5 years** (dissolvable). The **Anglo-Indian** nomination (`Art 331`) was discontinued (**104th Amendment, 2020**).
- **Rajya Sabha:** max **250** (238 elected by state/UT assemblies via **STV proportional representation** + **12 nominated** by the President for art/science/literature/social service). It is a **permanent House** (not dissolved); **one-third retire every 2 years**; member term **6 years**.

**Sessions.** Three usually: **Budget, Monsoon, Winter**. The gap between two sessions cannot exceed **6 months** (`Art 85`). Key devices: **Question Hour**, **Zero Hour** (Indian innovation), **adjournment, prorogation, dissolution**; **quorum = 1/10** of total members.

**Bills.**
- **Ordinary bills:** either House; disagreement resolved by **joint sitting** (`Art 108`, presided by Lok Sabha Speaker).
- **Money bills (`Art 110`):** only in Lok Sabha (on President's recommendation); Rajya Sabha has only **14 days** and **recommendatory** power; **Speaker certifies** a money bill. No joint sitting.
- **Financial bills** and **Constitutional amendment bills** (special majority) differ.

**Budget (`Art 112` — Annual Financial Statement).** Stages: presentation, general discussion, **cut motions** (Policy/Economy/Token), voting on **Demands for Grants** (only Lok Sabha votes), **Appropriation Bill**, **Finance Bill**. Related: **Vote on Account**, **Consolidated Fund/Contingency Fund/Public Account**.

**Committees.** **Financial committees:** **Public Accounts Committee (PAC)**, **Estimates Committee**, **Committee on Public Undertakings (COPU)**. **Department-related Standing Committees (DRSCs)**. The **PAC** (chaired by an Opposition MP by convention) examines CAG reports.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Lok Sabha (max **550**, 5-yr, directly elected) + Rajya Sabha (max **250**, permanent, **12 nominated**, 6-yr, 1/3 retire every 2 yr). Money bill (`Art 110`) = **Lok Sabha only**, **Speaker certifies**, RS **14 days** recommendatory. **PAC/Estimates/COPU** are the financial committees.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**LS 550 (5-yr, elected) vs RS 250 (permanent, 12 nominated, 6-yr); money bill = LS + Speaker + 14-day RS; PAC-Estimates-COPU.**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Only Lok Sabha** votes on Demands for Grants and originates money bills; the **Speaker certifies** a money bill (final). **No joint sitting** for money or amendment bills. The **Estimates Committee** has **only Lok Sabha** members; PAC & COPU have both Houses. Zero Hour is **not** in the rules (informal).

💡 **Example:** The **CAG audit report** is examined by the **Public Accounts Committee** — the "watchdog" of public finance.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- LS max 550 (Art 81); RS max 250, 12 nominated (Art 80).
- Money bill Art 110; Speaker certifies; RS 14 days; no joint sitting.
- Budget: cut motions, Demands for Grants (LS votes), Appropriation & Finance Bills.
- Financial committees: PAC, Estimates (LS only), COPU.

## 🏺 History: Telangana Handlooms & Handicrafts (GI Products)

Telangana's **handlooms and handicrafts** — many with **Geographical Indication (GI)** tags — are a staple TGPSC topic (match the craft to its **place**).

**Handlooms (sarees/textiles).**
- **Pochampally Ikat** (Bhoodan Pochampally, Yadadri-Bhongir) — tie-dye "**Ikat**" weave; a famous GI; Pochampally is a UN/tourism "best village."
- **Gadwal** sarees (Jogulamba-Gadwal) — cotton body with silk (zari) borders ("**sico**").
- **Narayanpet** sarees (Narayanpet) — distinctive borders, Maratha influence.
- **Siddipet Gollabhama** sarees — motif of a milkmaid (Gollabhama).

**Handicrafts.**
- **Cheriyal scroll paintings** (Cheriyal, Siddipet) — narrative scroll art (Nakashi community); GI.
- **Nirmal paintings & toys** (Nirmal) — soft-wood toys and paintings with golden hues; GI.
- **Bidriware** (originated in **Bidar**, now Karnataka; practised in Hyderabad) — blackened zinc-copper alloy with silver inlay.
- **Karimnagar silver filigree** — fine silver wirework; GI.
- **Dokra/Adilabad metal craft** (Ojha/Woj tribe) — lost-wax bell-metal casting.
- **Warangal durries** (dhurries/carpets) — GI cotton rugs.
- **Pembarthi brass/metal craft** (Jangaon).

## 🇮🇳 National Movement: Revolutionary Movement — Phase II (Bhagat Singh, Azad, Surya Sen)

The **second phase of revolutionary nationalism (1920s–1930s)** revived after the Non-Cooperation Movement's withdrawal, now with **socialist ideals** and organised groups.

**HRA → HSRA.** The **Hindustan Republican Association (HRA, 1924)** — **Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqulla Khan, Chandrashekhar Azad, Sachindra Sanyal** — carried out the **Kakori Conspiracy (1925)** (train robbery of government treasury near Kakori); Bismil, Ashfaqulla and others were **executed (1927)**. The HRA was reorganised as the **Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA, 1928, Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi)** adding a **socialist** goal, led by **Bhagat Singh, Azad, Sukhdev, Rajguru, Bhagwati Charan Vohra**.

**Key actions.**
- **Saunders' murder (Dec 1928, Lahore):** Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Azad killed police officer **Saunders** to avenge **Lala Lajpat Rai**'s death (Simon protest).
- **Central Assembly bomb (8 April 1929):** **Bhagat Singh & B.K. Dutt** threw a (non-lethal) bomb and leaflets in the Assembly to "make the deaf hear," then courted arrest.
- **Lahore Conspiracy Case:** Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru were **hanged on 23 March 1931** (Shaheed Diwas).
- **Chandrashekhar Azad** died fighting at **Alfred Park, Allahabad (Feb 1931)** (shot himself with his last bullet — never to be captured alive).

**Bengal (Surya Sen).** The **Chittagong Armoury Raid (18 April 1930)** led by **Surya Sen (Masterda)** aimed to seize armouries; women revolutionaries (**Pritilata Waddedar**, **Kalpana Dutt**) participated. Surya Sen was **hanged (1934)**.

| Group/Event | Year | Figures |
|---|---|---|
| HRA / Kakori | 1924/1925 | Bismil, Ashfaqulla, Azad |
| HSRA | 1928 | Bhagat Singh, Azad, Sukhdev |
| Assembly bomb | 8 Apr 1929 | Bhagat Singh, B.K. Dutt |
| Chittagong raid | 18 Apr 1930 | Surya Sen, Pritilata |

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Phase II: **HRA (1924) → Kakori (1925)** (Bismil/Ashfaqulla executed 1927) → **HSRA (1928, socialist)**; **Saunders (1928)**, **Assembly bomb (8 Apr 1929)**; **Bhagat Singh-Sukhdev-Rajguru hanged 23 Mar 1931**; **Azad died Feb 1931 (Allahabad)**; **Surya Sen's Chittagong raid (1930)**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**HRA (Kakori 1925) → HSRA (1928) → Saunders 1928 → Assembly bomb 1929 → hanged 23 Mar 1931. Chittagong = Surya Sen 1930.**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Kakori (1925) = HRA**; **HSRA formed 1928** (added "Socialist"). **Bhagat Singh hanged 23 March 1931** (not 1929 — that was the Assembly bomb). **Chittagong Armoury Raid = Surya Sen (1930)**, Bengal. Azad died at **Allahabad (Alfred Park)**.

💡 **Example:** Pair *"Central Assembly bomb, 1929"* → **Bhagat Singh & B.K. Dutt**; *"Chittagong Armoury Raid"* → **Surya Sen (1930)**.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- HRA 1924; Kakori Conspiracy 1925 (Bismil, Ashfaqulla, Roshan Singh, Lahiri executed 1927).
- HSRA 1928 (socialist); Saunders 1928; Assembly bomb 8 Apr 1929 (Bhagat Singh, Dutt).
- Lahore Conspiracy → hanged 23 Mar 1931; Azad Feb 1931 Allahabad.
- Chittagong Armoury Raid 1930: Surya Sen, Pritilata Waddedar, Kalpana Dutt.

## 🗺️ Geography: Area Focus — Nalgonda-Suryapet

The **Nalgonda-Suryapet** region in **southern Telangana** is a **Krishna-basin** belt known for the **Nagarjunasagar** project, fluoride-affected groundwater, and historic Buddhist heritage.

**Location & physiography.** Part of old composite Nalgonda (now **Nalgonda, Suryapet, Yadadri-Bhongir** partly; verify). It lies in the **Krishna basin** (the **Krishna** and the **Musi** — a Krishna tributary — drain it), on granite/gneiss terrain with **black and red soils**. The region has historically suffered **high groundwater fluoride** (fluorosis), now addressed by **Mission Bhagiratha** piped water.

**Water & agriculture.** The **Nagarjunasagar** dam (on the Krishna, shared AP-Telangana; a masonry dam with left/right canals) is the lifeline; also **AMR-SLBC (Alimineti Madhava Reddy / Srisailam Left Bank Canal)** tunnel. Crops: **paddy, cotton, chillies, redgram**. **Pochampally** (Ikat handloom) and **Bhoodan** movement (Vinoba Bhave began Bhoodan at **Pochampally, 1951**) are in this belt (Yadadri-Bhongir).

**Heritage & sites.** **Nagarjunakonda / Nagarjunasagar** — a major **Buddhist** heritage site (Ikshvaku capital Vijayapuri, now an island museum after the dam submerged it); **Phanigiri** (Buddhist site, Suryapet); **Kolanupaka** (Jain & Someshwara temple, Yadadri-Bhongir); **Yadadri (Yadagirigutta)** Lakshmi Narasimha temple; **Bhuvanagiri (Bhongir) Fort** (monolithic hill fort).
