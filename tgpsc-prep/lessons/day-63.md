## 🏰 Telangana History: The Telangana Armed Struggle (1946-51)

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **Telangana Armed Struggle (Telangana Rebellion / Telangana Sayudha Poratam, 1946-1951)** was a **peasant/communist-led armed uprising** against the **Nizam's feudal order** (deshmukhs, jagirdars, doras) and later against the Indian Army's post-Operation-Polo phase.

**Causes:** oppressive feudal exactions — **vetti (forced/bonded labour)**, illegal levies, land-grabbing by landlords, and the autocratic Nizam-Razakar repression.

**Course:**
- Began around **1946** in **Nalgonda (Jangaon/Kadavendi)** — the killing of **Doddi Komarayya** (a village activist, 1946) is often cited as a spark.
- Led by the **Andhra Mahasabha (communist wing)** and the **Communist Party**; leaders **Ravi Narayana Reddy, Baddam Yella Reddy, Chandra Rajeswara Rao, Arutla Ramachandra Reddy & Arutla Kamala Devi**; and **Doddi Komarayya**.
- Peasants formed **village soviets/gram rajyams**, seized land, abolished vetti, and redistributed ~**1 million acres** (verify) in liberated villages (spread across Nalgonda, Warangal, Khammam).
- **Chakali Ailamma** (a washerwoman of Palakurthi, Warangal) famously resisted a landlord's land-grab — an iconic symbol.

**End:** after **Operation Polo (Sept 1948)** merged Hyderabad into India, the communists continued the struggle against the Indian government's approach for a time, but it was **withdrawn in 1951** (following land reforms/abolition of jagirdari and the CPI's decision). It profoundly shaped land reforms and left politics in the region.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Telangana Armed Struggle (1946-51)**: peasant/communist revolt vs Nizam's feudalism (**vetti**, doras); sparked in **Nalgonda** (Doddi Komarayya, 1946); leaders **Ravi Narayana Reddy** et al.; **Chakali Ailamma** icon; gram rajyams, land redistribution; withdrawn **1951**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The struggle **began 1946** (before Operation Polo 1948) and was **withdrawn in 1951**. **Chakali Ailamma** (Palakurthi) and **Doddi Komarayya** are key figures; **Ravi Narayana Reddy** a prominent leader. Core grievance = **vetti** (bonded labour) & feudal exactions.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Who was Chakali Ailamma, and why is she remembered?* → A **washerwoman of Palakurthi (Warangal)** who resisted a landlord's attempt to seize her land — an early iconic act of the Telangana Armed Struggle.

## 🇮🇳 Indian National Movement: Revolutionary Movement Phase II (Round 4)

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **second phase of revolutionary nationalism** (1920s-30s) revived after Non-Cooperation's withdrawal.

**HRA (1924)** — Hindustan Republican Association (Kanpur), founded by **Sachindra Nath Sanyal, Ram Prasad Bismil, Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee**. Landmark: the **Kakori train robbery (9 Aug 1925)** — Bismil, Ashfaqulla Khan, Roshan Singh, Rajendra Lahiri hanged (1927).

**HSRA (1928)** — reorganised at **Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi** as the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association; leaders **Chandrashekhar Azad, Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru, Bhagwati Charan Vohra**.
- **Saunders killing (Dec 1928)** — Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Azad avenged Lala Lajpat Rai's death.
- **Central Assembly bomb (8 Apr 1929)** — Bhagat Singh & B. K. Dutt threw (harmless) bombs shouting **"Inquilab Zindabad"**, courting arrest.
- **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru hanged 23 March 1931** (Lahore Conspiracy Case) — **Shaheed Diwas**.
- **Chandrashekhar Azad** died at **Alfred Park, Allahabad (27 Feb 1931)**.

**Bengal — Chittagong Armoury Raid (18 Apr 1930)** led by **Surya Sen (Masterda)** with **Pritilata Waddedar, Kalpana Dutta**; Surya Sen hanged 1934.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **HRA 1924 → Kakori 1925**; **HSRA 1928** (Azad, Bhagat Singh); **Assembly bomb 8 Apr 1929** ("Inquilab Zindabad"); **hanged 23 Mar 1931**; Azad died **27 Feb 1931**; **Surya Sen → Chittagong Armoury Raid 1930**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — HRA (1924) → **HSRA (1928)** with "Socialist" added. **23 Mar 1931** = Bhagat Singh/Sukhdev/Rajguru hanged (≠ Azad's death 27 Feb 1931). Chittagong raid (1930) = Surya Sen.

## 🗺️ Telangana Geography: Rivers — The Krishna System (Round 4)

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **Krishna** is Telangana's **second major river** (~**21%** of the state's drainage), flowing along/through the **southern** districts.

**Course & entry.** Rises at **Mahabaleshwar (Western Ghats, Maharashtra)**; enters Telangana in the **south-west (Mahabubnagar/Jogulamba Gadwal — near Krishna/Alampur)**, forms parts of the southern boundary, and flows **east/south-east** toward Andhra Pradesh (Nagarjuna Sagar → Bay of Bengal).

**Major tributaries (in Telangana):**
- **Tungabhadra** — joins the Krishna at **Alampur (Jogulamba Gadwal)** — the "Kudali/Sangam"; the largest Krishna tributary.
- **Bhima** — flows along the north-western Krishna sub-basin (Maharashtra/Karnataka border side).
- **Musi** (through Hyderabad, joins near Wadapally/Nalgonda), **Dindi**, **Paleru**, **Halia** — smaller Telangana tributaries.

**Major projects on the Krishna system:**
- **Priyadarshini Jurala (PJP)** — the **first major project** on the Krishna as it enters Telangana (Mahabubnagar).
- **Srisailam** (across the Krishna; left-bank canal serves Telangana), **Nagarjuna Sagar** (masonry dam, Nalgonda border), **Kalwakurthy, Nettempadu, Bhima, Koil Sagar** lift schemes.
- **Palamuru-Rangareddy LIS** — lifts Krishna water to the drought-prone south.

**Water disputes:** the **Krishna Water Disputes Tribunal** and the **Krishna River Management Board (KRMB)** govern allocation between Telangana and Andhra Pradesh (a live inter-state issue).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Krishna** = ~21% of drainage; rises **Mahabaleshwar**; enters at **Mahabubnagar/Alampur**. **Tungabhadra** (largest tributary, joins at **Alampur**); **Bhima, Musi, Dindi**. Projects: **Jurala (first), Srisailam, Nagarjuna Sagar, Palamuru-Rangareddy**. **KRMB** governs sharing.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Krishna rises at Mahabaleshwar** (≠ Godavari's Trimbak). **Tungabhadra** joins the Krishna at **Alampur**. **Jurala** = first Krishna project in Telangana; **Musi** is a Krishna tributary (joins near Wadapally, Nalgonda).
