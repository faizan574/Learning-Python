## 🏛️ Polity: Fundamental Rights — Art 15-18 (Right to Equality contd.)

### 📖 Deep Dive

Articles 15-18 continue the **Right to Equality** (Art 14-18).

**Article 15 — Prohibition of discrimination.** The State shall not discriminate against any **citizen** on grounds **only** of **religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth** (or any of them).
- **15(1)** — no discrimination by the State; **15(2)** — no disability re: shops, public restaurants, wells, roads, public places.
- **15(3)** — special provision for **women and children**.
- **15(4)** — special provision for advancement of **SEBCs, SCs, STs** (added by **1st Amendment, 1951**, after *Champakam Dorairajan*).
- **15(5)** — reservation in **educational institutions** including private (added by **93rd Amendment, 2005**).
- **15(6)** — reservation for **EWS (Economically Weaker Sections)** — up to 10% (added by **103rd Amendment, 2019**).

**Article 16 — Equality of opportunity in public employment.**
- **16(1)/(2)** — equality; no discrimination.
- **16(3)** — Parliament may prescribe residence requirement for certain jobs.
- **16(4)** — reservation for **backward classes** inadequately represented.
- **16(4A)** — reservation in **promotions** for SC/ST (77th Amendment, 1995).
- **16(4B)** — carry-forward of unfilled vacancies (81st Amendment).
- **16(6)** — EWS reservation (103rd Amendment). The **Indra Sawhney (Mandal) case, 1992** capped total reservation at **50%** (barring extraordinary circumstances) and introduced the **creamy layer** for OBCs.

**Article 17 — Abolition of Untouchability.** "Untouchability" is abolished and its practice **forbidden**; enforced via the **Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955** and the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989. It is an **absolute** right (available against private individuals too).

**Article 18 — Abolition of titles.** No title (except **military and academic** distinctions) shall be conferred by the State; citizens cannot accept titles from foreign states. **National awards (Bharat Ratna, Padma)** were held valid (not "titles" to be used as prefixes/suffixes) in *Balaji Raghavan* (1996).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Art 15** (no discrimination; 15(4) SEBC, 15(5) education, 15(6) EWS); **Art 16** (public-employment equality; 16(4) reservation, 16(4A) promotions, 16(6) EWS); **Art 17** untouchability abolished (absolute); **Art 18** titles abolished (military/academic exempt). 50% cap = **Indra Sawhney 1992**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Art 15 & 16 protect **citizens** (Art 15 uses "citizen"), while **Art 17 & 18** apply more broadly/absolutely. The word "**only**" in Art 15 matters — discrimination on those grounds **plus** others may be permissible.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Which case fixed the 50% ceiling on reservations and the creamy-layer concept?* → **Indra Sawhney v. Union of India (1992)** — the Mandal case.

- Art 18 exempts **military & academic** distinctions.

## 🏰 Telangana History: Literature & Language

### 📖 Deep Dive

Telangana's literary tradition spans classical Telugu to modern movement poetry:

**Classical / medieval:**
- **Palkuriki Somanatha** (13th c.) — a pioneer of **desi/Telangana Telugu**, author of **Basava Purana** and **Panditaradhya Charitra**; championed the Virasaiva tradition and native metres (*dwipada*).
- **Bammera Potana** (15th c.) — from the Telangana region, rendered the **Andhra Maha Bhagavatam (Bhagavata Purana)** into Telugu; revered for devotional simplicity.
- **Gona Budda Reddy** — **Ranganatha Ramayanam** (dwipada Ramayana).

**Modern & movement-era:**
- **Dasarathi Krishnamacharya** — poet of the Telangana liberation struggle ("*Na Telangana koti ratanala veena*"); anti-Nizam voice.
- **Kaloji Narayana Rao** ("**Praja Kavi**", People's Poet) — **Jnanpith**-honoured? (he received **Padma Vibhushan**; his birthday **9 Sept** is **Telangana Language Day / Telangana Bhasha Dinotsavam**); wrote *Naa Godava*.
- **C. Narayana Reddy (Cinare)** — **Jnanpith Award (1988)** for **Viswambhara**; a towering modern Telugu poet.
- **Gaddar** and **Suddala Hanmanthu** — balladeers of the people's movements.
- **Vignana Sarvaswam** — Telugu encyclopaedia effort; Telangana dialect and *Suravaram Pratapa Reddy* (**Andhrula Sanghika Charitra**, first social history in Telugu; edited *Golconda Patrika*).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Palkuriki Somanatha** (Basava Purana, dwipada), **Bammera Potana** (Bhagavatam). Modern: **Dasarathi** (liberation poet), **Kaloji** (Praja Kavi; **9 Sept = Telangana Language Day**), **C. Narayana Reddy/Cinare** (**Jnanpith 1988, Viswambhara**), **Suravaram Pratapa Reddy** (Golconda Patrika).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Cinare (C. Narayana Reddy)** won the **Jnanpith (1988)** for *Viswambhara*. **Kaloji** = "Praja Kavi" (Padma Vibhushan; Language Day on his birthday, 9 Sept). Don't swap these two.

## 🇮🇳 Indian National Movement: WWII, August Offer, Cripps Mission & Quit India

### 📖 Deep Dive

**WWII and Indian politics.** When Britain declared India a party to WWII (**1939**) without consulting Indians, the **Congress provincial ministries resigned (1939)** in protest; the Muslim League observed a **"Deliverance Day."**

**August Offer (1940).** Viceroy **Linlithgow** offered **Dominion Status** as the goal, expansion of the Viceroy's Executive Council, and a post-war constituent body — but gave minorities a veto. **Rejected** by the Congress; Gandhi launched **individual satyagraha** (1940-41), with **Vinoba Bhave** as the first satyagrahi and **Nehru** the second.

**Cripps Mission (1942).** **Sir Stafford Cripps** offered **Dominion Status** with the right of provinces to secede (a "Pakistan" concession) and a post-war constituent assembly, but **no immediate transfer of power** and only advisory defence control. Both Congress and League rejected it; Gandhi called it a **"post-dated cheque on a crashing bank."**

**Quit India Movement (1942).** The Congress passed the **Quit India Resolution at Bombay (Gowalia Tank) on `8 August 1942`**; Gandhi gave the mantra **"Do or Die."** The British arrested the entire leadership on **9 August 1942**, and the movement became a **spontaneous, leaderless mass upsurge** (parallel governments at **Ballia, Tamluk (Midnapore), Satara**). It was the **most militant** phase; **Aruna Asaf Ali** hoisted the flag at Gowalia Tank; **Usha Mehta** ran an underground **Congress Radio**. Suppressed by 1943, but it demonstrated the depth of anti-British sentiment.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — August Offer **1940** (Linlithgow, Dominion Status, rejected → individual satyagraha, Vinoba first). **Cripps 1942** ("post-dated cheque"). **Quit India 8 Aug 1942** (Gowalia Tank, "Do or Die"); leaders arrested 9 Aug; parallel govts at Ballia/Tamluk/Satara.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The Quit India **resolution** was passed **8 Aug 1942** (Gowalia Tank, Bombay); mass arrests **9 Aug**. Cripps (1942) preceded Quit India. **Vinoba Bhave** was the **first** individual satyagrahi, not Nehru.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Who described the Cripps offer as a "post-dated cheque"?* → **Mahatma Gandhi** (some add "on a crashing/failing bank").

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- Congress ministries resigned **1939**; League "Deliverance Day."
- August Offer 1940 → **individual satyagraha** (Vinoba first, Nehru second).
- Cripps 1942 → Dominion Status, provinces may secede; rejected.
- Quit India: **8 Aug 1942**, "Do or Die," Aruna Asaf Ali, Usha Mehta's radio.

## 🗺️ Telangana Geography: Minerals & Energy

### 📖 Deep Dive

Telangana's geology (Archaean granites-gneisses, Gondwana coal basins, Cuddapah/Deccan formations) endows it with significant minerals and energy resources.

**Minerals:**
- **Coal** — the state's most important mineral, in the **Godavari Valley (Pranahita-Godavari) Gondwana coalfields**, mined by **Singareni Collieries Company Ltd (SCCL)** — India's first coal PSU; belts around **Ramagundam, Godavarikhani, Kothagudem, Mancherial, Bellampalli, Yellandu**.
- **Limestone** — abundant (feeds the cement industry) in **Nalgonda, Suryapet, Adilabad, Yerraguntla-type belts**.
- **Granite / dimensional stone** — famous **black granite** (e.g. from Karimnagar, Warangal, Khammam) exported widely; Telangana is a major granite producer.
- Others: **bauxite, iron ore, dolomite, quartz, feldspar, clays, barytes, and gold traces** (Ramagiri-type in the region — verify).

**Energy:**
- **Thermal** — dominant; **NTPC Ramagundam**, **Kothagudem Thermal (KTPS)**, **Kakatiya TPS (Bhupalpally)**, **Yadadri TPS (Nalgonda)** — coal-fed near the Singareni belt.
- **Hydel** — on the Krishna & Godavari: **Nagarjuna Sagar, Srisailam (left bank), Pochampad, Nizam Sagar, Priyadarshini Jurala, Lower Jurala, Pulichintala**.
- **Renewables** — growing **solar** capacity (Telangana has pushed large solar parks); some wind is limited.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Coal** = key mineral, **Godavari Valley Gondwana** fields, mined by **SCCL** (Ramagundam/Kothagudem/Mancherial). **Limestone** (cement) in Nalgonda/Suryapet; famous **black granite** exports. Power: mostly **thermal** (Ramagundam, KTPS, Yadadri) + hydel (Nagarjuna Sagar/Srisailam) + growing **solar**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Telangana's coal is **Gondwana** (Godavari valley), not Tertiary. **SCCL** (Singareni) is the mining company. Don't place major coalfields in the south — they're in the **north/north-east (Godavari belt)**.
