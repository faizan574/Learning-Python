## 🏛️ Subject 1 — Polity: Parliament — Composition, Sessions, Bills, Budget & Committees

### Deep Dive

**Composition.** Parliament = **President + Lok Sabha (House of the People) + Rajya Sabha (Council of States)**.
- **Lok Sabha:** max strength **552** (`530` from states + `20` from UTs + formerly 2 Anglo-Indian nominees — **abolished by the 104th Amendment**). **Term 5 years** (extendable in emergency). Directly elected.
- **Rajya Sabha:** max **250** (`238` elected by state/UT assemblies via **proportional representation, STV** + `12` **nominated** by the President for art/science/literature/social service). **Permanent body** — **one-third retire every 2 years** (member term **6 years**); Vice-President is **ex-officio Chairman**.

**Sessions.** Usually three: **Budget, Monsoon, Winter**. The President **summons** each House; the gap between two sessions **cannot exceed 6 months**. Tools: **Question Hour, Zero Hour, adjournment/no-confidence motions**; **quorum = 1/10** of the House.

**Bills.**
- **Ordinary bill** — either House; both must pass (deadlock → joint sitting).
- **Money bill (Art 110)** — only in **Lok Sabha** (on President's recommendation); **Rajya Sabha** can only **recommend** (within **14 days**), not amend/reject; **Speaker certifies** a money bill.
- **Financial** and **Constitutional Amendment (Art 368)** bills.

**Budget (Art 112).** The **Annual Financial Statement**; stages — general discussion, **demands for grants** (voted by LS), **Appropriation Bill**, **Finance Bill**. Instruments: cut motions, vote on account.

**Committees.** **Public Accounts Committee (PAC)**, **Estimates Committee**, **Committee on Public Undertakings (CoPU)**, and **Departmentally-Related Standing Committees** — the "mini-Parliaments" doing detailed scrutiny.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — LS max **552** (Anglo-Indian seats removed, **104th Amdt**); RS max **250** (238 + 12), **1/3 retire every 2 yrs**. **Money bill = Art 110** (LS only; RS 14-day recommendation; Speaker certifies). Budget = **Art 112**. Max inter-session gap **6 months**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — A **money bill** can be introduced **only in the Lok Sabha**, and the Rajya Sabha has **no power to amend/reject** it (only recommend within 14 days). The **Rajya Sabha is permanent** (not dissolved). Anglo-Indian nomination was **abolished (104th Amendment, 2019)**.

💡 **Example:** On a disputed bill, the **Speaker's certification** that it is a "money bill" limits the Rajya Sabha to recommendations — a frequent PYQ nuance.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- LS 552 (104th Amdt removed Anglo-Indian); RS 250 (238+12), STV.
- RS permanent; 1/3 retire every 2 years; term 6 years.
- Money bill Art 110 (LS only); budget Art 112; quorum 1/10.
- Committees: PAC, Estimates, CoPU, standing committees.

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Which House can introduce a money bill?* → only the **Lok Sabha** (Art 110).

## 🏰 Subject 3 — Telangana History: The Satavahanas

### Deep Dive

The **Satavahanas** were the **first major dynasty of the Deccan** (and a foundational Telangana dynasty), rising after the Mauryas (c. 1st c. BCE – 2nd/3rd c. CE).

- **Founder:** **Simuka**. Early power centres included **Kotilingala** (Telangana, Karimnagar region — early coins) and the capital **Pratishthana (Paithan)**; **Amaravati/Dharanikota** was a major centre.
- **Greatest ruler:** **Gautamiputra Satakarni** — defeated the **Western Kshatrapas (Shakas, Nahapana)**; praised in the **Nasik Prashasti** inscription of his mother **Gautami Balasri**. His successor **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** extended the dynasty.
- **Language/culture:** **Prakrit** (official), **Brahmi** script; **matronymic** names (named after mothers — Gautamiputra, Vasishthiputra). King **Hala** compiled the **Gathasaptasati (Gatha Saptashati)** in Prakrit.
- **Economy:** flourishing **trade** (Roman coins found), guilds; **lead/potin coins**. **Religion:** patronised both **Buddhism** (the **Amaravati stupa**, Nagarjunakonda nearby later) and **Brahmanism** (performed Vedic sacrifices).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Satavahanas: founder **Simuka**; greatest **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (defeated Shaka **Nahapana**; Nasik inscription by **Gautami Balasri**); **Prakrit/Brahmi**, **matronymics**; **Hala's Gathasaptasati**; early mint at **Kotilingala**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (not Simuka) is the greatest ruler; **Simuka** is the **founder**. The **Nasik Prashasti** was issued by his mother **Gautami Balasri**. Names are **matronymic** (mother's name prefix).

💡 **Example:** **Gautamiputra Satakarni's** victory over **Nahapana** (and restriking his coins) is the classic Satavahana PYQ, along with **Hala's Gathasaptasati**.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Founder Simuka; capital Pratishthana (Paithan); early mint Kotilingala (Telangana).
- Gautamiputra Satakarni (defeated Shakas); Nasik inscription (Gautami Balasri).
- Prakrit/Brahmi; matronymics; Hala's Gathasaptasati.
- Amaravati stupa; Roman trade; lead coins.

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Who was the greatest Satavahana ruler?* → **Gautamiputra Satakarni**.

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4 — Indian National Movement: Rowlatt Act, Jallianwala Bagh & Khilafat

### Deep Dive

**Rowlatt Act (1919).** The **Anarchical and Revolutionary Crimes Act** allowed **detention without trial** and curbed civil liberties — "**no appeal, no vakil, no daleel**." Gandhi launched the **Rowlatt Satyagraha** (nationwide hartal), his first all-India agitation.

**Jallianwala Bagh Massacre (13 April 1919).** On **Baisakhi day** at Amritsar, **General Dyer** ordered troops to fire on an unarmed crowd (protesting the Rowlatt Act and the arrest of local leaders), killing hundreds. **Rabindranath Tagore renounced his knighthood** in protest; the **Hunter Commission** inquired. It was a turning point that radicalised the moderate opinion.

**Khilafat Movement (1919-1922).** Indian Muslims (led by the **Ali brothers — Mohammad Ali & Shaukat Ali**, with Maulana Azad, Hakim Ajmal Khan) protested the harsh treatment of the **Ottoman Caliph (Khalifa)** after WWI. **Gandhi supported Khilafat** to forge **Hindu-Muslim unity**, and **merged it with the Non-Cooperation Movement (1920)**. The movement faded after **Turkey abolished the Caliphate (1924)**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Rowlatt Act 1919** (detention without trial) → **Rowlatt Satyagraha**; **Jallianwala Bagh 13 Apr 1919** (Dyer, Baisakhi; Tagore gave up knighthood; Hunter Commission); **Khilafat (Ali brothers)** merged with **Non-Cooperation (1920)**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Jallianwala Bagh = 13 April 1919** (General **Dyer**; O'Dwyer was the Punjab Lt-Governor). **Khilafat** concerned the **Ottoman Caliph**, and Gandhi linked it to **Non-Cooperation** for Hindu-Muslim unity.

💡 **Example:** **Tagore renouncing his knighthood** after Jallianwala Bagh, and the **Khilafat-Non-Cooperation merger**, are the two anchor PYQs of this phase.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Rowlatt Act 1919: detention without trial; Rowlatt Satyagraha.
- Jallianwala Bagh 13 Apr 1919 (Dyer, Baisakhi); Tagore; Hunter Commission.
- Khilafat 1919-22: Ali brothers; Ottoman Caliph.
- Khilafat merged with Non-Cooperation (1920).

✅ **Quick self-check:** *On what day did the Jallianwala Bagh massacre occur?* → **13 April 1919** (Baisakhi).

## 🗺️ Subject 5 — Telangana Geography: Area Focus — Khammam-Bhadradri

### Deep Dive

The **Khammam-Bhadradri** belt occupies **south-eastern Telangana**, bordering **Andhra Pradesh and Chhattisgarh** — **forested, tribal and mineral-rich** (*verify current district boundaries: Khammam, Bhadradri-Kothagudem*).

**Rivers & irrigation.** The **Godavari** forms the eastern/southern boundary; tributaries include the **Kinnerasani** and **Munneru**. The **Kinnerasani** has a **dam and wildlife sanctuary**; **Sammakka-Sagar / Sitarama** lift schemes draw Godavari water.

**Economy & minerals.** **Kothagudem/Paloncha** is a **coal (Singareni) and power** hub (**Kothagudem Thermal Power Station, KTPS**). **Khammam** is a major **chillies (mirchi)** market; **cotton, paddy** and **tobacco** are important. Forest produce and **tribal (Koya)** livelihoods feature strongly.

**Heritage & tourism.** **Bhadrachalam** — the famous **Sri Sita Ramachandra Swamy temple** on the Godavari (associated with **Bhakta Ramadasu**); **Parnasala** (Ramayana exile site) nearby; **Kinnerasani** eco-tourism; **Khammam Fort (Stambhadri)**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Khammam-Bhadradri = SE Telangana (borders AP & Chhattisgarh); **Godavari + Kinnerasani/Munneru**; **Bhadrachalam** Rama temple; **Kothagudem (KTPS) coal-power**; **Khammam chillies**; Koya tribal belt.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Bhadrachalam** (Rama temple) is on the **Godavari** in **Bhadradri-Kothagudem**. **Kothagudem/Paloncha** = coal + KTPS thermal. Khammam Fort is also called **Stambhadri**. Keep the river (Godavari/Kinnerasani) pairings exact.
