## 📘 Subject 1: Indian Constitution & Polity
### 🎯 Constitutional Bodies: EC, UPSC/TGPSC, CAG, AG & the Commissions

**Constitutional bodies** derive their powers directly from the Constitution (as opposed to statutory bodies created by law). Know each one's article, composition and function.

### 📖 Concept

| Body | Article | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Election Commission** | `324` | Superintends elections to Parliament, State legislatures, President & VP |
| **UPSC** | `315-323` | Recruitment to All-India & central services; advises on appointments |
| **State PSC (TGPSC)** | `315` | State-service recruitment |
| **CAG** | `148-151` | Audits Union & State accounts; "guardian of the public purse" |
| **Attorney-General** | `76` | Highest law officer of the Union |
| **Advocate-General (state)** | `165` | Highest law officer of a state |
| **Finance Commission** | `280` | Centre-state tax devolution (Day 16) |
| **SC/ST/BC Commissions** | `338 / 338A / 338B` | Safeguard rights of SC / ST / Backward Classes |

**Election Commission (`Art 324`).** A **multi-member** body (CEC + 2 ECs since 1993); CEC removable **like a Supreme Court judge** (impeachment) — a strong independence safeguard; ECs removable on CEC's recommendation.

**UPSC / State PSC (`Art 315`).** Members appointed by the **President (UPSC)** / **Governor (SPSC)**; hold office for **6 years or 65 (UPSC) / 62 (SPSC)**; removable only by the **President** on grounds of misbehaviour (after SC inquiry). Conducts exams; its advice is **advisory** (not binding).

**CAG (`Art 148`).** Appointed by the President; same removal as a SC judge; audits all Union/State expenditure; reports to the **President/Governor**, laid before the legislature, examined by the **PAC**. Ambedkar called the CAG the **most important officer** under the Constitution.

**Attorney-General (`Art 76`).** Appointed by the President; must be qualified to be a **Supreme Court judge**; not a full-time govt servant (can take private practice); has **right of audience in all courts** and can speak in Parliament (no vote).

**SC/ST/BC Commissions:** **National Commission for SCs (Art 338), STs (Art 338A — 89th Amendment 2003), Backward Classes (Art 338B — 102nd Amendment 2018)**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **EC (324), UPSC (315-323), CAG (148), AG (76), FC (280), SC/ST/BC Commissions (338/338A/338B)**. The **CEC and CAG are removed like Supreme Court judges** (a key independence guarantee). The **NCBC became constitutional via the 102nd Amendment (2018)**.

💡 **Example:** The **CAG** audits government spending and reports to Parliament, where the **Public Accounts Committee** scrutinises it — the accountability chain that makes the CAG "the guardian of the public purse."

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **CEC & CAG removal = same as SC judge** (impeachment-type).
- **UPSC advice is advisory**; members removed only by the President.
- **NCSC (338), NCST (338A, 89th Amdt), NCBC (338B, 102nd Amdt)**.
- **AG (76) = Union**, **Advocate-General (165) = state**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**324 EC, 315 PSC, 148 CAG, 76 AG, 280 FC, 338/A/B for SC/ST/BC.** CEC & CAG = SC-judge-level protection."

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **UPSC's advice is NOT binding** (advisory only). The **NCBC became a constitutional body only in 2018** (102nd Amendment) — earlier it was statutory. Don't confuse the **AG (Union, Art 76)** with the **Advocate-General (state, Art 165)**.

*(Art 324; the CAG is removed in the same manner as a Supreme Court judge.)*

## 🏛️ Subject 2: Telangana Schemes & Important Incidents (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision Deep-Dive: Cheyutha Pensions & Rajiv Aarogyasri

Continuing the **revision pass**, today consolidates **Cheyutha (social-security pensions)** and **Rajiv Aarogyasri (health cover)** — originally Day 5. *(Verify all latest amounts/eligibility before the exam.)*

### 📖 Concept

**Cheyutha / Aasara pensions — social security guarantee.**
- Monthly pensions to **the elderly, widows, persons with disabilities, beedi workers, single women, weavers, toddy tappers, HIV+ persons, etc.** (multiple categories).
- The **monthly amount** and category-specific rates are current-affairs (**verify** — e.g., higher rate for the disabled). The scheme continues/expands the earlier **Aasara** pensions.
- Aim: dignified income support for vulnerable groups.

**Rajiv Aarogyasri — health guarantee.**
- **Cashless secondary/tertiary healthcare** for BPL/eligible families at empanelled hospitals, with a **coverage ceiling** (e.g., several lakh ₹ per family per year — **verify latest amount**; recently enhanced).
- One of India's pioneering state health-insurance schemes (predates Ayushman Bharat).
- **Overlaps central Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY, ₹5 lakh)** — the state's convergence/enhancement decision is current-affairs.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Cheyutha/Aasara** = monthly **pensions** for elderly/widows/disabled and other vulnerable categories; **Rajiv Aarogyasri** = **cashless tertiary health cover** (ceiling per family/year), overlapping central **Ayushman Bharat (₹5 lakh)**. Amounts are current-affairs — **verify latest official figure**.

💡 **Example:** An elderly widow receives a monthly **Cheyutha pension** for income support and, if she needs surgery, cashless treatment under **Rajiv Aarogyasri** (or the overlapping Ayushman Bharat) — social security + health together.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Cheyutha/Aasara = pensions** (many categories; disabled get a higher rate).
- **Aarogyasri = cashless tertiary care** (per-family ceiling); pioneering state scheme.
- **Aarogyasri overlaps Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY ₹5 lakh)**.
- Amounts/ceilings: **"verify latest official figure."**

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Cheyutha = monthly pension (elderly/widow/disabled); Aarogyasri = cashless hospital cover (Ayushman overlap).**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Cheyutha = pensions** (social security), **Aarogyasri = health insurance** — don't merge them. Pension rates and the Aarogyasri ceiling are current-affairs; **verify the latest official figure**.

## 🏰 Subject 3: Telangana History
### 🎯 Formation of Telangana & After (2014–Present Political History)

Telangana became a state on **2 June 2014**; the decade since has seen the **BRS (TRS) era**, a **2023 change of government**, and evolving politics. *(Verify latest political developments before the exam.)*

### 📖 Concept

**Formation & first governments.**
- **2 June 2014:** Telangana formed (29th state) under the **AP Reorganisation Act 2014**; **K. Chandrashekar Rao (KCR)** became the **first Chief Minister** (TRS).
- **Hyderabad** = common capital with AP for up to **10 years** (i.e., to 2024); Governor **ESL Narasimhan** was common initially.
- **2018:** KCR/TRS won a second term (early elections).
- **2020:** TRS **renamed BRS (Bharat Rashtra Samithi)** — a national ambition.

**Governance & signature initiatives (2014-2023, TRS/BRS):**
- **Mission Bhagiratha** (piped drinking water to every household), **Mission Kakatiya** (tank restoration), **Rythu Bandhu** (per-acre farm support) & **Rythu Bima**, **Kaleshwaram Lift Irrigation Scheme**, **2BHK housing**, **Aasara pensions**, **KCR Kit**, **Haritha Haram** (afforestation), **new districts (creating 33 districts)**, the **new Secretariat & Telangana Martyrs' Memorial**.

**2023 change & current phase.**
- **December 2023:** the **Congress** won the Assembly election; **A. Revanth Reddy** became **Chief Minister**, ending ~9.5 years of TRS/BRS rule.
- The new government launched the **Six Guarantees** (Days 1-18 topics), the **caste survey**, **HYDRAA**, **Musi rejuvenation**, and the **Kaleshwaram/Medigadda judicial commission**.
- **2024:** the 10-year shared-capital period with AP ended (Hyderabad now solely Telangana's capital — **verify**).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Telangana formed **2 June 2014** (KCR first CM, TRS); TRS **renamed BRS (2020)**; won **2014 & 2018**. **December 2023:** Congress won; **Revanth Reddy** became CM, launching the **Six Guarantees**. Key TRS-era programmes: **Mission Bhagiratha, Kaleshwaram, Rythu Bandhu, Mission Kakatiya**.

💡 **Example:** The decade splits into two phases — **TRS/BRS (2014-2023)** with irrigation/water/farm-support flagships, and **Congress (2023-present)** with the Six Guarantees — a clean before/after that anchors most political-history questions.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **2 June 2014; KCR first CM; 29th state**.
- **TRS → BRS (2020)**; won **2014, 2018**.
- **Dec 2023:** Congress; **Revanth Reddy** CM.
- TRS flagships: **Mission Bhagiratha, Mission Kakatiya, Rythu Bandhu, Kaleshwaram, Haritha Haram**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**2014 KCR/TRS → BRS (2020) → 2023 Congress/Revanth Reddy.** TRS = Bhagiratha/Kakatiya/Kaleshwaram/Rythu Bandhu; Congress = Six Guarantees."

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **KCR** was the **first CM (2014-2023)**; **Revanth Reddy (Congress)** is CM from **Dec 2023**. **TRS renamed BRS in 2020**. Latest developments (post-2024) are current-affairs — **verify the latest official figure/position**.

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4: Indian National Movement (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision: Pre-Congress Associations, Socio-Religious Reform & the Moderate Era

Continuing the **INM revision pass**, today reconsolidates the **early nationalist awakening** — reform movements, pre-Congress associations, and the **Moderate phase (1885-1905)** (Days 2-3).

### 📖 Concept

**Socio-religious reform movements (19th c.):**

| Movement | Founder / year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Brahmo Samaj** | Raja Ram Mohan Roy, 1828 | against sati (abolished 1829), caste, idolatry |
| **Arya Samaj** | Dayananda Saraswati, 1875 | "Back to the Vedas", shuddhi |
| **Ramakrishna Mission** | Vivekananda, 1897 | service, Vedanta |
| **Aligarh Movement** | Syed Ahmad Khan | modern education for Muslims |
| **Prarthana Samaj** | Atmaram Pandurang / Ranade | western India reform |
| **Theosophical Society** | Blavatsky & Olcott; Annie Besant | revivalism |

**Pre-Congress political associations:** **Landholders' Society (1838), British India Association (1851), East India Association (Dadabhai Naoroji, London 1866), Poona Sarvajanik Sabha (1870), Indian Association (Surendranath Banerjee, 1876), Madras Mahajana Sabha (1884), Bombay Presidency Association (1885)**.

**Formation of the INC (1885).** Founded by **A. O. Hume** (a retired British civil servant) with Indian leaders; first session **Bombay, Dec 1885**, presided by **W. C. Bonnerjee**; 72 delegates. (The "safety valve" theory is debated.)

**The Moderate era (1885-1905).** Leaders: **Dadabhai Naoroji ("Grand Old Man", drain theory), Gopal Krishna Gokhale, Pherozeshah Mehta, S. N. Banerjee, W. C. Bonnerjee**.
- **Methods:** **"3 P's" — Petitions, Prayers, Protests**; constitutional agitation, faith in British justice.
- **Demands:** legislative reforms, Indianisation of services, reduction of military expenditure, economic critique (**drain of wealth** — Naoroji's *Poverty and Un-British Rule in India*).
- **Achievements:** created national awakening, economic critique, **Indian Councils Act 1892**; criticised as elitist/limited.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Brahmo Samaj (Ram Mohan Roy, 1828), Arya Samaj (Dayananda, 1875), Ramakrishna Mission (Vivekananda, 1897)**. **INC founded 1885 (A. O. Hume; first president W. C. Bonnerjee, Bombay)**. **Moderates (Naoroji/Gokhale)** used **petitions & prayers** and advanced the **drain-of-wealth** critique.

💡 **Example:** **Dadabhai Naoroji's "drain of wealth"** theory — that Britain was economically bleeding India — became the Moderates' most powerful intellectual weapon and shaped nationalist economics for decades.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Reformers & years:** Brahmo (1828), Arya (1875), RK Mission (1897).
- **INC 1885 — Hume — Bonnerjee — Bombay — 72 delegates**.
- **Moderates: 3 P's; Naoroji drain theory; Indian Councils Act 1892**.
- Distinguish **Moderates (1885-1905)** from **Extremists (Lal-Bal-Pal, post-1905)**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Reform (Brahmo 1828 → Arya 1875 → RK 1897) → INC 1885 (Hume/Bonnerjee) → Moderates (3 P's, Naoroji drain).**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The INC was founded in **1885** by **A. O. Hume** with **W. C. Bonnerjee** as first president (Bombay). **Moderates (petitions/prayers)** ≠ **Extremists (Swadeshi/boycott, post-1905)**. The **drain theory** is **Naoroji's**.

### 🎯 Area Focus: Hyderabad–Rangareddy Urban Region

The **Hyderabad–Rangareddy** urban region is Telangana's economic engine — IT, pharma, the historic city, and the sprawling metropolitan belt inside the ORR.

**🗺️ Reference map — Telangana regions (Hyderabad-Rangareddy urban core):**

### 📖 Concept

**Location & administration.** The urban region spans **Hyderabad district** (fully urban, smallest by area, highest density) plus **Rangareddy, Medchal-Malkajgiri, and Sangareddy** parts (reorganised 2016). Governed by the **GHMC (Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation)** and **HMDA (Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority)**.

**Physiography & geology.**
- On the **Deccan granite plateau** (~500 m), classic **granite tors/boulders** (e.g., around the city); the **Musi river** (Krishna tributary) bisects Hyderabad.
- Reservoirs: **Osman Sagar (Gandipet), Himayat Sagar** (on the Musi/Esi, drinking water), **Hussain Sagar** (Qutb Shahi-era lake, links Hyderabad & Secunderabad), plus **Mission Bhagiratha/Krishna & Godavari** water.

**Economy (the identity).**
- **IT/ITES:** **HITEC City (Madhapur), Cyberabad, Gachibowli-Nanakramguda** financial district — global capability centres.
- **Pharma/life sciences:** **Genome Valley (Shameerpet), Hyderabad Pharma City (Mucherla, Rangareddy)**; "**Vaccine capital**."
- **Aerospace/defence, e-commerce, startups (T-Hub)**; **Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (Shamshabad, Rangareddy)**; **ORR (158 km)**, proposed **RRR**, **Hyderabad Metro**.

**Heritage & culture (the historic core):**
- **Charminar (1591)**, **Golconda Fort**, **Qutb Shahi Tombs**, **Mecca Masjid**, **Chowmahalla Palace**, **Salar Jung Museum**, **Falaknuma Palace** — the Qutb Shahi & Asaf Jahi legacy.
- **Ramoji Film City** (world's largest film studio complex, Rangareddy); **Birla Mandir**; **Buddha statue (Hussain Sagar)**.
- **Cuisine:** Hyderabadi **biryani, haleem**; **Deccani/Dakhni** culture; **Bonalu & Bathukamma** festivals.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Hyderabad-Rangareddy = Telangana's **economic core**: **HITEC City/Cyberabad IT + Genome Valley/Pharma City + RGIA airport + ORR**, governed by **GHMC/HMDA**, on the **Musi** with **Osman Sagar/Himayat Sagar/Hussain Sagar**; heritage crown = **Charminar, Golconda, Qutb Shahi Tombs, Chowmahalla, Salar Jung**.

💡 **Example:** "Geology + culture of Hyderabad" → **granite plateau + Musi river + reservoirs** (geology) with **Charminar/Golconda (Qutb Shahi) + IT/pharma economy + biryani/Bonalu** (culture/economy) — the definitive urban area-focus.
