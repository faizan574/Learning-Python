## 🏛️ Subject 1 — Polity: Local Government — 73rd & 74th Amendments

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **73rd and 74th Amendments (1992)** gave **constitutional status** to local self-government.

**73rd Amendment (Panchayati Raj — rural):** added **Part IX (Art 243-243O)** and the **11th Schedule (29 subjects)**. Key features:

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Structure | **Three-tier**: Gram Panchayat → Mandal/Block (Panchayat Samiti) → **Zilla Parishad** |
| **Gram Sabha** | Village assembly (foundation of the system) |
| Elections | Every **5 years**, by the **State Election Commission** |
| Reservation | SC/ST (proportional) + **women (⅓, often ½)** |
| Finances | **State Finance Commission (Art 243-I)** every 5 years |

**74th Amendment (Municipalities — urban):** added **Part IXA (Art 243P-243ZG)** and the **12th Schedule (18 subjects)**, with three types: **Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — The **73rd Amendment** came into force on **`24 April 1993`** (now **National Panchayati Raj Day**), the **74th** on **1 June 1993**. The **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957)** first recommended the **three-tier** system; the **Ashok Mehta Committee (1977)** suggested a two-tier model.

Telangana enacted the **Telangana Panchayat Raj Act, 2018** (replacing the 1994 Act) to strengthen its rural local bodies.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **73rd = rural (11th Schedule, 29 subjects)**; **74th = urban (12th Schedule, 18 subjects)**. The **Gram Sabha** is the base; elections are run by the **State Election Commission** (not the ECI). **24 April** = Panchayati Raj Day.

### 💡 Example
Reservation of **one-third (now one-half in many states, including Telangana) of seats for women** in panchayats is the amendment's most transformative social feature.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- **73rd (rural)** — Part IX, 11th Schedule (29); **74th (urban)** — Part IXA, 12th Schedule (18).
- In force **24 Apr 1993** / 1 Jun 1993.
- **Balwant Rai Mehta (1957)** = 3-tier; **Ashok Mehta (1977)** = 2-tier.
- Elections by **State Election Commission**; **State Finance Commission** (Art 243-I).

### ✅ Quick Self-Check
*Which Schedules list the subjects for panchayats and municipalities?*
→ The **11th Schedule** (panchayats, 29) and the **12th Schedule** (municipalities, 18).

The **Telangana Budget** and the **Socio-Economic Outlook / Economic Survey** are recurring TGPSC topics — but the **numbers change every year**, so learn the **structure** and always **verify the latest figures**.

| Item | What to know (verify latest official figure) |
|---|---|
| **Budget** | Total outlay, revenue vs capital, key allocations (welfare, irrigation, education) |
| **GSDP & growth** | Telangana's Gross State Domestic Product and growth rate |
| **Per capita income** | Among the **highest** of Indian states |
| **Sector shares** | **Services-dominated** (IT, pharma), with agriculture and industry |

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Telangana's economy is **services-led** (IT/ITES and pharma around Hyderabad), and its **per-capita income is among the highest** among Indian states. ## 🏯 Subject 3 — Telangana History: Operation Polo & Hyderabad State (1948-56)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Operation Polo (the "Police Action"), `13–17 September 1948`,** was the Indian military operation — led by **Major General J.N. Chaudhuri** — that ended the Nizam's bid for independence. The **Razakar** militia (under **Qasim Razvi**) was crushed, and the **Nizam surrendered on `17 September 1948`**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — After the Police Action, **J.N. Chaudhuri** was **Military Governor (1948-49)**, followed by a civil administration under **M.K. Vellodi** as Chief Minister (1949-52). The first **general election (1952)** made **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** the **first (and only) elected Chief Minister of Hyderabad State (1952-56)**. The **Sunderlal Committee (1948)** investigated the post-Police-Action violence.

**States Reorganisation (1956):** the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** **trifurcated** Hyderabad State — the **Telangana** region merged with **Andhra State** to form **Andhra Pradesh (`1 November 1956`)**; the **Marathwada** districts went to Bombay and the **Kannada** districts to Mysore. The **Gentlemen's Agreement (1956)** provided safeguards for Telangana.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Operation Polo (Sept 1948) → Vellodi → Burgula (1952 elected CM) → 1956 merger into Andhra Pradesh**". **17 September** is a landmark date; **Gentlemen's Agreement** = Telangana safeguards.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Operation Polo = 1948** (integration); **formation of Andhra Pradesh = 1 Nov 1956** (reorganisation) — two different events. **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** was the elected CM of **Hyderabad State**; the **Gentlemen's Agreement** preceded the 1956 merger.

### 💡 Example
The **Gentlemen's Agreement (1956)** — safeguards on jobs, education, and development for Telangana — became central to later grievances and the **Telangana statehood movement**, linking 1956 to 2014.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- **Operation Polo** — 13-17 Sep 1948, J.N. Chaudhuri; Nizam surrendered **17 Sep 1948**.
- **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** — first elected CM of Hyderabad State (1952-56).
- **SRA 1956** — Telangana merged with Andhra → **AP (1 Nov 1956)**.
- **Gentlemen's Agreement (1956)** = Telangana safeguards.

### ✅ Quick Self-Check
*Who was the first elected Chief Minister of Hyderabad State, and when was Andhra Pradesh formed?*
→ **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao (1952)**; Andhra Pradesh formed on **1 November 1956**.

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4 — Indian National Movement: Revolutionary Movement Phase I

### 📖 Deep Dive

The first phase of revolutionary nationalism (early 1900s-1910s) grew in **Bengal, Maharashtra, Punjab, and abroad**.

**Maharashtra:** the **Chapekar brothers** assassinated the plague commissioner **Rand (Pune, 1897)**; **Savarkar** founded **Abhinav Bharat (1904)**.

**Bengal:** the **Anushilan Samiti (1902)** and **Jugantar** organised revolutionaries. In the **Alipore Bomb Case**, **Khudiram Bose** and **Prafulla Chaki** attacked (targeting the magistrate **Kingsford**) at **Muzaffarpur (1908)** — **Khudiram was hanged (1908)**, among the youngest martyrs; **Aurobindo Ghosh** was tried and acquitted.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — The **Ghadar Party** was founded in **`1913` at San Francisco (USA)** by **Lala Hardayal, Sohan Singh Bhakna, and Kartar Singh Sarabha**, publishing the **Ghadar** newspaper and planning the **1915 mutiny**. The **Komagata Maru incident (1914)** fuelled its anger. Earlier, **India House, London (Shyamji Krishna Varma, 1905)** nurtured revolutionaries; **Madan Lal Dhingra** shot **Curzon Wyllie (1909)**.

**Punjab:** discontent over the **1907 land laws** (**"Pagdi Sambhal Jatta"**, Ajit Singh & Lala Lajpat Rai).

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Chapekars (1897) → Anushilan/Alipore (Khudiram 1908) → India House (London) → Ghadar (1913, USA) → Komagata Maru (1914)**".

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **Ghadar Party** was formed **abroad (USA, 1913)**, not in India. **Khudiram Bose & Prafulla Chaki** = Muzaffarpur/Alipore (1908). **Abhinav Bharat** = Savarkar. Don't confuse Phase I with Phase II (Bhagat Singh/HSRA, 1920s).

### 💡 Example
The **Komagata Maru incident (1914)** — a ship of would-be Indian immigrants turned away from Canada and fired upon on return — became a powerful **Ghadar** rallying cause, linking overseas racism to the freedom struggle.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- **Chapekar brothers** — Rand assassination (1897).
- **Anushilan Samiti (1902)**; **Khudiram Bose** — Alipore/Muzaffarpur (1908).
- **Ghadar Party (1913, USA)** — Hardayal, Bhakna, Sarabha; **Komagata Maru (1914)**.
- **India House (1905, London)** — Shyamji Krishna Varma; **Dhingra (1909)**.

### ✅ Quick Self-Check
*Where and when was the Ghadar Party founded, and who led it?*
→ In **San Francisco, USA (1913)**, by **Lala Hardayal, Sohan Singh Bhakna, and Kartar Singh Sarabha**.

## 🗺️ Subject 5 — Telangana Geography: Area Focus — Hyderabad-Rangareddy Urban Region

![Telangana Regions Map](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/faizan574/Learning-Python/main/tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_regions.svg)

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **Hyderabad-Rangareddy** urban region is Telangana's **economic and demographic core**. **Hyderabad district** is the **smallest, most densely populated, and fully urban**; **Rangareddy** and **Medchal-Malkajgiri** surround it.

**Governance & water:** the **Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC)** and **Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority (HMDA)** administer the metro. The **Musi** flows through the city; **Osman Sagar (Gandipet)** and **Himayat Sagar** are drinking-water reservoirs; **Hussain Sagar** is the iconic lake.

**Economy:**

| Sector | Highlight |
|---|---|
| **IT / ITES** | **HITEC City, Gachibowli, Financial District** (Cyberabad) |
| **Life sciences** | **Genome Valley**; proposed **Hyderabad Pharma City (Mucherla)** |
| **Infrastructure** | **Outer Ring Road (ORR)**, **Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (Shamshabad)**, **Metro Rail** |
| **Tourism/media** | Charminar, Golconda, **Ramoji Film City** (world's largest film studio complex) |

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Hyderabad district** is Telangana's smallest and **most densely populated**, fully urban. The metro is run by **GHMC/HMDA**, watered by **Osman Sagar & Himayat Sagar**, and anchored by the **HITEC City/Cyberabad** IT hub and **Genome Valley**. *(Verify latest population/economic figures.)*

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Cyberabad + Genome Valley + ORR + RGIA**": IT, biotech, ring road, airport. **GHMC/HMDA** govern; **Osman Sagar/Himayat Sagar** supply water; **Ramoji Film City** in Rangareddy.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Osman Sagar (Gandipet)** and **Himayat Sagar** are on the **Musi/Esi** (drinking water); **Hussain Sagar** links Hyderabad & Secunderabad. **Ramoji Film City** and **RGIA (Shamshabad)** are in **Rangareddy**. Hyderabad district is the **smallest** but **densest**.

### 💡 Example
The concentration of **IT, pharma, and biotech** in the Hyderabad-Rangareddy belt drives Telangana's **services-led, high-per-capita** economy (linking to today's Subject 2) — a strong integrated answer. *(Verify latest.)*

- **HITEC City/Cyberabad** IT; **Genome Valley** biotech; **RGIA Shamshabad**.
- **Ramoji Film City** (Rangareddy) = world's largest film studio.
