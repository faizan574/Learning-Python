## 🏛️ Polity: Governor, Chief Minister & State Legislature

The **state executive** (`Art 153–167`) = Governor + CM + Council of Ministers; the **state legislature** (`Art 168–212`) = Governor + Legislative Assembly (± Council).

**Governor (`Art 153–162`).** The constitutional **head of the state**, appointed by the **President** (holds office during the President's pleasure; usual term 5 years). Qualifications: citizen, **35+**. Powers mirror the President's at state level: **executive** (appoints CM, ministers, Advocate-General, state EC), **legislative** (summons/prorogues, dissolves Assembly, assents to bills, **reserves bills for the President** `Art 200/201`, ordinances `Art 213`, nominates members), **financial**, **judicial** (`Art 161` pardon — narrower than President; **no** pardon for court-martial or death-sentence remission exclusivity). **Discretionary powers** (`Art 163`): reserving bills, appointing CM in a hung House, `Art 356` report, etc. — real discretion unlike the President.

**Chief Minister & Council (`Art 163–164`).** The CM is appointed by the Governor (leader of the majority); the Council of Ministers is **collectively responsible to the Legislative Assembly**. The **91st Amendment** caps state ministers at **15% of Assembly strength** (minimum 12). The CM is the real head of the state executive.

**State Legislature.** **Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha):** directly elected, term 5 years, max **500/min 60** members; the **Speaker** presides. **Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad):** the **upper house** — only in some states (created/abolished by Parliament on the state's resolution, `Art 169`); **permanent** (1/3 retire every 2 years), members by composite election (MLAs, local bodies, teachers, graduates, nominated). Telangana has a **Legislative Council**. A Council is **weaker** (money bills: 14-day delay only; no joint sitting for the state).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Governor = state head, appointed by **President** (`Art 155`), has **real discretion** (`Art 163`); pardon under `Art 161`. CM's Council **collectively responsible to the Assembly**; state ministers ≤ **15%** (91st Amd). Legislative Council (`Art 169`) is **optional**, created/abolished by **Parliament**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Governor = President's state mirror (but real discretion); CM responsible to Assembly; Council optional (Art 169), weaker house.**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Governor appointed by the **President** (not elected). `Art 161` pardon is **narrower** than `Art 72` (no court-martial; cannot pardon a **death sentence** — only President can). A **Legislative Council** is created/abolished by **Parliament** (`Art 169`) on the state Assembly's resolution. State has **no joint sitting**.

💡 **Example:** In a hung Assembly, the **Governor** uses discretion (`Art 163`) to invite a CM — a frequently litigated power.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Governor: Art 153-162; President-appointed; Art 161 pardon; Art 200/201 reserve bills; Art 163 discretion.
- CM & Council: Art 164; responsible to Assembly; 15% cap (91st Amd).
- Assembly: max 500; Council: optional (Art 169), permanent, weaker.
- Telangana has a Legislative Council.

## 🤝 Schemes: The Six Guarantees — Overview (Round 8)

The **Six Guarantees (Aaru Hamీలు)** are the Telangana Congress government's flagship welfare promises (from the 2023 manifesto), rolled out from Dec 2023. A quick consolidated overview (verify current amounts/coverage).

**The six (with their sub-schemes):**
1. **Mahalakshmi** — women-centric: **free RTC bus travel** for women, **₹500 LPG cylinder**, and **₹2,500/month** to eligible women (verify rollout).
2. **Rythu Bharosa** — farmer investment support (₹/acre per season; verify amount) + **₹12,000 to agricultural labourers/tenant** support; **₹2 lakh crop-loan waiver**.
3. **Gruha Jyothi** — **200 units free electricity** to eligible households.
4. **Indiramma Indlu** — **housing** (₹/unit grant; new houses + house-site support).
5. **Cheyutha** — **Rajiv Aarogyasri ₹10 lakh** health cover + **₹4,000 monthly pension** (Aasara-type) for the aged/widows/disabled (verify figures).
6. **Yuva Vikasam** — youth **skill development** + education support (₹5 lakh "Vidya Bharosa" idea; Young India schools/Skills University).

**Delivery mechanism.** Applications were collected via the **Praja Palana** drive (Dec 2023–Jan 2024); grievances via **Praja Vani**. The guarantees dominate the **state budget's welfare** allocation.

## 🏺 History: Telangana Temples, Monuments & Tourism Heritage

Telangana's built heritage spans **Kakatiya, Qutb Shahi and Asaf Jahi** eras — a frequent TGPSC culture topic (match monument to place/dynasty).

**Kakatiya-era temples.** **Ramappa (Rudreshwara) Temple**, Palampet (Mulugu) — **UNESCO World Heritage (2021)**, famous for floating bricks and Nataraja sculptures; **Thousand Pillar Temple**, Hanumakonda; **Warangal Fort & Kakatiya Kala Thoranam** (state emblem arch).

**Vaishnava/Shaiva pilgrimage.** **Bhadrachalam** (Sri Sita Ramachandra Swamy, Godavari); **Yadadri/Yadagirigutta** (Lakshmi Narasimha, redeveloped); **Vemulawada** (Raja Rajeshwara Swamy, "Dakshina Kashi"); **Basara** (Jnana Saraswati, Godavari); **Kaleshwaram** (Shiva); **Alampur** (Jogulamba Shakti Peetha + Navabrahma temples).

**Qutb Shahi/Asaf Jahi monuments (Hyderabad).** **Charminar** (1591, Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah); **Golconda Fort** (acoustics, diamond trade); **Qutb Shahi Tombs**; **Mecca Masjid**; **Chowmahalla Palace**, **Falaknuma Palace**, **Purani Haveli**; **Salar Jung Museum** (one of the world's largest one-man collections); **Public Gardens/Assembly**; **Osmania University/General Hospital** (Osman Ali Khan era).

**Nature/tourism.** Waterfalls (**Kuntala, Bogatha, Pochera, Gayatri**); lakes (**Hussain Sagar** with Buddha statue); hill/eco (Ananthagiri, Nallamala/Amrabad Tiger Reserve).

## 🇮🇳 National Movement: WWII, August Offer, Cripps Mission & Quit India

The WWII years (1939–45) saw a cycle of **British offers and Congress rejections**, climaxing in the **Quit India Movement (1942)**.

**WWII & resignations.** India was declared a belligerent (1939) without consulting Indians; the **Congress provincial ministries resigned (1939)**.

**August Offer (1940).** By Viceroy **Linlithgow** — promised **Dominion status** (eventually), expansion of the Executive Council, and a post-war constituent body; gave minorities a **veto**. **Rejected** by Congress (too little) and the League.

**Cripps Mission (1942).** Sir **Stafford Cripps** offered **Dominion status after the war**, a constituent assembly, and the right of provinces to **secede** (opt out) — but **not immediate self-government**. Gandhi called it "**a post-dated cheque on a crashing bank**." **Rejected** by all.

**Quit India Movement (1942).** The **AICC Bombay session (8 August 1942)** passed the **Quit India Resolution**; Gandhi gave the call "**Do or Die**." The British swiftly arrested the entire leadership. A **spontaneous mass uprising** followed (strikes, sabotage, parallel governments at **Ballia, Tamluk (Bengal), Satara (Maharashtra)**); women like **Aruna Asaf Ali** (hoisted the flag at Gowalia Tank), **Usha Mehta** (underground Congress Radio) led. It was **suppressed** by 1943 but demonstrated the depth of the independence demand — the **last mass movement**.

| Event | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Ministries resign | 1939 | WWII without consultation |
| August Offer | 1940 | Linlithgow; Dominion status (rejected) |
| Cripps Mission | 1942 | Dominion after war (post-dated cheque) |
| Quit India | 1942 | "Do or Die"; Bombay 8 Aug; parallel govts |

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **August Offer (1940, Linlithgow)** → **Cripps Mission (1942, "post-dated cheque")** → **Quit India (8 Aug 1942, Bombay, "Do or Die")**; parallel governments at **Ballia, Tamluk, Satara**; **Aruna Asaf Ali** (Gowalia Tank flag), **Usha Mehta** (Congress Radio).

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**1940 August Offer → 1942 Cripps (post-dated cheque) → 1942 Quit India (Do or Die, Bombay 8 Aug).**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Cripps (1942) = "post-dated cheque"** (Gandhi); it allowed provinces to **secede** (seed of Pakistan demand). **Quit India launched 8 August 1942 (Bombay/Gowalia Tank)**; slogan **"Do or Die."** Aruna Asaf Ali hoisted the flag; Usha Mehta ran the underground radio.

💡 **Example:** Pair *"post-dated cheque on a crashing bank"* → **Cripps Mission**; *"Do or Die, Quit India"* → **Bombay, 8 Aug 1942**.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- 1939 ministries resign; August Offer 1940 (Linlithgow, Dominion status, rejected).
- Cripps 1942: Dominion after war, secession option; rejected (post-dated cheque).
- Quit India 8 Aug 1942 (Bombay); Do or Die; parallel govts (Ballia, Tamluk, Satara).
- Aruna Asaf Ali, Usha Mehta, Jayaprakash Narayan.

## 🗺️ Geography: Area Focus — Hyderabad-Rangareddy Urban Region

The **Hyderabad-Rangareddy (+ Medchal-Malkajgiri)** region is Telangana's **capital metropolitan core** — the economic engine (IT, pharma), on the **Deccan plateau** by the **Musi**.

**Location & physiography.** The **Hyderabad district** (fully urban, GHMC core) plus surrounding **Rangareddy** and **Medchal-Malkajgiri**. It sits on **granite** terrain (famous boulders/tors) at ~**500 m** elevation, drained by the **Musi** (Krishna tributary) with historic lakes (**Hussain Sagar, Osman Sagar, Himayat Sagar, Durgam Cheruvu**). Osman Sagar & Himayat Sagar (on the Musi/Esi) are drinking-water reservoirs.

**Economy & infrastructure.** India's IT hub — **HITEC City/Cyberabad, Gachibowli, Financial District** (Rangareddy); **pharma** (Genome Valley; the planned Hyderabad Pharma City, Mucherla, Rangareddy); the **Outer Ring Road (ORR ~158 km)** encircling the metro; **Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (Shamshabad, Rangareddy)**; **Hyderabad Metro** & **MMTS**; **ICRISAT** nearby. The region concentrates a huge share of state **GSDP**.

**Heritage & urban.** **Charminar, Golconda, Qutb Shahi Tombs, Chowmahalla, Falaknuma, Salar Jung Museum, Mecca Masjid** (old city); **Hussain Sagar/Buddha statue**, **Birla Mandir**, **Ramoji Film City** (Rangareddy), **Nehru Zoological Park**. Governance: **GHMC**, **HMDA**, and **HYDRAA** (lake/land protection).
