## 🏛️ Subject 1 — Polity: DPSP (Art 36-51) & Fundamental Duties (51A)

### Deep Dive

**Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP)** — **Part IV, Articles 36-51**, borrowed from the **Irish Constitution**. They are **non-justiciable** (not enforceable in court) but **fundamental in governance**, aiming at a **welfare state** (social & economic democracy).

**Classification (by nature):**
- **Socialistic** — Art 38 (welfare), **39** (equitable distribution, equal pay), **39A** (free legal aid), **41** (right to work/education), **42** (humane work & maternity relief), **43** (living wage), **47** (nutrition/public health).
- **Gandhian** — **40** (village panchayats), **43** (cottage industries), **46** (promotion of SC/ST/weaker sections), **47** (prohibition of intoxicants), **48** (cow/cattle; organise agriculture).
- **Liberal-intellectual** — **44** (Uniform Civil Code), **45** (early-childhood care/education), **48A** (environment — added by 42nd Amdt), **49** (monuments), **50** (separation of judiciary from executive), **51** (international peace).

**DPSP vs Fundamental Rights.** When they conflict, the courts seek a **balance** (*Minerva Mills*): FRs and DPSPs are **complementary** — "two wheels of the chariot."

**Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Art 51A).** Added by the **42nd Amendment (1976)** on the **Swaran Singh Committee's** recommendation; **originally 10**, now **11** (the **11th** — a parent's duty to provide education, ages 6-14 — added by the **86th Amendment, 2002**). They are **non-justiciable** moral obligations of citizens.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — DPSP (**Part IV, 36-51**, Irish, non-justiciable); FD (**Part IVA, 51A**, 42nd Amdt, **11 duties**, Swaran Singh Committee). Key DPSPs: **40** (panchayats), **44** (UCC), **48A** (environment), **50** (judiciary-executive separation).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Both DPSP and FDs are **non-justiciable**. DPSP from **Ireland**; FDs from the **USSR** model. There are now **11** Fundamental Duties (not 10) — the 86th Amendment added the education duty.

💡 **Example:** **Art 40** (village panchayats) inspired the **73rd Amendment**; **Art 39A** (legal aid) led to **Lok Adalats/NALSA** — DPSPs turned into law.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- DPSP: Part IV (36-51), Irish, non-justiciable; 3 categories.
- Key: 39A, 40, 44, 45, 48A (42nd), 50, 51.
- FDs: Art 51A, 42nd Amdt, 11 duties, Swaran Singh Committee.
- Minerva Mills: FR-DPSP balance.

✅ **Quick self-check:** *How many Fundamental Duties are there now?* → **11** (the 11th added by the 86th Amendment, 2002).

## 🗳️ Subject 2 — Telangana Schemes & Incidents: Mahalakshmi

### Deep Dive

**Mahalakshmi** is the **women-focused** guarantee, bundling three benefits (*verify all amounts against the latest official notification*):
1. **Free bus travel** for women — **zero-ticket (free) travel** on TGSRTC **ordinary/express** services within the state (women and girl children).
2. **Subsidised LPG** — a cooking-gas cylinder at **₹500** for eligible households.
3. **Monthly financial assistance** to eligible women (a fixed monthly amount — *verify the figure and rollout status*).

**Rationale & impact.** The scheme targets **women's mobility, household fuel costs, and direct income support** — boosting female workforce participation and easing cost-of-living pressure. The **free-bus-travel** component substantially raised **women's ridership** on state buses (*verify latest ridership/outlay data*).

**Governance note.** Enrolment ran through **Praja Palana**; the free-travel component is implemented via **TGSRTC** with state reimbursement.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Mahalakshmi** = women's guarantee: **free TGSRTC bus travel + ₹500 LPG + monthly assistance**. Free travel is on **ordinary/express** services (not premium/AC categories — verify).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **monthly-assistance amount and bus-category eligibility change** — write **"verify latest official figure."** Mahalakshmi is the **women's** guarantee; don't confuse its LPG component with the central **Ujjwala** scheme.

💡 **Example:** A woman commuting free on a TGSRTC **Pallevelugu/Express** bus and receiving a **₹500 LPG** cylinder illustrates two of Mahalakshmi's three legs — a common current-affairs PYQ.

## 🏰 Subject 3 — Telangana History: Handlooms & Handicrafts (GI Products)

### Deep Dive

Telangana's **handlooms & handicrafts**, many with **Geographical Indication (GI)** tags, are a high-yield culture topic:

- **Pochampally Ikat** — **tie-and-dye (Ikat)** silk/cotton weaving from **Bhoodan Pochampally** (Yadadri-Bhongir); **GI-tagged**, famed as the "**Silk City**."
- **Gadwal sarees** — **cotton body with silk (zari) pallu/border**; GI (Jogulamba Gadwal).
- **Narayanpet sarees** — distinctive cotton/silk weaves (Narayanpet).
- **Siddipet Gollabhama sarees** — motifs of the **milkmaid (Gollabhama)**; Siddipet.
- **Cheriyal scroll paintings** — **Nakashi** narrative scroll art (near Warangal/Siddipet); **GI**.
- **Nirmal paintings & toys** — lacquered **soft-wood toys & paintings** from **Nirmal** (Adilabad region); **GI**.
- **Bidriware** — **metal inlay (zinc-copper with silver)**; associated with **Bidar/Hyderabad** craft tradition.
- **Karimnagar silver filigree** — fine **silver-wire** ornamental work.
- **Dokra / Adilabad metalcraft** — tribal **lost-wax (cire-perdue)** brass casting.
- **Warangal durries** — woven cotton **carpets/rugs**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Pochampally Ikat** (tie-dye, GI, "Silk City"); **Cheriyal** scroll (Nakashi, GI); **Nirmal** toys/paintings (GI); **Gadwal** sarees (GI); **Bidriware** (metal inlay); **Karimnagar silver filigree**; **Dokra** (lost-wax, Adilabad).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Pochampally = Ikat (tie-dye)**; **Cheriyal = scroll painting (Nakashi)**; **Nirmal = toys/paintings**; **Bidri = metal inlay** — keep craft-to-place pairings exact. Several carry **GI tags** (common PYQ).

💡 **Example:** "Which craft is called Ikat and comes from a town also famous for the Bhoodan movement?" → **Pochampally Ikat** (Bhoodan Pochampally).

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Pochampally Ikat (GI); Gadwal/Narayanpet/Siddipet sarees.
- Cheriyal scrolls (Nakashi, GI); Nirmal toys/paintings (GI).
- Bidriware (inlay); Karimnagar silver filigree; Dokra (Adilabad).

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4 — Indian National Movement: Partition of Bengal, Swadeshi & the Extremists; Surat Split

### Deep Dive

**Partition of Bengal (1905).** Viceroy **Lord Curzon** partitioned Bengal (effective **`16 October 1905`**), ostensibly for administrative convenience but widely seen as **"divide and rule"** (splitting Hindu-majority west from Muslim-majority east Bengal).

**Swadeshi & Boycott Movement.** The partition triggered the **Swadeshi (use Indian goods) and Boycott (of British goods)** movement — the first mass anti-colonial agitation. Features: **boycott** of foreign cloth, promotion of **swadeshi** industries, **national education** (Bengal National College), and **"Vande Mataram"** as a rallying cry.

**Rise of the Extremists.** The movement energised the **Extremists (Garam Dal)** — **Lal-Bal-Pal**: **Lala Lajpat Rai, Bal Gangadhar Tilak** ("**Swaraj is my birthright and I shall have it**"), and **Bipin Chandra Pal**, with **Aurobindo Ghosh**. They favoured **passive resistance, boycott and self-reliance** over Moderate petitions.

**Surat Split (1907).** At the **Surat session (1907)**, the Congress **split** into **Moderates and Extremists** over the pace of agitation and the presidency; the party remained divided until the **Lucknow Pact (1916)** reunion.

**Reversal.** The British **annulled the partition in `1911`** (and shifted the **capital from Calcutta to Delhi**).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Partition of Bengal `1905`** (Curzon) → **Swadeshi/Boycott** + Vande Mataram; **Extremists = Lal-Bal-Pal**; **Surat Split `1907`**; partition **annulled `1911`** (capital to Delhi).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **Surat Split (1907)** divided the Congress into Moderates/Extremists; reunion came at **Lucknow (1916)**. The **1905** partition was **reversed in 1911**, not 1947. "Swaraj is my birthright" is **Tilak's**.

💡 **Example:** The **Swadeshi movement's** national-education strand (Bengal National College, Aurobindo) and **Tilak's** birthright slogan are classic Extremist-era PYQs.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Partition of Bengal 1905 (Curzon, 16 Oct); divide and rule.
- Swadeshi & Boycott; Vande Mataram; national education.
- Lal-Bal-Pal (Lajpat Rai, Tilak, Bipin Chandra Pal).
- Surat Split 1907; partition annulled 1911 (capital to Delhi).

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Who said "Swaraj is my birthright and I shall have it"?* → **Bal Gangadhar Tilak**.

## 🗺️ Subject 5 — Telangana Geography: Area Focus — Warangal & the Kakatiya Belt

### Deep Dive

**Warangal (Orugallu)** was the **capital of the Kakatiya dynasty** and is the cultural-historical heart of north-central Telangana. The region now spans several districts — **Warangal, Hanamkonda, Jangaon, Mahabubabad, Jayashankar Bhupalpally, Mulugu** (*verify current boundaries*).

**Physiography.** Part of the **Telangana plateau**, drained by **Godavari tributaries**; dotted with **Kakatiya-era tanks (cheruvu)** — **Pakhal, Laknavaram, Ramappa** lakes — a model of medieval **chain-tank irrigation**.

**Heritage (high-yield):**
- **Warangal Fort** — Kakatiya capital; the iconic **Kakatiya Kala Thoranam** (the ornamental gateway in the **state emblem**).
- **Thousand Pillar Temple** (Hanamkonda) — Kakatiya architecture (Rudreshwara/Trikutalaya).
- **Ramappa Temple (Rudreshwara), Palampet** — a **UNESCO World Heritage Site (inscribed 2021)**, famed for its **floating bricks** and **Nandi**.
- **Bhadrakali Temple** (Warangal).

**Natural & tribal:** **Pakhal and Eturnagaram Wildlife Sanctuaries**; **Laknavaram** suspension-bridge lake; the **Medaram (Sammakka-Saralamma) jatara** in **Mulugu**; the **Pembarti** metalcraft.

![Telangana regions base map — locate the Warangal/Kakatiya belt and its tank-irrigation heartland](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/faizan574/Learning-Python/main/tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_regions.svg)

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Warangal = **Kakatiya capital (Orugallu)**; **Kakatiya Kala Thoranam** (state emblem); **Ramappa Temple (Palampet)** = **UNESCO WHS 2021**; **Thousand Pillar Temple** (Hanamkonda); Kakatiya tanks (**Pakhal, Laknavaram, Ramappa**).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Ramappa (Rudreshwara) at Palampet** is the UNESCO site (2021), **not** the Thousand Pillar Temple. The **Kakatiya Kala Thoranam** (Warangal fort gateway) features in the **state emblem**.

💡 **Example:** **Ramappa Temple's UNESCO World Heritage status (2021)** is a very common current-affairs + heritage PYQ; the **Kakatiya tanks** a classic geography one.

- Warangal = Kakatiya capital (Orugallu).
- Ramappa (Palampet) UNESCO WHS 2021; Thousand Pillar Temple (Hanamkonda).
- Kakatiya Kala Thoranam → state emblem.
- Tanks: Pakhal, Laknavaram, Ramappa; Medaram (Mulugu).

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Which Telangana temple became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2021?* → the **Ramappa (Rudreshwara) Temple, Palampet**.
