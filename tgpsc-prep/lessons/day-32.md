## 🏛️ Subject 1 — Polity: DPSP (Art 36-51) & Fundamental Duties (51A)

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP)** are in **Part IV, Articles `36-51`**, borrowed from the **Irish Constitution**. They are **non-justiciable** (`Art 37` — not enforceable in court) but **fundamental in the governance** of the country.

**Classification (by nature):**

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Socialistic** | `Art 38` (welfare state), `39` (equitable resources, equal pay), `39A` (free legal aid), `41`, `42`, `43` (living wage), `43A` (worker participation), `47` (nutrition, public health) |
| **Gandhian** | `Art 40` (village panchayats), `43` (cottage industries), `46` (SC/ST/weaker sections), `47` (prohibition), `48` (cow slaughter ban) |
| **Liberal-Intellectual** | `Art 44` (Uniform Civil Code), `45` (early childhood care), `48` (agriculture/animal husbandry), `48A` (environment), `49` (monuments), `50` (separate judiciary from executive), `51` (international peace) |

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Several DPSPs were added by the **42nd Amendment (1976)**: `39A` (equal justice/free legal aid), `43A` (participation of workers), and `48A` (protection of environment). In **Minerva Mills (1980)**, the SC held that a **balance between Fundamental Rights and DPSP** is part of the **basic structure**.

**Fundamental Duties** are in **Part IVA, Article `51A`**, added by the **42nd Amendment (1976)** on the **Swaran Singh Committee's** recommendation. Originally **`10` duties**; the **`11th`** (duty of a parent/guardian to provide education to children aged 6-14) was added by the **86th Amendment (2002)** — total **`11`**. They are **non-justiciable**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — DPSP came from **Ireland**; Fundamental Duties from the erstwhile **USSR** (added later, not in the original Constitution). There are now **11** Fundamental Duties (not 10). `Art 44` (UCC) is a DPSP, **not** a Fundamental Right.

### 💡 Example
When a court cannot enforce a DPSP directly, it often reads it **with a Fundamental Right** (e.g. `Art 21` + `Art 48A` for the right to a clean environment) — showing how directives gain teeth indirectly.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- DPSP = **Part IV, Art 36-51**, from **Ireland**, **non-justiciable** (Art 37).
- 42nd Amendment added **39A, 43A, 48A**.
- **Fundamental Duties** = Part IVA, **Art 51A**, 42nd Amendment, now **11** (11th by 86th).
- **Art 44** = UCC (a DPSP).

→ From **Ireland**; they are **non-justiciable** (Art 37).

## 🏯 Subject 3 — Telangana History: Musunuri Nayaks, Recherla Velamas & Bahmani Kingdom

### 📖 Deep Dive

After the **Kakatiyas fell in `1323`**, the Delhi Sultanate occupied Warangal (renamed **Sultanpur**). Telangana's history then passed through three players.

**Musunuri Nayaks:** **Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka** and his cousin **Kapaya Nayaka** led a **confederation of about 75 nayakas** and **recaptured Warangal (~`1336`)** from the Delhi Sultanate, briefly restoring indigenous rule. **Kapaya Nayaka** (r. ~`1336-1368`) is the best-known; the **Vilasa grant** records Prolaya's efforts to restore order.

**Recherla Velamas (Padmanayakas):** ruling from **Rachakonda and Devarakonda**, they rose as the Musunuri power waned, fighting the **Bahmanis** and the **Reddis** of Kondavidu. Rulers include **Singama Nayaka** and **Anapota Nayaka**.

**Bahmani Kingdom:** founded in **`1347`** by **Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah (Hasan Gangu)** — the **first independent Muslim kingdom in the Deccan**. Capital first at **Gulbarga (Ahsanabad)**, later **Bidar (Muhammadabad)**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — The great Bahmani statesman **Mahmud Gawan** (prime minister under Muhammad Shah III) expanded the kingdom and reformed administration, but was **executed in `1481`**. The Bahmani kingdom later fragmented into the **five Deccan Sultanates**: **Bijapur (Adil Shahi), Golconda (Qutb Shahi), Ahmadnagar (Nizam Shahi), Bidar (Barid Shahi), and Berar (Imad Shahi)** — of which **Golconda** covered Telangana.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Kapaya frees Warangal → Velamas at Rachakonda → Bahmani (1347) → 5 Sultanates**". Telangana's slice of the split is **Golconda (Qutb Shahi)**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Note the sequence: **Musunuri** (Telugu revivalists) → **Recherla Velamas** → **Bahmani** dominance. **Mahmud Gawan's execution (1481)** hastened the Bahmani decline. The **Qutb Shahis of Golconda** are Telangana's successor state.

### 💡 Example
The **Vilasa grant** of the Musunuri period is a key source describing the chaos after the Kakatiya fall and the nayakas' effort to restore order — a favourite source-based question.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- **Kapaya Nayaka** — recaptured Warangal (~1336), confederation of 75 nayakas.
- **Recherla Velamas** — Rachakonda & Devarakonda.
- **Bahmani** founded **1347** by **Hasan Gangu**; **Mahmud Gawan** executed **1481**.
- Five Sultanates: Bijapur, **Golconda**, Ahmadnagar, Bidar, Berar.

### ✅ Quick Self-Check
*Who founded the Bahmani kingdom and in which year, and which successor state covered Telangana?*
→ **Hasan Gangu (Bahman Shah)** in **`1347`**; **Golconda (Qutb Shahi)**.

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4 — Indian National Movement: Subhas Bose & the INA; RIN Revolt

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Subhas Chandra Bose** was elected **Congress President** at **Haripura (1938)** and again at **Tripuri (1939)** — where he defeated Gandhi's nominee **Pattabhi Sitaramayya** but **resigned** amid Gandhi's opposition, then founded the **Forward Bloc (1939)**.

Escaping India in `1941`, Bose reached **Germany**, then **Japanese-occupied Southeast Asia**. The **Indian National Army (INA / Azad Hind Fauj)** — first raised by **Mohan Singh** in `1942` from Indian POWs — was **revived and led by Bose from `1943`**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — In **October `1943`, Bose proclaimed the Provisional Government of Free India (Azad Hind)** at **Singapore**. The INA had a women's **Rani of Jhansi Regiment** (led by **Lakshmi Sahgal**). Bose's slogans: "**Give me blood, and I shall give you freedom**," "**Delhi Chalo**," and "**Jai Hind**." The INA fought alongside the Japanese at **Imphal-Kohima (1944)**.

Bose is officially reported to have **died in a plane crash at Taipei on `18 August 1945`**.

**INA Trials (Red Fort, 1945-46):** three officers — **Prem Kumar Sahgal, Shah Nawaz Khan, Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon** — were tried together; the defence (Bhulabhai Desai, Nehru, others) and huge **public outrage** forced leniency.

**RIN Revolt (Royal Indian Navy Mutiny, Feb `1946`):** ratings of **HMIS Talwar** in **Bombay** mutinied over poor conditions and racial discrimination; it spread to **~78 ships and 20,000 ratings**. Though suppressed (with Patel and Jinnah persuading surrender), it signalled that Britain **could no longer rely on the Indian armed forces** — a major push toward independence.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Tripuri → Forward Bloc → Azad Hind (1943) → Red Fort trials → RIN revolt (1946)**". Netaji: "**Delhi Chalo**", "**Jai Hind**".

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **INA was first formed by Mohan Singh (1942)**, then **revived by Bose (1943)**. The **Provisional Government (Azad Hind)** was declared at **Singapore, Oct 1943**. The **RIN revolt (1946)** was a naval mutiny, distinct from the INA.

### 💡 Example
The **INA trials** turned the accused into national heroes; the surge of public sympathy (cutting across communities) is often cited as a decisive factor pushing Britain toward a swift transfer of power.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- Bose: **Tripuri 1939**, resigned; **Forward Bloc 1939**.
- **INA** — Mohan Singh (1942), revived by **Bose (1943)**; **Azad Hind** Singapore Oct 1943.
- **Rani of Jhansi Regiment** — Lakshmi Sahgal.
- **INA trials** at Red Fort (1945-46); **RIN revolt** Feb **1946**.

### ✅ Quick Self-Check
*Where and when did Bose proclaim the Provisional Government of Free India?*
→ At **Singapore**, in **October 1943**.

## 🗺️ Subject 5 — Telangana Geography: Area Focus — Warangal & the Kakatiya Belt

![Telangana Regions Map](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/faizan574/Learning-Python/main/tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_regions.svg)

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **Warangal** region — the **Kakatiya heartland** — has been reorganised into several districts: **Warangal (Hanumakonda), Warangal Rural, Jangaon, Jayashankar Bhupalpally, Mahabubabad, and Mulugu** *(verify current boundaries)*.

**Physiography & water:** it lies on the **Deccan granite plateau** within the **Godavari basin**. The Kakatiyas endowed it with great **tanks** — **Pakhal, Laknavaram, Ramappa** lakes — still central to irrigation.

**Heritage (Kakatiya belt):**

| Site | Significance |
|---|---|
| **Warangal Fort & Orugallu** | Kakatiya capital; **Kala Thoranam** (state emblem) |
| **Thousand Pillar Temple**, Hanamkonda | Trikutalaya (Shiva/Vishnu/Surya) |
| **Ramappa (Palampet, Mulugu)** | **UNESCO World Heritage 2021** |
| **Medaram (Mulugu)** | **Sammakka-Saralamma Jatara** — Asia's largest tribal fair |

**Economy & infrastructure:** paddy and cotton agriculture; **Warangal is a traditional handloom/textile hub** (a **Kakatiya Mega Textile Park** has been developed — verify status). **Kazipet** is a major **railway junction**; **Kakatiya Thermal Power Station** (Chelpur, Bhupalpally) and **Singareni coal** (Bhupalpally) anchor energy. **Pakhal and Eturnagaram** are wildlife sanctuaries; **Bogatha** waterfall is in Mulugu.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — The Warangal/Kakatiya belt holds **three iconic heritage sites** — **Warangal Fort, Thousand Pillar Temple, and Ramappa (UNESCO 2021)** — plus **Medaram**, host of the **Sammakka-Saralamma Jatara**, Asia's largest tribal congregation. *(Verify latest figures for the textile park and power capacity.)*

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Orugallu + tanks + Medaram**": Kakatiya capital, the great irrigation lakes, and the giant tribal jatara. **Kazipet = rail junction**; **Ramappa = UNESCO 2021**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Ramappa** is at **Palampet in Mulugu** district (not Warangal city). **Medaram (Sammakka-Saralamma Jatara)** is also in **Mulugu**. The old Warangal district has been **split** — quote current boundaries only after **verifying**.

### 💡 Example
The **Kakatiya tank network** around Warangal was the model for the modern **"Mission Kakatiya"** minor-irrigation revival — a static-to-current linkage examiners like.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- Kakatiya capital **Orugallu (Warangal)**; **Kala Thoranam** = state emblem.
- **Ramappa** (Palampet, Mulugu) — UNESCO 2021.
- **Medaram** — Sammakka-Saralamma Jatara (Mulugu).
- **Kazipet** rail junction; tanks: **Pakhal, Laknavaram, Ramappa**.
