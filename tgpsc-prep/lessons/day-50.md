## 🏛️ Polity: Fundamental Rights — Art 23-30 & Art 32 (Writs)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Right against Exploitation (Art 23-24):**
- **Art 23** — prohibits **traffic in human beings, begar and forced labour**. Enforced via the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976. (The State may impose compulsory service for public purposes, e.g. military service, without discrimination.)
- **Art 24** — prohibits employment of **children below 14** in factories, mines or hazardous work.

**Right to Freedom of Religion (Art 25-28):**
- **Art 25** — freedom of conscience and free profession, practice and propagation of religion (subject to public order, morality, health).
- **Art 26** — freedom to manage religious affairs (establish institutions, own property).
- **Art 27** — no compulsion to pay taxes for promotion of any particular religion.
- **Art 28** — freedom from religious instruction in wholly State-funded educational institutions.

**Cultural & Educational Rights (Art 29-30):**
- **Art 29** — protection of interests of **minorities** (language, script, culture); no denial of admission on grounds of religion/race/caste/language.
- **Art 30** — right of **minorities** (religious/linguistic) to **establish and administer educational institutions** of their choice.

**Right to Constitutional Remedies (Art 32)** — **Dr. Ambedkar called Art 32 the "heart and soul of the Constitution."** It guarantees the right to move the **Supreme Court** directly for enforcement of Fundamental Rights, empowering it to issue **five writs**:

| Writ | Meaning / Use |
|---|---|
| **Habeas Corpus** | "produce the body" — against unlawful detention (issued to private persons too) |
| **Mandamus** | "we command" — directs a public authority to do its duty (not against private, President/Governor) |
| **Prohibition** | "stay" — issued by a higher court to stop a lower court exceeding jurisdiction (before judgment) |
| **Certiorari** | "to be certified" — quash an order of a lower court/tribunal (after judgment; can transfer) |
| **Quo-Warranto** | "by what authority" — questions the legality of a person holding a public office |

The **High Courts** have wider writ power under **Art 226** (for FRs **and** other legal rights). Art 32 itself is a Fundamental Right; it can be suspended only during Emergency (except Art 20/21).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Art 23 (forced labour/trafficking), Art 24 (child labour <14). Art 25-28 (religion). Art 29-30 (minority rights). **Art 32 = "heart and soul" (Ambedkar)** → 5 writs. **Art 226** gives HCs wider writ jurisdiction (FRs + legal rights).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Habeas Corpus** can be issued against **private** individuals; **Mandamus** cannot (only public authorities, and not the President/Governor personally). Art 30 minority-institution right — note Art 29 is broader (also protects any citizen from admission denial on those grounds).

## 🎁 Telangana Schemes: SIR (Special Intensive Revision) & Electoral Roll Issues

### 📖 Deep Dive

**SIR — Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls** is an **Election Commission of India (ECI)** exercise to thoroughly revise and clean up the voter list — door-to-door verification, removing duplicate/dead/shifted entries and adding eligible new voters — as distinct from the routine **Summary Revision**. It became a significant political and administrative issue nationally (notably discussed around the Bihar exercise) and in the Telangana context (*verify the latest status, coverage and any court directions before the exam*).

Key points (verify latest):
- **Purpose** — ensure an accurate, error-free electoral roll (no bogus/duplicate/ineligible entries; no genuine voter wrongly deleted).
- **Concerns raised** — risk of **wrongful deletions** of genuine voters, documentation burden, and the timing relative to elections; parties and civil society have debated the process, and it has seen **judicial scrutiny** (*verify current court status*).
- **Distinction** — **Intensive** revision = fresh house-to-house enumeration; **Summary** revision = updating the existing roll with claims/objections.
- Related concepts: **electoral roll**, **EPIC** (voter ID), **Form 6/7/8** (inclusion/objection/correction), and the constitutional basis (**Art 324** — ECI superintendence; **Art 326** — universal adult suffrage, voting age 18).

This links to Polity (Art 324/326, Election Commission) — a useful cross-subject connection.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **SIR = Special Intensive Revision** of electoral rolls by the **ECI** (house-to-house verification to clean the roll). Distinct from **Summary Revision**. Concerns: wrongful deletions, timing, documentation — under judicial/political scrutiny. **Verify latest status.**

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — SIR is an **electoral-roll** exercise (Election Commission), not a welfare scheme in the Six-Guarantees sense — it appears here under "important incidents." Distinguish **Intensive** (fresh enumeration) from **Summary** revision.

### 💡 Example

*Q: What does SIR aim to achieve?* → An accurate electoral roll via intensive (house-to-house) verification — adding eligible voters and removing duplicate/ineligible entries.

- Intensive = fresh enumeration; Summary = update.
- Constitutional basis: **Art 324** (ECI), **Art 326** (adult suffrage, 18).

## 🏰 Telangana History: Cuisine, Attire, Tribes & State Symbols (Living Heritage)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Cuisine.** Telangana food is millet/jowar-based and spicy: **Sarva Pindi/Ginne Appa, Sakinalu** (festive snack), **Jonna rotte** (jowar roti), **Golichina Mamsam, Natu Kodi pulusu** (country-chicken curry), **Pachi pulusu**, **Qubani ka Meetha** and **Hyderabadi Biryani/Haleem** (Haleem has a **GI tag**). Bathukamma/Bonalu foods feature too.

**Attire & culture.** Traditional handloom sarees (Pochampally, Gadwal); tribal ornaments and dress (Gond, Lambada/Banjara colourful embroidery and jewellery).

**Major Tribes.** Telangana has notable Scheduled Tribes: **Gonds** (Adilabad; Dandari-Gussadi, Nagoba jatara), **Koyas** (Bhadradri/Godavari belt; Sammakka-Saralamma), **Lambadas/Banjaras** (largest ST group, tandas), **Chenchus** (Nallamala food-gathering tribe), **Kolams, Andhs, Pardhans, Thotis**. **Komaram Bheem** (Gond leader, "Jal-Jangal-Jameen") is an icon.

**State Symbols of Telangana:**

| Symbol | Name |
|---|---|
| State animal | **Jinka (Deer / Chinkara)** |
| State bird | **Palapitta (Indian Roller)** |
| State tree | **Jammi Chettu (Prosopis cineraria)** |
| State flower | **Tangedu (Tanner's Cassia)** |
| State festival | **Bathukamma** (and Bonalu) |
| State language | **Telugu** (Urdu also official/second) |

**Telangana Formation Day** (2 June) and the revival of state cultural symbols were part of asserting Telangana identity post-2014.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — State symbols: **animal Jinka**, **bird Palapitta (Indian Roller)**, **tree Jammi**, **flower Tangedu**, **festival Bathukamma**. **Haleem = GI tag**. Major tribes: **Gond, Koya, Lambada (largest), Chenchu**. Komaram Bheem = Gond icon ("Jal-Jangal-Jameen").

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — State **bird = Palapitta (Indian Roller)**, **flower = Tangedu**, **tree = Jammi** — a favourite matching question. **Lambadas/Banjaras** are the **largest** ST group; **Chenchus** are a Nallamala food-gathering tribe.

## 🇮🇳 Indian National Movement: Cabinet Mission to Independence & Partition

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Wavell Plan & Simla Conference (1945)** — Viceroy Wavell's attempt to reconstitute the Executive Council failed over the Muslim League's insistence on nominating all Muslim members.

**Cabinet Mission (1946)** — three British ministers (**Pethick-Lawrence, Stafford Cripps, A.V. Alexander**) proposed a **united India** with a **three-tier federation** (groups of provinces, a weak centre handling defence/foreign affairs/communications) — **rejecting the demand for a full Pakistan**. It provided for a **Constituent Assembly** (elected by provincial assemblies). Both Congress and League initially accepted, then differences over "grouping" broke it down.

**Direct Action Day (16 August 1946)** — the Muslim League, having withdrawn acceptance, called for direct action; it led to the **Great Calcutta Killings** and communal riots.

**Interim Government (Sept 1946)** — formed under **Jawaharlal Nehru**; the League joined later (Liaquat Ali Khan as Finance Member) but obstructed from within.

**Mountbatten Plan (3 June 1947)** — Viceroy **Louis Mountbatten** proposed **partition**: British India to be divided into **India and Pakistan**; provinces/assemblies to decide; Bengal & Punjab to be partitioned; a **referendum** in NWFP and Sylhet; princely states to accede.

**Independence & Partition** — the **Indian Independence Act 1947** (British Parliament) gave effect to it. **Pakistan on 14 August, India on 15 August 1947.** The **Radcliffe Line** (Sir Cyril Radcliffe) demarcated the boundary, announced after independence, triggering massive migration and violence. **Lord Mountbatten** became free India's first Governor-General; **C. Rajagopalachari** succeeded him (first Indian G-G).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Cabinet Mission 1946** (Pethick-Lawrence, Cripps, Alexander; united India, 3-tier, no Pakistan; Constituent Assembly). **Direct Action Day 16 Aug 1946**. **Mountbatten Plan 3 Jun 1947** → **partition**; **Indian Independence Act 1947**; **Radcliffe Line**. Independence **15 Aug 1947**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **Cabinet Mission (1946) rejected** the demand for a separate Pakistan and proposed a **united** India with grouping. Partition came via the **Mountbatten Plan (3 Jun 1947)**. The Constituent Assembly was set up under the **Cabinet Mission**, not the 1947 Act.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Which plan proposed the partition of India?* → The **Mountbatten Plan (3 June 1947)**, implemented by the Indian Independence Act 1947.

## 🗺️ Telangana Geography: Industries & Transport

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Industry.** Hyderabad anchors Telangana's economy as a national hub for:
- **IT/ITES** — **HITEC City, Gachibowli, Financial District** (Cyberabad); Telangana is a top IT-exporting state (*verify latest export figures*); global tech campuses.
- **Pharma & life sciences** — **Genome Valley** (India's first organised life-sciences cluster) and the upcoming **Hyderabad Pharma City** (one of the world's largest planned pharma clusters — verify status); Hyderabad is called the **"Vaccine Capital / Bulk Drug Capital"** (Bharat Biotech, etc.).
- **Aerospace & defence** — around **Adibatla** (Aerospace SEZ); DRDO/HAL/BDL/ECIL/DLRL public-sector units.
- **Others** — cement (Nalgonda limestone belt), granite, textiles (Kakatiya Mega Textile Park, Warangal), Singareni coal, engineering.

**Transport.**
- **Roads** — dense highway network; the **Outer Ring Road (ORR, ~158 km)** around Hyderabad and the planned **Regional Ring Road (RRR)**; National Highways (NH-44 the longest N-S highway passes through, NH-65, NH-163).
- **Rail** — South Central Railway (HQ **Secunderabad**); Kazipet is a major junction; **MMTS** (Multi-Modal Transport System) suburban rail; **Hyderabad Metro Rail** (one of India's largest metro PPP projects).
- **Air** — **Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, Shamshabad (GMR)** — a major international hub.
- **Inland** — limited; the Godavari/Krishna are not major navigation routes.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **IT** (HITEC City/Cyberabad), **Pharma** (Genome Valley, Pharma City; "Vaccine Capital"), **Aerospace** (Adibatla). Transport: **ORR (~158 km)** + planned **RRR**; **SCR HQ Secunderabad**; **Hyderabad Metro**; **RGIA Shamshabad**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Genome Valley** = life sciences cluster; **HITEC City** = IT — don't swap. **NH-44** (longest in India) passes through Telangana. SCR headquarters = **Secunderabad**.
