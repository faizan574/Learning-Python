## 🏛️ Subject 1 — Polity: Citizenship (Articles 5-11, Citizenship Act 1955, CAA)

### Deep Dive

India has **single citizenship** (unlike the USA's dual national/state citizenship). **Articles 5-11 (Part II)** deal only with **citizenship at the commencement** of the Constitution; the detailed law is left to Parliament.

**Constitutional provisions:**
- **Art 5** — citizenship at commencement: a person **domiciled** in India **and** either **born** in India, or **either parent born** in India, or **ordinarily resident** for `5` years before commencement.
- **Art 6** — rights of migrants **from Pakistan** to India.
- **Art 7** — rights of migrants who went **to Pakistan** (and returned).
- **Art 8** — citizenship of **persons of Indian origin residing abroad**.
- **Art 9** — voluntarily acquiring **foreign citizenship** ends Indian citizenship (**no dual citizenship**).
- **Art 10** — continuance of citizenship.
- **Art 11** — **Parliament's power** to regulate citizenship by law.

**Citizenship Act, 1955.** Acquisition by **(1) birth, (2) descent, (3) registration, (4) naturalisation, (5) incorporation of territory**. Loss by **renunciation, termination** (acquiring foreign citizenship), or **deprivation** (by government). The Act also provides the **Overseas Citizen of India (OCI)** card — **not** dual citizenship and **without** voting/office rights.

**CAA (Citizenship Amendment Act, 2019).** Amended the 1955 Act to offer a **faster path to citizenship** for **Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi and Christian** migrants from **Pakistan, Bangladesh and Afghanistan** who entered India **on or before `31 December 2014`**. It was highly debated; the **rules were notified in 2024** (*verify latest status/implementation*).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — India = **single citizenship**; **Art 11** lets Parliament legislate (→ **Citizenship Act 1955**). CAA 2019 covers **6 non-Muslim communities** from **3 countries**, cutoff `31 Dec 2014`.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **OCI is not dual citizenship** (no vote, no public office, no constitutional posts). Arts 5-11 fix citizenship **at commencement only** — all later rules come from the **1955 Act**, not the Constitution directly.

💡 **Example:** A person acquiring another country's passport **automatically loses** Indian citizenship under Art 9 / the 1955 Act — India permits **no dual citizenship**, only the limited OCI status.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Arts 5-11: citizenship at commencement; **Art 11** = Parliament's power.
- Citizenship Act 1955: 5 modes of acquisition; 3 modes of loss.
- OCI ≠ dual citizenship.
- CAA 2019: 6 communities, 3 countries, cutoff 31 Dec 2014 (verify status).

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Does India allow dual citizenship?* → **No** — only the limited **OCI** card, which is not citizenship.

## 🗳️ Subject 2 — Telangana Schemes & Incidents: Budgets & Economic Survey Highlights

### Deep Dive

The **Telangana State Budget** is presented annually by the **Finance Minister** in the Legislative Assembly, alongside a **Socio-Economic Outlook / Economic Survey** that summarises the state's economy. *(In the current government the Finance/Deputy CM handles the budget — verify the presenter and all figures for the latest year.)*

**Key budget concepts (know the structure, verify the numbers):**
- **Total outlay** split into **Revenue expenditure** (salaries, subsidies, welfare) and **Capital expenditure** (assets, irrigation, roads).
- **Receipts:** own tax revenue (**GST/SGST, stamps & registration, excise, motor vehicle tax**), own non-tax revenue, central transfers/grants, and borrowings.
- **Deficits:** **Revenue deficit/surplus**, **Fiscal deficit** (target `≤ 3%` of GSDP under the **FRBM Act**), and outstanding debt as a share of GSDP.

**Economy — recurring highlights (verify latest data):**
- Telangana has one of India's **higher per-capita incomes** and a large **services sector** share (IT/ITeS around Hyderabad).
- Sector split: **services** dominate GSVA, followed by **industry** and **agriculture**.
- Welfare spending is a large share of the budget (the **Six Guarantees**).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Under the **FRBM Act**, the fiscal deficit target is **`≤ 3%` of GSDP** (with conditional relaxations). Telangana's economy is **services-led**, anchored by Hyderabad's IT/pharma.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **All budget numbers** (total outlay, GSDP, per-capita income, deficit) **change every year** — write **"verify latest official figure"** and never quote a stale amount in the exam. Know the **structure and terms**, which don't change.

💡 **Example:** A question on "the fiscal-deficit ceiling under FRBM" tests the **`3%` of GSDP** norm — a stable, high-value fact — whereas the exact rupee outlay must be checked against the latest budget.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Budget = Revenue + Capital expenditure; receipts = tax + non-tax + transfers + borrowings.
- FRBM fiscal-deficit target: **≤ 3% of GSDP**.
- Economy: **services-led** (IT, pharma); high per-capita income.
- Always **verify latest** rupee figures.

✅ **Quick self-check:** *What is the FRBM fiscal-deficit target as a share of GSDP?* → about **3%** (with permitted relaxations).

## 🏰 Subject 3 — Telangana History: 1970-2000 — TPS, Naxalism & Identity Politics

### Deep Dive

After the **1969 agitation**, the **Telangana Praja Samithi (TPS)** contested the 1971 elections and won many seats, but soon **merged with the Congress (1971)** — dampening the statehood demand for a time. The next three decades saw two powerful currents.

**Naxalite / Maoist movement.** Inspired by the **Naxalbari (1967)** and **Srikakulam** uprisings, the radical-left movement spread into Telangana's **Karimnagar, Warangal, Adilabad and Khammam** forests. Landmarks:
- The **Jagtial-Sircilla** peasant upsurge (**1978**) against landlords.
- Formation of the **People's War Group (PWG)** in **1980** by **Kondapalli Seetharamaiah**.
- The movement drew on agrarian grievance, forest/tribal rights and caste oppression; the state responded with prolonged counter-insurgency. *(Verify specific dates/leaders.)*

**Identity politics & cultural assertion.** A parallel current revived **Telangana cultural identity** — dialect, literature, folk arts — and kept alive grievances over **jobs, water and development**. Key markers:
- **GO 610 (1985)** — an order to correct violations in local (Telangana) employment (implementation of Mulki/Six-Point safeguards).
- The founding of the **Telugu Desam Party (1982, N.T. Rama Rao)** reshaped state politics.

These undercurrents — agrarian radicalism plus identity assertion — fed into the **statehood movement** that revived after 2001.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **PWG founded 1980** (Kondapalli Seetharamaiah); **Jagtial-Sircilla** movement `1978`; **GO 610 (1985)** addressed local-employment violations; **TDP founded 1982** (NTR).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **TPS merged with Congress (1971)**, so the statehood demand went dormant in the 1970s-90s; it **revived** only around **2001 (TRS)**. Don't conflate the 1969 and post-2001 phases.

💡 **Example:** **GO 610 (1985)** and its **non-implementation** is a recurring PYQ link between the 1970s-80s employment grievances and the later statehood demand.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- TPS → merged with Congress (1971).
- Naxalism: Jagtial-Sircilla 1978; **PWG 1980** (Kondapalli Seetharamaiah).
- **GO 610 (1985)** — local employment safeguards.
- TDP founded 1982 (NTR).

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Who founded the People's War Group in 1980?* → **Kondapalli Seetharamaiah**.

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4 — Indian National Movement: Cabinet Mission to Independence & Partition

### Deep Dive

**Cabinet Mission (1946).** Three British ministers — **Pethick-Lawrence, Stafford Cripps, A.V. Alexander** — came to plan a transfer of power. It **rejected the demand for a separate Pakistan** and instead proposed:
- A **federal Union of India** (Centre with defence, foreign affairs, communications) with provinces free to form **three groups (A, B, C)**.
- A **Constituent Assembly** (indirectly elected) to frame the constitution.
- An **interim government**.

**Interim Government (Sept 1946).** Formed under **Jawaharlal Nehru**; the **Muslim League** first stayed out, having called **"Direct Action Day" on `16 August 1946`**, which triggered the **Great Calcutta Killings**; the League later joined the interim government.

**Mountbatten Plan (`3 June 1947`).** With deadlock and communal violence rising, **Lord Mountbatten** proposed **partition** — the division of British India into **two dominions**.

**Indian Independence Act, 1947.** Passed by the British Parliament (July 1947), it created **two independent dominions, India and Pakistan**, from **`15 August 1947`**, gave each a Constituent Assembly with full legislative power, and lapsed British **paramountcy** over princely states.

**Partition & the Radcliffe Line.** The **Boundary Commission under Sir Cyril Radcliffe** drew the India-Pakistan borders in **Punjab and Bengal**, published just after Independence; the partition unleashed **mass migration and communal violence**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Cabinet Mission (1946)** rejected Pakistan, proposed grouping + Constituent Assembly; **Direct Action Day `16 Aug 1946`**; **Mountbatten Plan `3 June 1947`**; **Indian Independence Act 1947** → two dominions on **`15 Aug 1947`**; borders by the **Radcliffe** commission.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **Cabinet Mission (1946)** is different from the earlier **Cripps Mission (1942)** — though **Cripps was in both**. The **Mountbatten Plan** (partition) came `3 June 1947`, **not** 1946.

💡 **Example:** The chain **Cabinet Mission (1946) → Direct Action Day → Interim Govt → Mountbatten Plan (June 1947) → Independence Act → 15 Aug 1947** is a very common sequence PYQ.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Cabinet Mission 1946: Pethick-Lawrence, Cripps, Alexander; grouping A/B/C.
- Direct Action Day: **16 Aug 1946** (Calcutta killings).
- Mountbatten Plan: **3 June 1947**; Independence Act 1947.
- Radcliffe Line: Punjab & Bengal boundaries.

✅ **Quick self-check:** *When was Direct Action Day?* → **16 August 1946** (led to the Great Calcutta Killings).

## 🗺️ Subject 5 — Telangana Geography: Forests, Wildlife Sanctuaries & Tiger Reserves

### Deep Dive

**Forests.** Telangana's forests are mainly **tropical dry deciduous** (with **teak, terminalia, bamboo**) and **thorn** types, concentrated in the northern and eastern districts (**Adilabad, Kumram Bheem Asifabad, Bhadradri-Kothagudem, Warangal, Nagarkurnool/Nallamala**). Forest cover is roughly **`24%`** of the geographical area (*verify the latest India State of Forest Report figure*).

**Tiger reserves (two):**
- **Amrabad Tiger Reserve** — in the **Nallamala** hills (Nagarkurnool), one of the **largest tiger reserves in India** by area; part of the former Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam reserve.
- **Kawal Tiger Reserve** — in **Adilabad/Jannaram**, a source area in the Godavari catchment.

**Major wildlife sanctuaries:** **Eturnagaram** (oldest, Warangal), **Pakhal, Kinnerasani** (Bhadradri), **Pranahita, Shivaram, Kawal** (northern belt), **Pocharam, Manjira** (Manjira — birds & marsh crocodile), **Kinnerasani**. **National parks (all near Hyderabad):** **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy (KBR), Mahavir Harina Vanasthali, Mrugavani**.

**Conservation notes:** the **Nallamala** forest (Amrabad) is a key tiger and biodiversity zone and a **Chenchu** tribal habitat; **Manjira** is important for **birds and the mugger crocodile**.

![Telangana regions base map — locate the northern/eastern forest belts and the Nallamala (south) tiger zone](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/faizan574/Learning-Python/main/tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_regions.svg)

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Telangana has **two tiger reserves**: **Amrabad (Nallamala)** and **Kawal (Adilabad)**. Three national parks — **KBR, Mahavir Harina Vanasthali, Mrugavani** — are near **Hyderabad**. Forest cover ~`24%` (verify ISFR).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Forest-cover and area figures are revised in each **India State of Forest Report** — write **"verify latest figure."** Remember: **Amrabad is in the south (Nallamala)**, **Kawal in the north (Adilabad)**.

💡 **Example:** **Amrabad Tiger Reserve (Nallamala)** — large area, Chenchu habitat, uranium-exploration controversy — is a frequent geography + environment PYQ.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Forest type: tropical dry deciduous (teak); ~24% cover (verify).
- Tiger reserves: **Amrabad (Nallamala/Nagarkurnool)** & **Kawal (Adilabad)**.
- National parks (Hyderabad): **KBR, Mahavir Harina Vanasthali, Mrugavani**.
- Manjira sanctuary: birds & mugger crocodile.

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Name Telangana's two tiger reserves.* → **Amrabad** and **Kawal**.
