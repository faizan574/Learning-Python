## 📘 Subject 1: Indian Constitution & Polity (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision: The Preamble & Its Keywords

Beginning the **Polity revision pass**, today reconsolidates the **Preamble** (originally Day 3) — the "identity card" of the Constitution.

### 📖 Concept

**Text & source.** The Preamble is based on the **Objectives Resolution** (drafted by **Jawaharlal Nehru**, 1946, adopted 1947). It reads:

> "We, the people of India, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a **SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC** and to secure to all its citizens: **JUSTICE** (social, economic, political); **LIBERTY** (of thought, expression, belief, faith, worship); **EQUALITY** (of status and opportunity); and **FRATERNITY** assuring the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the nation…"

**Keywords:**

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sovereign** | Free, supreme authority, no external control |
| **Socialist** | (added 42nd) democratic socialism, mixed economy |
| **Secular** | (added 42nd) equal respect to all religions; no state religion |
| **Democratic** | Government by the people (universal adult franchise) |
| **Republic** | Elected head of state (no hereditary monarch) |
| **Justice** | Social, economic, political (from Russian Revolution) |
| **Liberty** | Thought, expression… (from French Revolution) |
| **Equality** | Status & opportunity (French Revolution) |
| **Fraternity** | Brotherhood + dignity of individual + unity |

**42nd Amendment (1976)** added **Socialist, Secular, and Integrity** to the Preamble.

**Status:**
- The Preamble is **part of the Constitution** (`Kesavananda Bharati, 1973`; **Berubari case, 1960 had said it was NOT** enforceable/part — later corrected).
- It is **not enforceable** in court (not a source of power/prohibition).
- It **can be amended** (42nd Amdt did) but **not so as to destroy the basic structure**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — The Preamble is based on **Nehru's Objectives Resolution**; **42nd Amendment (1976) added Socialist, Secular, Integrity**. It **is part of the Constitution (Kesavananda 1973)** but is **non-justiciable** (not enforceable) and **amendable** within basic-structure limits.

💡 **Example:** The words **"Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity"** echo the **French Revolution** (Liberty, Equality, Fraternity) and the **Russian Revolution** (Justice) — a favourite source-matching question.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Objectives Resolution → Nehru (1946)**.
- **42nd Amdt added: Socialist, Secular, Integrity**.
- **Liberty-Equality-Fraternity = French Revolution; Justice = Russian Revolution**.
- Preamble = **part of Constitution (Kesavananda)**, **non-justiciable**, amendable.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic** (SSSDR); 42nd added **S-S-I** (Socialist, Secular, Integrity). JLEF: Justice (Russian), Liberty-Equality-Fraternity (French)."

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Socialist, Secular & Integrity** were added by the **42nd Amendment (1976)** — not in the original 1949 Preamble. The Preamble is **part of the Constitution** but **not enforceable** (Berubari's "not part" view was overruled by Kesavananda).

**✅ Quick self-check:** Which three words did the 42nd Amendment add, and is the Preamble enforceable in court?
*(Socialist, Secular, Integrity; no — it is non-justiciable, though it is part of the Constitution.)*

### 🎯 Revision: SIR (Special Intensive Revision) & Electoral-Roll Issues

Continuing the **revision pass**, today consolidates the **electoral-roll revision (SIR)** issue (originally Day 8). *(Verify latest status before the exam.)*

### 📖 Concept

**SIR — Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls.**
- A drive by the **Election Commission of India** to **intensively revise/verify** the voter rolls (door-to-door enumeration, additions, deletions, corrections) — distinct from the routine **Summary Revision**.
- Aims to remove **duplicate/ineligible/dead** entries and add eligible new voters, improving roll accuracy.
- Became a **political controversy** in various states over alleged **wrongful deletions** and process concerns (`verify the Telangana-specific dispute and latest status`).

**Electoral-roll administration (constitutional link):**
- **Election Commission of India (Art 324)** superintends electoral rolls & elections.
- **Electoral roll** = list of eligible voters for a constituency; revised periodically (qualifying date **1 January**).
- Related terms: **ERO (Electoral Registration Officer), BLO (Booth Level Officer), Form 6/7/8** (additions/deletions/corrections), **NVSP** portal.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **SIR (Special Intensive Revision)** is an **ECI** drive to intensively verify and clean the **electoral rolls** (door-to-door), beyond the routine summary revision; it drew controversy over **deletions/process**. The **ECI (Art 324)** superintends rolls (qualifying date **1 January**). **Verify latest official status.**

💡 **Example:** During an SIR, **Booth Level Officers (BLOs)** verify each entry door-to-door; disputes arose over **large-scale deletions** allegedly affecting genuine voters — a governance/current-affairs issue linking the **ECI's role** to on-ground implementation.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **SIR = Special Intensive Revision** (intensive roll verification by ECI).
- **ECI = Art 324**; qualifying date for rolls = **1 January**.
- **BLO/ERO, Forms 6/7/8, NVSP** — roll machinery.
- Treat the specific dispute/status as **"verify latest official figure."**

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**SIR = intensive door-to-door roll clean-up by ECI (Art 324); controversy = deletions.** Routine = summary revision; qualifying date 1 Jan."

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **SIR (intensive)** differs from the routine **summary revision**. It is an **ECI (Art 324)** function. The specific Telangana controversy and its outcome are **current-affairs** — **verify the latest official status**.

**✅ Quick self-check:** What does SIR stand for, and which body conducts it?
*(Special Intensive Revision (of electoral rolls); the Election Commission of India (Art 324).)*

## 🏰 Subject 3: Telangana History
### 🎯 Telangana Handlooms & Handicrafts (GI Products)

Telangana's **GI-tagged handlooms and handicrafts** are among the highest-yield **culture** topics — match each craft to its place.

### 📖 Concept

| Craft / Handloom | Place | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Pochampally Ikat (Ikkat)** | Bhoodan Pochampally (Yadadri) | **Tie-dye silk/cotton sarees**; GI; "Silk City"; UNESCO craft-village |
| **Gadwal sarees** | Gadwal (Jogulamba Gadwal) | Cotton body + silk pallu/zari; GI |
| **Narayanpet sarees** | Narayanpet | Silk/cotton, Maratha influence; GI |

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Pochampally Ikat (tie-dye), Gadwal/Narayanpet/Siddipet-Gollabhama sarees, Cheriyal scrolls, Nirmal toys/paintings, Karimnagar silver filigree, Adilabad Dokra, Warangal durries** — all GI-linked Telangana crafts. Match **craft ↔ place**.

### 🎯 Revision: Climate, Rainfall, Droughts, Soils & Agriculture

*(If the map doesn't display in email, open the lesson on GitHub — the SVG is at `tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_regions.svg`.)*

### 📖 Concept

**Climate.**
- **Semi-arid to sub-humid tropical**; hot summers (up to 45°C+ in the north/Ramagundam), mild winters.
- Three seasons: **summer (Mar-May), monsoon (Jun-Sep), winter (Oct-Feb)**.

**Rainfall.**
- Mostly from the **South-West Monsoon (~80%)** (Jun-Sep); some from the **North-East Monsoon** (Oct-Nov, south/southeast).
- Average annual rainfall ~**900-950 mm** (`verify latest figure`); **Adilabad/north** wetter, **Mahabubnagar/Palamuru south** drier (rain-shadow, drought-prone).
- Recurrent **droughts** in the southern/western districts.

**Soils.**

| Soil | Where | Crops |
|---|---|---|
| **Red soils (chalka)** | most of Telangana (granite) | coarse cereals, groundnut, pulses |
| **Black cotton (regur)** | Adilabad, parts of north (basalt) | cotton, jowar |
| **Alluvial** | river valleys (Godavari/Krishna) | paddy, sugarcane |
| **Laterite** | patches | — |

**Agriculture.**
- Main crops: **rice/paddy (dominant, under Kaleshwaram/SRSP/Nagarjuna Sagar), cotton, maize, red gram (pulses), chillies, turmeric, groundnut, sugarcane**.
- **Kharif (monsoon) & Rabi (winter)** seasons; heavy reliance on **irrigation (lift schemes)** and **groundwater** (borewells).
- Telangana is a leading producer of **rice, cotton, maize, turmeric, chillies** (verify rankings).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Telangana climate = **semi-arid tropical**; ~**80% rainfall from the SW monsoon** (~900-950 mm); **red (chalka) soils dominate**, **black cotton (regur) in the north (Adilabad)**, **alluvial in river valleys**. Key crops: **paddy, cotton, maize, turmeric, chillies**.

💡 **Example:** **Adilabad's black cotton soil** (from Deccan basalt) supports **cotton**, while most of Telangana's **red chalka soil** (granite) suits **coarse cereals & pulses** — soil-crop-geology matching is the exam pattern.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **~80% rainfall = SW monsoon**; south/west (Palamuru) drought-prone.
- **Red (chalka) soil dominant; black cotton (regur) in Adilabad; alluvial in valleys**.
- **Paddy dominant** (irrigation-led); cotton, maize, turmeric, chillies.
- **Kharif & Rabi** seasons.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Semi-arid, 80% SW monsoon; red chalka soil everywhere, black regur in Adilabad, alluvial in valleys; paddy + cotton + maize + turmeric + chillies.**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Most rainfall is from the **South-West monsoon** (not NE). **Red (chalka)** soil dominates Telangana (granite); **black cotton (regur)** is mainly **northern (Adilabad, basalt)**. The **south (Palamuru)** is the driest/most drought-prone.
