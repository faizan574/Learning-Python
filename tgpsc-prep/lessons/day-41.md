## 🏛️ Subject 1 — Polity: Non-Constitutional Bodies (NITI Aayog, NHRC, CIC, Lokpal)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Non-constitutional bodies** are created by **executive resolution or statute**, not directly by the Constitution.

| Body | Basis | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **NITI Aayog** | **Executive resolution (1 Jan 2015)** | Replaced the **Planning Commission (1950)**; a **think-tank**; PM chairs; **no fund-allocation powers**; promotes cooperative federalism |
| **NHRC** | **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993** | Chair (retired CJI/SC judge) + members; **recommendatory** powers |
| **CIC** | **RTI Act, 2005** | Chief Information Commissioner + ICs; oversees the Right to Information |
| **Lokpal** | **Lokpal & Lokayuktas Act, 2013** | Anti-corruption **ombudsman**; first Lokpal (**Justice P.C. Ghose**) appointed **2019** |

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **NITI Aayog** (2015) replaced the **Planning Commission** and, unlike it, has **no power to allocate funds** — it is a **policy think-tank** advancing cooperative federalism. **NHRC (1993), CIC (2005), and Lokpal (2013)** are **statutory** bodies. **CVC** (statutory, 2003, post-Vineet Narain) and **CBI** (Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946) are also non-constitutional.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **NITI Aayog is an executive body** (resolution), **not statutory or constitutional**. **NHRC/CIC/Lokpal are statutory** (created by Acts). These differ from **constitutional** bodies (EC, UPSC, CAG). NHRC's powers are **recommendatory**, not binding.

### 💡 Example
The shift from the fund-allocating **Planning Commission** to the advisory **NITI Aayog** marked a move from **top-down planning** to **cooperative federalism** — a favourite comparison question.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- **NITI Aayog** — executive resolution (2015), replaced Planning Commission, **no fund allocation**.
- **NHRC (1993), CIC (2005), Lokpal (2013)** — statutory.
- **Lokpal** first appointed **2019** (Justice P.C. Ghose).
- Constitutional (EC/UPSC/CAG) ≠ these non-constitutional bodies.

### ✅ Quick Self-Check
*What did NITI Aayog replace, and how does its power differ?*
→ The **Planning Commission**; NITI Aayog is advisory with **no fund-allocation** power.

## 🏯 Subject 3 — Telangana History: Mulki Rules & the 1969 Jai Telangana Agitation

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Mulki rules** were **domicile rules** (dating to a Nizam-era firman, and requiring long local residence — typically **15 years**) that reserved government jobs in Hyderabad State for **locals (Mulkis)**. After the **1956 merger**, the **Gentlemen's Agreement** was supposed to protect Telangana's jobs and revenue.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — The **violation of Mulki rules and the Gentlemen's Agreement** (non-locals getting Telangana jobs, revenue diverted) sparked the **1969 "Jai Telangana" agitation**. Beginning as a **student movement (January 1969)**, it grew under **Marri Chenna Reddy's Telangana Praja Samithi (TPS)** into a demand for a **separate Telangana**; over **300 people died** in police firing.

**Aftermath:** the **Supreme Court upheld the Mulki rules (1972)**, which in turn triggered the reverse **"Jai Andhra" agitation (1972-73)** in the Andhra region. The crisis was settled by the **Six-Point Formula (1973)**, which led to later measures like **Presidential Order / GO 610**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Mulki violated → 1969 Jai Telangana (students → TPS, Marri Chenna Reddy) → SC upholds Mulki 1972 → Jai Andhra → Six-Point Formula 1973**".

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **1969 agitation** was for **separate Telangana** (jobs/Mulki grievance); the **1972 Supreme Court verdict** upholding Mulki rules triggered the **opposite "Jai Andhra"** movement. **Marri Chenna Reddy** led the **TPS**. These 1969-73 events are the direct ancestors of the 2014 statehood.

### 💡 Example
The **Six-Point Formula (1973)** and the later **GO 610** (on local employment) trace directly back to the **Mulki-rules dispute** — showing how a jobs grievance shaped decades of Telangana politics.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- **Mulki rules** = local-domicile job reservation (Nizam-era origin).
- **1969 Jai Telangana** — students → **TPS (Marri Chenna Reddy)**; ~300+ died.
- **SC upheld Mulki 1972** → **Jai Andhra** → **Six-Point Formula 1973**.

### ✅ Quick Self-Check
*Who led the Telangana Praja Samithi during the 1969 agitation?*
→ **Marri Chenna Reddy**.

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4 — Indian National Movement: Gandhi's Arrival & Early Satyagrahas

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Mahatma Gandhi returned to India on `9 January 1915`** (from South Africa) — now marked as **Pravasi Bharatiya Divas**. On the advice of his political guru **Gopal Krishna Gokhale**, he toured India for a year and founded the **Sabarmati (Kochrab) Ashram**. He then tested **satyagraha** in three early struggles:

| Satyagraha | Year | Cause & significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Champaran (Bihar)** | 1917 | Against the **tinkathia** system (forced indigo cultivation) — **first civil disobedience in India**; Rajendra Prasad assisted |
| **Kheda (Gujarat)** | 1918 | Peasants unable to pay revenue after crop failure — **first non-cooperation**; **Sardar Patel** emerged |
| **Ahmedabad Mill Strike** | 1918 | Mill workers' wage demand — Gandhi's **first hunger strike**; won a 35% raise |

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Champaran (1917)** was Gandhi's **first civil-disobedience** experiment in India (against the **tinkathia** indigo system). **Kheda (1918)** was his first **non-cooperation** effort (revenue), where **Vallabhbhai Patel** rose to prominence, and **Ahmedabad (1918)** featured his first **hunger strike**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Return 1915 → Champaran 1917 (indigo, civil disobedience) → Kheda 1918 (tax, Patel) → Ahmedabad 1918 (mill, fast)**". Gokhale = political guru.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Champaran = indigo (tinkathia)**, Bihar, **1917**; **Kheda = revenue**, Gujarat, **1918**; **Ahmedabad = mill workers (hunger strike)**, 1918. These three established Gandhi's method **before** the national movements (Non-Cooperation etc.).

### 💡 Example
At **Champaran**, Gandhi converted a **local indigo grievance** into a moral confrontation the Raj could not ignore — the template (local issue → mass mobilisation → negotiated victory) he reused across the freedom struggle.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- Gandhi returned **9 Jan 1915**; guru **Gokhale**.
- **Champaran 1917** — tinkathia indigo, first civil disobedience.
- **Kheda 1918** — revenue, **Patel** emerged.
- **Ahmedabad 1918** — mill strike, first hunger strike.

### ✅ Quick Self-Check
*Which was Gandhi's first civil-disobedience experiment in India, and against what?*
→ **Champaran (1917)**, against the **tinkathia** (forced indigo) system.

## 🗺️ Subject 5 — Telangana Geography: Telangana at a Glance

![Telangana Regions Map](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/faizan574/Learning-Python/main/tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_regions.svg)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Telangana** is the **29th state of India**, formed on **`2 June 2014`** (State Formation Day) by bifurcating **Andhra Pradesh** under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**.

| Feature | Detail (verify latest official figure) |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Southern India, on the **Deccan Plateau**; **landlocked** |
| **Area** | ≈ `1,12,077 km²` (about the 11th-largest state) |
| **Capital** | **Hyderabad** (a shared capital with AP for up to 10 years, till 2024) |
| **Borders** | **Maharashtra** (N), **Chhattisgarh** (NE), **Andhra Pradesh** (S/E), **Karnataka** (W) |
| **Rivers** | **Godavari** (drains most of the state) and **Krishna** |

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Telangana (formed **2 June 2014**, 29th state) sits on the **Deccan Plateau**, is **landlocked**, and is drained mainly by the **Godavari** (with the Krishna in the south). It borders **four states**: Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh, and Karnataka. *(Verify the current number of districts — it has been reorganised several times.)*

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**29th state, 2 June 2014, Deccan Plateau, Godavari-drained, landlocked, 4 neighbours**". Capital **Hyderabad**; borders **MH-CG-AP-KA**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Telangana is **landlocked** (no coastline — the coast went to AP). It is the **29th** state (2014). The number of **districts has changed** with reorganisations — **verify the current count**. The **Godavari** basin covers the larger share of the state.

### 💡 Example
Telangana being **landlocked** (unlike coastal AP after bifurcation) shapes its economy toward **IT/services and irrigation-based agriculture** rather than ports/fisheries — a useful geography-economy link.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- **29th state**, formed **2 June 2014** (AP Reorganisation Act 2014).
- **Deccan Plateau**, **landlocked**; **Godavari** + Krishna.
- Borders: **Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka**.
- Capital **Hyderabad**; **district count — verify latest**.
