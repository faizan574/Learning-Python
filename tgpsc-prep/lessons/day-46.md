## 🏛️ Polity: Citizenship (Art 5-11, Citizenship Act, CAA)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Citizenship** defines the full membership of the state. The Constitution deals with it in **Part II, Articles `5-11`**, but only identifies who were citizens **at commencement** (`26 Jan 1950`) — it leaves the ongoing law to Parliament.

- **Art 5** — citizenship at commencement (domicile + birth in India / parents born in India / ordinary residence for 5 years).
- **Art 6** — rights of persons who **migrated from Pakistan** to India.
- **Art 7** — rights of persons who **migrated to Pakistan** (and returned).
- **Art 8** — citizenship of **persons of Indian origin residing abroad**.
- **Art 9** — voluntarily acquiring **foreign citizenship** → not a citizen.
- **Art 10** — continuance of citizenship rights.
- **Art 11** — **empowers Parliament** to make any law regarding citizenship (acquisition, termination).

India has **single citizenship** (unlike the USA's dual state+national citizenship) — one national citizenship for the whole country.

Under **Art 11**, Parliament enacted the **Citizenship Act, 1955**, which provides **five modes of acquiring** citizenship: **birth, descent, registration, naturalisation, and incorporation of territory**; and modes of **loss**: **renunciation, termination, deprivation**.

The **Citizenship (Amendment) Act, `2019` (CAA)** provides a fast-tracked path to citizenship for **Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, Parsis and Christians** who entered India **on or before `31 December 2014`** from **Pakistan, Bangladesh and Afghanistan** — reducing the naturalisation residency requirement for them. It notably does **not** include Muslims, which is the basis of legal challenges (*verify the latest status of Rules and litigation before the exam*).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Citizenship = **Part II, Art 5-11**; India has **single citizenship**. **Citizenship Act 1955** → 5 modes of acquisition. **CAA 2019** = fast-track for 6 non-Muslim communities from Pakistan/Bangladesh/Afghanistan who entered by **31 Dec 2014**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The Constitution (Art 5-11) does **not** lay down a permanent law of citizenship — **Art 11** delegates that to Parliament (Act of 1955). "Overseas Citizen of India (OCI)" is **not** dual citizenship.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Which article empowers Parliament to legislate on citizenship?* → **Article 11** (leading to the Citizenship Act, 1955).

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- Part II, **Art 5-11**; single citizenship.
- 5 acquisition modes: **birth, descent, registration, naturalisation, incorporation**.
- CAA 2019: cut-off **31 Dec 2014**; 3 countries; 6 communities.
- Art 9: foreign citizenship → automatic loss.

## 🎁 Telangana Schemes: Gruha Jyothi & Indiramma Indlu

### 📖 Deep Dive

Two of the Six Guarantees address **household energy** and **housing**:

1. **Gruha Jyothi** — provides **free electricity up to `200 units` per month** to eligible domestic households in Telangana. Launched in **2024** (*verify exact launch date and eligibility conditions before the exam*). Households consuming within 200 units/month pay nothing; the state reimburses the DISCOMs for the consumed units. It targets reducing the household power bill burden for low/middle-income families.

2. **Indiramma Indlu (Indiramma Housing)** — a housing scheme providing financial assistance to eligible poor families to **build a house**, with a per-unit assistance around **`₹5 lakh`** (*verify latest sanctioned amount and beneficiary criteria*). It revives the "Indiramma" housing brand, targeting the houseless poor and those living in dilapidated homes; each eligible constituency receives a target number of houses.

Together, **Gruha Jyothi** (energy) and **Indiramma Indlu** (shelter) complement the other guarantees (Mahalakshmi–women, Rythu Bharosa–farmers, Cheyutha–pensions, Yuva Vikasam–youth). Contrast with central schemes: **PM Awas Yojana** (central housing) may overlap for some beneficiaries.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Gruha Jyothi** = free electricity up to **`200 units`/month** (state reimburses DISCOMs). **Indiramma Indlu** = housing assistance (~`₹5 lakh`/unit, **verify**) for houseless/poor families.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Gruha Jyothi's free-power cap is **200 units/month** — consumption **above** 200 units in a month typically makes the **whole bill** payable (slab condition varies; verify). Don't confuse Indiramma Indlu with the central PM Awas Yojana.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Up to how many units per month does Gruha Jyothi provide free electricity?* → **200 units per month** to eligible households.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers

- Indiramma Indlu: housing, ~**`₹5 lakh`** per unit (verify).
- Both under the **Six Guarantees** (post-Dec 2023).
- Central overlaps: PM Awas Yojana (housing).

## 🏰 Telangana History: Folk & Performing Arts

### 📖 Deep Dive

Telangana's performing arts are rooted in temple, tribal and storytelling traditions:

- **Perini Shivatandavam (Perini Thandavam)** — a vigorous **warrior dance** dedicated to **Lord Shiva**, revived from the **Kakatiya** era (associated with **Nataraja Ramakrishna**, who revived it). Once performed by warriors before going to battle; sculptures at **Ramappa temple** depict its poses.
- **Oggu Katha** — a ballad/storytelling folk form of the **Yadava/Kuruma** communities, narrating tales of **Mallanna, Beerappa, and Yellamma**; **Gaddar** and Chukka Sattaiah are noted names.
- **Burra Katha** — a narrative art using the **tambura** and a pot-drum (*burra/gummeti*), with a lead performer and two co-performers; used for social messaging and freedom-movement propaganda; **Sheikh Nazar** modernised it.
- **Chindu Bhagavatam & Yakshaganam** — theatrical dance-drama forms.
- **Gussadi** — a **Gond tribal dance** of Adilabad (during Dandari festival), performers wearing peacock-feather headgear (**Kanaka Raju** — Padma Shri).
- **Dappu, Kolatam, Golla Suddulu, Bathukamma songs** — community folk-dance/percussion forms.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Perini Shivatandavam** = Kakatiya-era warrior dance to Shiva (revived by **Nataraja Ramakrishna**, Ramappa sculptures). **Oggu Katha** (Yadava/Kuruma ballads), **Burra Katha** (tambura + burra drum), **Gussadi** (Gond, Adilabad).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Perini Shivatandavam is a **male warrior** dance linked to **Kakatiyas/Ramappa**, not a Bathukamma-style women's festival dance. Match the art to its community carefully.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Which dance form, revived by Nataraja Ramakrishna, is depicted in Ramappa temple sculptures?* → **Perini Shivatandavam** (the Kakatiya-era warrior dance).

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- **Perini** — Shiva, Kakatiya, Ramappa; revived by Nataraja Ramakrishna.
- **Oggu Katha** — Yadava/Kuruma; Mallanna/Beerappa tales.
- **Burra Katha** — tambura + burra drum; social/political messaging.
- **Gussadi** — Gond, Adilabad; Kanaka Raju (Padma Shri).

## 🇮🇳 Indian National Movement: Revolutionary Movement Phase II

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **second phase of revolutionary nationalism** (1920s-30s) revived after the withdrawal of Non-Cooperation.

**Hindustan Republican Association (HRA), 1924** — founded (Kanpur) by **Sachindra Nath Sanyal, Ram Prasad Bismil, Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee**. Its landmark action was the **Kakori Conspiracy / train robbery (`9 August 1925`)** — looting a train carrying government money at Kakori. Leaders **Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqulla Khan, Roshan Singh, Rajendra Lahiri** were hanged (1927).

**Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA), 1928** — the HRA was reorganised at **Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi**, adding "Socialist"; **Chandrashekhar Azad** was a key leader, with **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru, Bhagwati Charan Vohra**.
- **Saunders killing (`Dec 1928`)** — Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Azad killed **J. P. Saunders** (mistaken for Scott) to avenge **Lala Lajpat Rai**'s death.
- **Central Assembly bomb (`8 April 1929`)** — **Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt** threw bombs (harmless) in the Central Legislative Assembly and courted arrest, shouting **"Inquilab Zindabad"**, to "make the deaf hear."
- **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru** were **hanged on `23 March 1931`** (Lahore Conspiracy Case).
- **Chandrashekhar Azad** died at **Alfred Park, Allahabad (`27 Feb 1931`)**, shooting himself with his last bullet.

**Bengal — Chittagong Armoury Raid (`18 April 1930`)** led by **Surya Sen (Masterda)**, with revolutionaries including **Pritilata Waddedar** and **Kalpana Dutta**; Surya Sen was hanged in 1934.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **HRA 1924** → Kakori (`1925`); **HSRA 1928** (Azad, Bhagat Singh). **Assembly bomb `8 Apr 1929`** (Bhagat Singh & B. K. Dutt, "Inquilab Zindabad"); **hanged `23 Mar 1931`**. **Surya Sen → Chittagong Armoury Raid `1930`**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Bhagat Singh, Rajguru & Sukhdev were hanged on **23 March 1931** (Shaheed Diwas), not the same as Azad's death (27 Feb 1931). HRA (1924) became **HSRA** (1928) with the addition of "Socialist".

### 💡 Example

*Q: Who led the Chittagong Armoury Raid (1930)?* → **Surya Sen (Masterda)**, with Pritilata Waddedar and others.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- **Kakori** train action: 9 Aug 1925 (HRA).
- HSRA reorganised 1928 (Delhi); Azad, Bhagat Singh.
- Assembly bomb 8 Apr 1929; Bhagat Singh & B. K. Dutt.
- **Shaheed Diwas 23 Mar 1931** (Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru).
- Chittagong Armoury Raid 1930 — Surya Sen.

## 🗺️ Telangana Geography: Irrigation Projects (Major, Medium & Lift Schemes)

### 📖 Deep Dive

Telangana is largely **upland/plateau**, so it depends heavily on **dams and lift-irrigation** to raise river water. Projects are classed by **ayacut (command area)**: **major** (>10,000 ha), **medium** (2,000-10,000 ha), and **minor** (<2,000 ha, incl. tanks).

**Godavari-basin projects:**
- **Kaleshwaram Lift Irrigation Project (KLIS)** — on the Godavari (Medigadda/Lakshmi barrage), one of the world's largest multi-stage lift schemes; lifts water across barrages (Medigadda → Annaram → Sundilla) and reservoirs (**Mid Manair, Mallanna Sagar, Kondapochamma Sagar**). *Verify latest operational status of the Medigadda barrage before the exam.*
- **Sri Ram Sagar Project (SRSP)** — at **Pochampad (Nizamabad)** on the Godavari; a major multipurpose project.
- **Nizam Sagar** — on the **Manjira** (Godavari tributary), Kamareddy.
- **Kadem, Sripada Yellampally, Devadula LIS, Kanti Velugu**.

**Krishna-basin projects:**
- **Nagarjuna Sagar** — on the **Krishna** (shared, at Nalgonda border); masonry dam.
- **Jurala (Priyadarshini Jurala)** — the **first major project** on the Krishna as it enters Telangana (Mahabubnagar).
- **Palamuru-Rangareddy LIS** — a large lift scheme to serve the drought-prone southern districts.
- **Srisailam** (across the Krishna, hydel + supply), **Kalwakurthy, Nettempadu, Bhima** lift schemes.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Kaleshwaram (KLIS)** = mega multi-stage **Godavari** lift scheme (Medigadda, Mallanna Sagar). **SRSP** at Pochampad. **Nagarjuna Sagar** & **Jurala** on the **Krishna** (Jurala = first project on the Krishna in Telangana). **Palamuru-Rangareddy LIS** for the drought-prone south.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Kaleshwaram is on the Godavari**, not the Krishna. **Jurala** is the first Krishna project in the state; **Nizam Sagar** is on the **Manjira** (a Godavari tributary), not the main Godavari.

### 💡 Example

*Q: On which river is the Kaleshwaram project, and name a key reservoir.* → On the **Godavari**; reservoirs include **Mallanna Sagar / Kondapochamma Sagar / Mid Manair**.
