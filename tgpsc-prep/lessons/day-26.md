## 📘 Subject 1: Indian Constitution & Polity (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision: Union & Its Territory (Art 1-4)

Revisiting **Union and its Territory** (Day 1) — how India is constituted and how states are reorganised.

### 📖 Concept

- **Art 1:** India, that is Bharat, shall be a **"Union of States"** (not a federation by agreement — states can't secede; "indestructible union of destructible states").
- **Art 2:** Parliament may **admit or establish new states** (into the Union).
- **Art 3:** Parliament may **form new states / alter areas, boundaries or names** of existing states — by a **simple majority**; the bill needs the **President's recommendation** and must be **referred to the state legislature** for its views (not binding).
- **Art 4:** laws under Art 2 & 3 are **not** deemed constitutional amendments under Art 368.

**Key facts:**
- Telangana was created (2014) via the **AP Reorganisation Act** under **Art 3** (simple majority).
- The phrase **"Union of States"** (Ambedkar) stresses that the Union is **not** the result of an agreement and states have **no right to secede**.
- **Territory of India** (Art 1) = states + UTs + territories that may be acquired.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Art 1: "Union of States"**; **Art 2: admit new states; Art 3: form/alter states (simple majority, President's recommendation, state's views non-binding); Art 4: not a 368 amendment.** Telangana formed under **Art 3**.

💡 **Example:** Telangana's creation (2014) needed only a **simple majority** under **Art 3** (with the President's recommendation) — the states' views are sought but **not binding**, which is why AP's objection didn't block it.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Art 1 = Union of States**; **Art 3 = reorganisation (simple majority)**.
- State legislature's view under Art 3 is **not binding**.
- **Art 4** — such laws are **not** Art 368 amendments.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**1 Union, 2 Admit, 3 Alter (simple majority, non-binding state view), 4 Not-an-amendment.** No right to secede."

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Reorganisation (Art 3) is by **simple majority**, needs **President's recommendation**, and the state's opinion is **non-binding**. States **cannot secede** ("indestructible Union"). Art 2 (admit external) ≠ Art 3 (reorganise internal).

**✅ Quick self-check:** Under which article was Telangana formed, and is the state legislature's view binding?
*(Article 3 (simple majority); no — the state legislature's view is not binding.)*

## 🏰 Subject 3: Telangana History (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision: The Satavahanas

Revisiting the **Satavahanas** (Day 1) — Telangana's first great imperial dynasty.

### 📖 Concept

- **Founder:** **Simuka** (c. 1st century BCE); the Satavahanas (Andhras) ruled the Deccan for ~**450 years**.
- **Capitals:** **Kotilingala (early, Telangana), Dhanyakataka/Amaravati, Pratishthana (Paithan)**.
- **Greatest rulers:** **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (defeated the Shakas/Nahapana; "restorer of Satavahana glory"; **Nasik inscription** by his mother **Gautami Balasri**); **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi**; **Yajna Sri Satakarni** (last great ruler).
- **Administration:** matronymic names (mother's name — e.g., Gautamiputra); feudatory system (rajas, maharathis).
- **Economy:** trade (Roman trade, ports), coins (lead, potin, silver); **first to grant land to Brahmins/officials**.
- **Religion:** Brahmanical (Vedic sacrifices) + patronage of **Buddhism** (Amaravati stupa, Nagarjunakonda nearby later).
- **Language:** **Prakrit** (Brahmi script); **Hala's "Gathasaptasati"** (Prakrit poetry).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Satavahanas: founder **Simuka**; greatest ruler **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (defeated Shakas, Nasik inscription by **Gautami Balasri**); early capital **Kotilingala (Telangana)**; **matronymic** names; **Prakrit**; **Hala's Gathasaptasati**; Amaravati Buddhist art.

💡 **Example:** **Gautamiputra Satakarni's** achievements are recorded in the **Nasik prashasti** by his mother **Gautami Balasri** — a signature exam fact (matronymic naming + Shaka victory).

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Simuka = founder**; **Gautamiputra Satakarni = greatest** (defeated Shaka Nahapana).
- **Kotilingala = early capital (Telangana)**; **Amaravati** = later.
- **Matronymic names; Prakrit; Hala's Gathasaptasati**.
- **First land grants** to Brahmins.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Simuka founds, Gautamiputra Satakarni peaks (beats Shakas, Nasik/Gautami Balasri). Kotilingala capital, Prakrit, Gathasaptasati, Amaravati stupa.**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Founder = Simuka** (not Gautamiputra). **Gautamiputra Satakarni** defeated the **Shakas (Nahapana)**. **Matronymic** = named after the mother. Early capital **Kotilingala** is in **Telangana (Karimnagar/Jagtial belt)**.

**✅ Quick self-check:** Who founded the Satavahanas and who was the greatest ruler?
*(Simuka founded them; Gautamiputra Satakarni was the greatest.)*

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4: Indian National Movement (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision: The Non-Cooperation Movement & Swarajists

Revisiting the **Non-Cooperation Movement (1920-22)** (Day 9).

### 📖 Concept

**Non-Cooperation Movement (NCM, 1920-22).**
- Launched by **Gandhi** (Congress, Nagpur Session 1920), combining with the **Khilafat** cause — Gandhi promised **"Swaraj in one year."**
- **Methods:** boycott of **schools, colleges, courts, foreign goods, titles, elections**; promotion of **swadeshi, khadi, national schools, panchayats**.
- **Mass participation** — students, women, peasants; huge success (first true mass movement).
- **Chauri Chaura incident (5 Feb 1922):** a mob burned a police station (22 policemen killed) → Gandhi **withdrew the movement (Feb 1922)** citing violence — controversial (many leaders disappointed).

**Swarajists (1923).**
- After NCM's withdrawal, **C. R. Das & Motilal Nehru** formed the **Swaraj Party (1923)** to **enter legislative councils** and obstruct from within ("council entry") — vs the **"No-changers"** (Gandhians who wanted constructive village work).
- Swarajists contested the **1923 elections**, did well, and used the councils to expose the government.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **NCM (1920-22)** = Gandhi + Khilafat, "**Swaraj in a year**", boycott of schools/courts/foreign goods; **withdrawn after Chauri Chaura (Feb 1922)**. **Swaraj Party (1923) = C. R. Das & Motilal Nehru** (council entry vs No-changers).

💡 **Example:** The **Chauri Chaura** violence (Feb 1922) led Gandhi to **call off the NCM** — a turning point that split the Congress into **Swarajists (council entry)** and **No-changers (constructive work)**.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **NCM 1920-22; Khilafat link; "Swaraj in a year"; boycott**.
- **Chauri Chaura (5 Feb 1922) → withdrawal**.
- **Swaraj Party 1923 = C. R. Das + Motilal Nehru (council entry)**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**NCM (1920, Gandhi+Khilafat, boycott) → Chauri Chaura (Feb 1922) → withdrawn → Swaraj Party (1923, Das & Motilal, council entry).**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — NCM was **withdrawn after Chauri Chaura (Feb 1922)** due to violence. The **Swaraj Party (1923)** wanted **council entry** (Das & Motilal Nehru), opposed by the **No-changers**. NCM combined with **Khilafat**.

**✅ Quick self-check:** What incident led to NCM's withdrawal, and who founded the Swaraj Party?
*(The Chauri Chaura incident, Feb 1922; C. R. Das and Motilal Nehru (1923).)*

## 🗺️ Subject 5: Telangana Geography (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision: The Godavari River System

Revisiting the **Godavari system** (Day 2) — Telangana's largest river system.

**🗺️ Reference map — Telangana river systems:**

![Telangana rivers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/faizan574/Learning-Python/main/tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_rivers.svg)

*(If the map doesn't display in email, open the lesson on GitHub — the SVG is at `tgpsc-prep/maps/telangana_rivers.svg`.)*

### 📖 Concept

- **Godavari** — India's **second-longest river** ("Dakshina Ganga"); rises at **Trimbak (Nashik, Maharashtra)**; enters Telangana in the north; largest share of Telangana's basin.
- **Major tributaries in Telangana:**

| Tributary | Note |
|---|---|
| **Pranhita** | Largest Godavari tributary in TG (Wardha + Wainganga); NE border |
| **Manjira** | Right-bank; Nizam Sagar, Singur dams |
| **Kinnerasani** | Khammam; Kinnerasani reservoir |
| **Kadem** | Adilabad/Nirmal; Kadem project |
| **Taliperu, Peddavagu, Murredu** | minor |
| **Sabari (Kolab)** | SE, joins near Bhadrachalam (border) |

- **Key projects:** **Sri Ram Sagar (Pochampad), Kaleshwaram, Nizam Sagar (Manjira), Singur, Sripada Yellampalli, Devadula, Kadem.**
- **Bhadrachalam** is on the Godavari; the river flows to the **Bay of Bengal** (via AP).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Godavari** (2nd-longest, "Dakshina Ganga"; rises at **Trimbak, Nashik**) is Telangana's largest system; tributaries **Pranhita (largest in TG), Manjira, Kinnerasani, Kadem, Sabari**; projects **SRSP, Kaleshwaram, Nizam Sagar, Singur**.

💡 **Example:** **Pranhita** (Wardha + Wainganga) is the **largest Godavari tributary in Telangana**, feeding the **Kaleshwaram** intake near Medigadda — linking the river system to the mega lift scheme.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Godavari rises at Trimbak (Nashik)**; "Dakshina Ganga"; 2nd-longest.
- **Pranhita = largest TG tributary** (Wardha+Wainganga); **Manjira = Nizam Sagar/Singur**.
- **Projects: SRSP/Pochampad, Kaleshwaram, Nizam Sagar**.
- **Bhadrachalam on the Godavari**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Godavari (Dakshina Ganga, Trimbak) — tributaries Pranhita (biggest), Manjira, Kinnerasani, Kadem, Sabari — projects SRSP, Kaleshwaram, Nizam Sagar.**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Godavari rises at Trimbak (Nashik, Maharashtra)**, not in Telangana. **Pranhita** (largest TG tributary) = **Wardha + Wainganga**. **Manjira** carries **Nizam Sagar & Singur** (not the main Godavari).

**✅ Quick self-check:** Where does the Godavari rise, and what is its largest tributary in Telangana?
*(Trimbak, Nashik (Maharashtra); the Pranhita (Wardha + Wainganga).)*
