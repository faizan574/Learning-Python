## 📘 Subject 1: Indian Constitution & Polity (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision: The President & Parliament (Quick Recall)

Continuing the **Polity revision**, a rapid recall of the **President and Parliament** (Days 11-13).

### 📖 Concept

**President (`Art 52-62`):**
```
Elected indirectly (electoral college: elected MPs + elected MLAs + Delhi/Puducherry MLAs)
Method: proportional representation, single transferable vote
Term 5 yrs; 35+; impeached (Art 61) for violation of Constitution
Ordinance Art 123; pardon Art 72; aid & advice Art 74 (binding)
Nominated members do NOT vote for President
```

**Vice-President (`Art 63-71`):** elected by both Houses (incl. nominated); ex-officio RS Chairman; acts as President max 6 months.

**Parliament (`Art 79`) = President + Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha:**
```
Lok Sabha: max 550, 5-yr term; Rajya Sabha: max 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated), permanent
Money Bill (Art 110): Lok Sabha only; RS delays max 14 days; no joint sitting
Joint sitting (Art 108): ordinary bills only; presided by LS Speaker
Budget (Art 112); PAC chaired by Opposition; Estimates Committee = LS only
Sessions gap ≤ 6 months (Art 85); Quorum = 1/10
```

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — President: **indirect election, nominated members don't vote, impeachment (Art 61)**. **Money Bill = Lok Sabha only (Art 110), no joint sitting**; **joint sitting (Art 108) for ordinary bills**; **PAC chaired by Opposition**.

💡 **Example:** A **Money Bill** cannot start in the Rajya Sabha and cannot go to a joint sitting — the Lok Sabha's financial supremacy, a certain exam question.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **President: nominated don't vote; VP: nominated do vote**.
- **Money Bill LS-only, 14-day RS delay, no joint sitting**.
- **Joint sitting = ordinary bills (Art 108)**; **PAC = Opposition chair**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**President indirect (no nominated); Money Bill LS-only (no joint sitting); joint sitting = ordinary bills; PAC = Opposition.**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **No joint sitting for Money Bills or Amendment Bills** (only ordinary bills). **Nominated members vote for VP but not President.** **PAC is chaired by the Opposition.**

**✅ Quick self-check:** Can a Money Bill go to a joint sitting, and who chairs the PAC?
*(No — no joint sitting for Money Bills; the PAC is chaired by a member of the Opposition.)*

## 🏰 Subject 3: Telangana History
### 🎯 Telangana Cuisine, Attire, Tribes & State Symbols *(Final syllabus topic!)*

The last new topic — Telangana's **living heritage**: food, dress, tribes, and official **state symbols**.

### 📖 Concept

**State symbols (high-yield — memorise the list):**

| Symbol | Telangana's |
|---|---|
| **State festival** | Bathukamma |
| **State bird** | **Palapitta** (Indian Roller / Blue Jay) |
| **State animal** | **Jinka** (Blackbuck / Deer) |
| **State tree** | **Jammi Chettu** (Prosopis cineraria / Shami) |
| **State flower** | **Tangedu** (Tanner's Cassia / Senna auriculata) |
| **State song** | "**Jaya Jaya He Telangana**" (Ande Sri) — `verify official status` |
| **State emblem** | Kakatiya Kala Thoranam + Charminar |
| **Formation Day** | 2 June (Telangana Formation Day / "Telangana Avirbhava Dinotsavam") |

**Cuisine.** Distinct from coastal Andhra — **spicier, millet/jowar-based** in rural areas:
- **Hyderabadi cuisine:** **biryani, haleem** (Ramzan), nihari, qubani ka meetha, double ka meetha — Nizami/Mughlai heritage.
- **Telangana rural:** **jonna rotte (jowar roti), sajja (bajra), sarva pindi, sakinalu, garijelu, bongulo chicken**; tamarind & red-chilli heavy; **Bathukamma/Bonalu** festival foods.

**Attire.** Traditional handloom sarees (**Pochampally, Gadwal, Narayanpet, Siddipet**); men's **dhoti/pancha**; tribal attire varies by community.

**Tribes (Adivasis).**
- Major tribes: **Gond, Koya, Lambada/Banjara, Chenchu, Kolam, Naikpod, Konda Reddi, Pardhan, Thoti, Andh**.
- **Chenchu** — hunter-gatherers of **Nallamala** (PVTG — Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group).
- **Gonds** (Adilabad) — Gussadi dance, Komaram Bheem; **Kolams** (PVTG).
- **Lambadas/Banjaras** — largest ST group (tandas), colourful attire.
- **Koyas** — Godavari belt, Medaram Jatara.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — State symbols: **bird = Palapitta (Indian Roller), animal = Jinka (Blackbuck), tree = Jammi, flower = Tangedu, festival = Bathukamma, Formation Day = 2 June.** Cuisine: **Hyderabadi biryani/haleem + rural jonna rotte/sarva pindi.** Major tribes: **Gond, Koya, Lambada, Chenchu (PVTG), Kolam (PVTG)**.

💡 **Example:** "Telangana's state bird" → **Palapitta (Indian Roller)**; "state flower" → **Tangedu** (used in Bathukamma). These symbol questions are near-certain one-markers.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Bird = Palapitta; Animal = Jinka; Tree = Jammi; Flower = Tangedu; Festival = Bathukamma**.
- **Formation Day = 2 June 2014**.
- **PVTGs: Chenchu & Kolam**; **Lambada = largest ST**.
- **Hyderabadi biryani/haleem**; rural **jonna rotte, sarva pindi, sakinalu**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Bird Palapitta, Animal Jinka, Tree Jammi, Flower Tangedu, Festival Bathukamma, Day 2 June. Tribes: Gond, Koya, Lambada, Chenchu (PVTG).**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Palapitta = Indian Roller** (state bird); **Tangedu = state flower** (not Bathukamma flower generically). **Chenchu & Kolam are PVTGs**; **Lambada/Banjara is the largest ST** in Telangana. Verify the official state-song status.

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4: Indian National Movement (✓✓ Revision)
### 🎯 Revision: Rowlatt Act, Jallianwala Bagh & Khilafat

Continuing the **INM revision**, a recall of **1919-1920** (Day 8).

### 📖 Concept

**Rowlatt Act (1919).** "**Black Act**" — allowed detention **without trial** ("no vakil, no appeal, no daleel"); Gandhi launched the **Rowlatt Satyagraha** (nationwide hartal, April 1919).

**Jallianwala Bagh Massacre (13 April 1919).** At Amritsar, **General Dyer** ordered firing on an unarmed crowd (Baisakhi gathering) — hundreds killed. **Rabindranath Tagore renounced his knighthood**; the **Hunter Commission** inquired; **Udham Singh** later (1940) killed **Michael O'Dwyer** in London.

**Khilafat Movement (1919-24).** Indian Muslims protested the dismemberment of the **Ottoman Caliphate** after WWI; led by the **Ali Brothers (Mohammad & Shaukat Ali), Maulana Azad**. Gandhi linked **Khilafat + Non-Cooperation** for **Hindu-Muslim unity** — launching the **Non-Cooperation Movement (1920)**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Rowlatt Act (1919) = detention without trial ("Black Act")**; **Jallianwala Bagh = 13 April 1919 (Dyer, Amritsar); Tagore renounced knighthood**; **Khilafat (1919-24, Ali Brothers)** merged with **Non-Cooperation (1920)** for Hindu-Muslim unity.

💡 **Example:** The **Rowlatt Act → Jallianwala Bagh → Khilafat** sequence turned moderate discontent into the **mass Non-Cooperation Movement (1920)** — Gandhi's first nationwide agitation.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Rowlatt 1919 = no-trial detention ("Black Act")**.
- **Jallianwala Bagh = 13 April 1919, Dyer, Amritsar; Tagore knighthood**.
- **Khilafat = Ali Brothers**, linked to **Non-Cooperation (1920)**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Rowlatt (Black Act, no trial) → Jallianwala Bagh (13 Apr 1919, Dyer) → Khilafat (Ali Brothers) + Non-Cooperation (1920).**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Jallianwala Bagh = 13 April 1919** (General **Dyer** fired; **O'Dwyer** was the Punjab Lt-Governor, killed later by Udham Singh). **Tagore renounced his knighthood** (not the Nobel). Khilafat is about the **Ottoman Caliphate**.

**✅ Quick self-check:** What did the Rowlatt Act allow, and what date was the Jallianwala Bagh massacre?
*(Detention without trial ("Black Act"); 13 April 1919.)*

### 📖 Concept

**Minerals:**

| Mineral | Where |
|---|---|
| **Coal** | **Singareni** (Godavari valley): Kothagudem, Ramagundam, Mancherial, Bellampalli, Yellandu, Manuguru |
| **Limestone** | Nalgonda, Adilabad, Karimnagar (cement industry) |
| **Granite** | Karimnagar, Warangal, Khammam (export "granite city") |
| **Bauxite, iron ore, dolomite, quartz, feldspar, barytes** | scattered |
| **Uranium** | Nallamala (Nagarkurnool) — proposed, contested |

- **Singareni Collieries Company Ltd (SCCL)** — the only major coal producer in South India; along the **Pranhita-Godavari (Gondwana) belt**.

**Energy / Power:**
- **Thermal:** dominant — **Ramagundam (NTPC + KTPS), Kothagudem (KTPS), Bhadradri, Yadadri (new), Kakatiya (Bhupalpally)**.
- **Hydro:** **Srisailam (LB), Nagarjuna Sagar, Jurala, Pochampad, Nizam Sagar, Lower Jurala, Pulichintala**.
- **Solar:** growing (Telangana among leaders in solar capacity — verify).
- Generation utilities: **TS GENCO / TG GENCO, TS TRANSCO, TS DISCOMs**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Coal = Singareni (Godavari/Gondwana belt: Kothagudem, Ramagundam, Mancherial)**; **limestone → cement (Nalgonda)**; **granite (Karimnagar)**. Power: **thermal-dominant (Ramagundam, Kothagudem, Yadadri)**, plus **hydro (Srisailam, Nagarjuna Sagar)** and growing **solar**.

💡 **Example:** **Ramagundam** = **Singareni coal + NTPC/KTPS thermal + RFCL fertiliser** — the classic mineral-energy cluster question (coal feeds the thermal plant).

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- **Singareni (SCCL) = coal**, Gondwana/Godavari belt.
- **Limestone → cement (Nalgonda)**; **granite (Karimnagar)**.
- **Thermal dominant** (Ramagundam/Kothagudem); **hydro (Srisailam/Nagarjuna Sagar)**.
- **Uranium = Nallamala (contested)**.

> 🧠 **MEMORY HOOK** — "**Singareni coal (Godavari belt) → thermal power (Ramagundam/Kothagudem); limestone→cement (Nalgonda); granite (Karimnagar); uranium (Nallamala, contested).**"

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Coal = Singareni**, in the **Godavari (Gondwana) valley**, not the Krishna basin. **Ramagundam** combines **coal + thermal + fertiliser**. Power is **thermal-dominant** (coal-based). Solar capacity figures are current-affairs — verify.
