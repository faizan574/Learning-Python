## 🏛️ Polity: Supreme Court & High Courts (Judicial Review, PIL, Collegium)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Supreme Court (Art 124-147).** The apex court and guardian of the Constitution.
- **Composition:** Chief Justice + judges (**sanctioned strength currently 34** incl. CJI — verify). Judges appointed by the President; retire at **65**.
- **Appointment:** via the **Collegium** system (CJI + senior-most judges) — evolved through the **Three Judges Cases** (1981, 1993, 1998); the **NJAC (99th Amendment, 2014)** was struck down (2015) as violating judicial independence (basic structure).
- **Jurisdiction:** **Original (Art 131)** (Centre-State/inter-state disputes), **Writ (Art 32)**, **Appellate (Art 132/133/134 — constitutional, civil, criminal)**, **Advisory (Art 143)**, and it is a **Court of Record (Art 129)** with contempt powers. **Judicial review** — power to declare laws unconstitutional.

**High Courts (Art 214-231).** One for each state (some common to states/UTs).
- Judges appointed by the President (collegium), retire at **62**.
- **Writ jurisdiction (Art 226)** — **wider** than the SC's (for FRs **and** other legal rights). **Superintendence (Art 227)** over subordinate courts.

**Key concepts:**
- **Judicial Review** — implied power (Art 13, 32, 226) to test laws against the Constitution; part of the **basic structure**.
- **PIL (Public Interest Litigation)** — relaxed **locus standi** (any public-spirited person can approach the court for the disadvantaged); developed from the late 1970s-80s (Justices Bhagwati, Krishna Iyer).
- **Judicial activism vs restraint**; **Judicial independence** (security of tenure, fixed salary, removal only by **impeachment** — "proved misbehaviour or incapacity," special majority).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — SC: Art 124-147; **collegium** appointments (NJAC struck down 2015); jurisdictions — original/writ/appellate/advisory; **Court of Record**. HC: Art 214; **Art 226 writ (wider than SC)**, Art 227 superintendence. Judges retire **SC 65 / HC 62**. **PIL** = relaxed locus standi.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **HC writ (Art 226) is wider** than SC writ (Art 32) — it covers FRs **and** ordinary legal rights. Judges are removed by **"impeachment"** for **proved misbehaviour/incapacity** (not "violation of Constitution" — that's the President). Retirement: **SC 65, HC 62**.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Whose writ jurisdiction is wider — Supreme Court or High Court?* → The **High Court's (Art 226)** — it covers legal rights beyond Fundamental Rights.

## 🎁 Telangana Schemes: Central Schemes Active in Telangana

### 📖 Deep Dive

Several **centrally-sponsored/central-sector schemes** operate in Telangana alongside state schemes — a favourite comparison area.

**Major central schemes:**
- **PM-KISAN** — ₹6,000/year to farmer families (central); stacks with state **Rythu Bharosa** (per-acre).
- **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)** — health cover up to ₹5 lakh/family; overlaps with state **Rajiv Aarogyasri**.
- **PM Awas Yojana (Urban & Gramin)** — housing; overlaps with **Indiramma Indlu**.
- **MGNREGA** — 100 days rural wage employment (central).
- **PM Ujjwala Yojana** — LPG connections; **Jal Jeevan Mission** (tap water); **Swachh Bharat**.
- **NSAP** (National Social Assistance — old-age/widow/disability pensions); overlaps with **Cheyutha/Aasara**.
- **PMFBY** (crop insurance), **PMKVY/Skill India** (skilling), **PM Poshan** (mid-day meal), **Samagra Shiksha**.

**State vs central — key comparisons (verify latest figures):**

| Domain | Central | Telangana state |
|---|---|---|
| Farmer income | PM-KISAN (₹6,000/yr) | Rythu Bharosa (per acre) |
| Health | Ayushman Bharat (₹5 L) | Rajiv Aarogyasri |
| Housing | PM Awas Yojana | Indiramma Indlu |
| Pensions | NSAP | Cheyutha/Aasara |
| Power | (Saubhagya, past) | Gruha Jyothi (200 units) |

Telangana sometimes **opts out or runs parallel** state versions (e.g. its own health/pension amounts often exceed central), and there are **Centre-State funding-share** debates (60:40 for most CSS).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Central schemes in Telangana: **PM-KISAN** (₹6,000), **Ayushman Bharat** (₹5 L), **PM Awas**, **MGNREGA**, **Ujjwala**, **Jal Jeevan**, **NSAP**. They **overlap** with state guarantees (Rythu Bharosa, Aarogyasri, Indiramma Indlu, Cheyutha). CSS funding often **60:40**.

## 🏰 Telangana History: Kakatiyas — Administration, Economy, Society & Architecture

### 📖 Deep Dive

Beyond politics, the Kakatiyas built a distinctive administrative and cultural order.

**Administration:**
- The **Nayankara system** — the king assigned land/revenue units to **Nayakas** (military chiefs) in return for maintaining troops and administering territory (a precursor to the Vijayanagara Nayankara/Amaranayaka system).
- The realm was divided into provinces/**nadus/sthala**; officials collected revenue; strong fort-based defence (Warangal Fort's concentric ramparts).

**Economy:**
- **Tank (cheruvu) irrigation** was the backbone — the Kakatiyas built thousands of **chain tanks** (Pakhal, Ramappa, Laknavaram, Ghanpur) transforming dryland agriculture; this "tank cascade" system is their enduring legacy (Mission Kakatiya revives it).
- **Trade** flourished via **Motupalli port** (Ganapati Deva's **Abhaya Sasanam** charter guaranteed fair duties to foreign merchants); textiles, diamonds (Golconda region), and agrarian surplus.

**Society & religion:**
- Predominantly **Shaiva** (Kakatiyas patronised Shaivism — the **Rudreshwara/Ramappa** temple), with Jain and Vaishnava presence; **Veerashaivism** spread. Relatively **open social order** — even lower-caste warriors rose as Nayakas.

**Architecture (the Kakatiya style):**
- **Star-shaped (stellate) plans**, high **jagati** platforms, intricately carved **black basalt/dolerite** pillars, **madanika/nagini** bracket figures, and the famous **floating bricks** of Ramappa's shikhara.
- Monuments: **Ramappa (Rudreshwara) Temple** (UNESCO 2021), **Thousand Pillar Temple (Hanamkonda)**, **Warangal Fort & Kakatiya Kala Thoranam** (the state emblem's gateway), **Ghanpur temple complex**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Kakatiya: **Nayankara** administration; **tank (cheruvu) irrigation** economy (Pakhal/Laknavaram); **Motupalli** trade (Abhaya Sasanam); **Shaiva** patronage; architecture = **stellate plans, carved basalt pillars, floating bricks (Ramappa)**, Kala Thoranam.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **Nayankara system** is Kakatiya (later refined by Vijayanagara). Their signature was **tank irrigation** and the **Kala Thoranam** (state emblem). Ramappa's **floating bricks** and stellate plan are the architectural highlights.

## 🇮🇳 Indian National Movement: Revolutionary Movement Phase I

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **first phase of revolutionary nationalism** (c. 1900s-1910s) arose alongside the Swadeshi movement, when militant youth turned to **individual heroic action** (assassinations, dacoities for funds, secret societies).

**Bengal:**
- **Anushilan Samiti** and **Jugantar** — secret revolutionary societies (Barindra Kumar Ghosh, Bhupendranath Datta).
- **Alipore Bomb Case (1908)** — after the **Muzaffarpur bombing** (Khudiram Bose & Prafulla Chaki targeted Magistrate Kingsford, 1908; **Khudiram** hanged, Chaki shot himself).
- **Rash Behari Bose** & **Sachindra Nath Sanyal** — the **Delhi Conspiracy (1912)** bomb on Viceroy **Hardinge**.

**Maharashtra:** the **Chapekar brothers** (Damodar Hari Chapekar) assassinated Rand (Plague Commissioner) in **Pune (1897)** — an early act.

**Punjab:** unrest around the 1907 agrarian agitation; later the **Ghadar** activity.

**Abroad:**
- **India House (London)** — **Shyamji Krishna Varma**; **V. D. Savarkar** (wrote *The Indian War of Independence 1857*); **Madan Lal Dhingra** assassinated Curzon-Wyllie (London, 1909).
- **Madame Bhikaji Cama** — unfurled an early version of the Indian flag at **Stuttgart (1907)**.
- **Ghadar Party (1913, San Francisco)** — **Lala Har Dayal, Sohan Singh Bhakna**; planned an armed uprising (the failed **Ghadar/1915 mutiny** plot during WWII), newspaper *Ghadar*.

The movement was **suppressed** but inspired later revolutionaries (HRA/HSRA — phase II).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Phase I: Bengal (**Anushilan/Jugantar**, **Alipore Bomb Case 1908**, **Khudiram Bose**), Maharashtra (**Chapekar brothers 1897**), abroad (**India House/Savarkar/Dhingra**, **Bhikaji Cama Stuttgart 1907**, **Ghadar Party 1913 SF — Har Dayal**).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Khudiram Bose & Prafulla Chaki** → Muzaffarpur (1908, Alipore case). **Ghadar Party 1913, San Francisco** (Har Dayal). **Bhikaji Cama** unfurled the flag at **Stuttgart (1907)**. Phase I = individual/heroic action (pre-HRA).

## 🗺️ Telangana Geography: Area Focus — Khammam & Bhadradri

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Khammam** and **Bhadradri Kothagudem** form the **south-eastern** part of Telangana, bordering Andhra Pradesh and Chhattisgarh — a forested, mineral-rich, Godavari-side region.

**Physiography & rivers:** the **Godavari** flows along/through the region (at **Bhadrachalam**), with tributaries **Kinnerasani, Munneru, Sabari (Savari)**. Hilly and heavily **forested** (part of the Dandakaranya belt); the **Kinnerasani Wildlife Sanctuary** is here. Fertile Godavari-side plains grow **paddy, chilli, cotton, tobacco**.

**Economy:**
- **Coal** — **Kothagudem** & **Yellandu** are major **Singareni (SCCL)** coalfields; **Kothagudem Thermal Power Station (KTPS)** is a large coal-fired plant.
- **Chilli** — Khammam has one of Asia's large **chilli/mirchi markets**.
- Paper (Sirpur — actually Adilabad), heavy water/irrigation from Godavari.

**Heritage & culture:**
- **Bhadrachalam** — the famous **Sri Sita Ramachandra Swamy temple** on the Godavari; associated with **Kancherla Gopanna (Bhakta Ramadasu)**, who built the temple in the 17th century; **Sri Rama Navami** kalyanam is grand here.
- **Parnasala** (near Bhadrachalam) — linked to the Ramayana (Sita's abduction site by tradition).
- Tribal (Koya) heartland; **Kinnerasani** reservoir/sanctuary.

Note: **Bhadrachalam** (and 7 mandals) were transferred to Telangana at bifurcation (2014) for the Polavaram project context.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Khammam/Bhadradri: SE Telangana, **Godavari** (Bhadrachalam), tributaries **Kinnerasani/Munneru/Sabari**; **Singareni coal (Kothagudem/Yellandu)** + **KTPS**; **chilli market**; **Bhadrachalam Rama temple** (Ramadasu); Koya tribal belt; Kinnerasani sanctuary.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Bhadrachalam** (Rama temple, Godavari, Ramadasu) is in **Bhadradri Kothagudem**. **Kothagudem/Yellandu** = Singareni coal + **KTPS**. Khammam = major **chilli** market. Sabari/Kinnerasani are Godavari tributaries here.
