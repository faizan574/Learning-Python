## 🏛️ Polity: Parliament — Composition, Sessions, Bills & Committees

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Parliament (Art 79)** = **President + Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha**.

**Lok Sabha (House of the People):** max strength **552** (530 states + 20 UTs + earlier 2 nominated Anglo-Indians, now discontinued by the **104th Amendment**). Directly elected; term **5 years** (extendable during Emergency). Presided by the **Speaker**.

**Rajya Sabha (Council of States):** max **250** (238 elected by state/UT assemblies via **PR-STV** + 12 nominated by the President for art/science/literature/social service). A **permanent** house (not dissolved); **1/3 retire every 2 years**; member term **6 years**. Chaired by the **Vice-President**.

**Sessions:** Budget, Monsoon, Winter. The gap between two sessions **cannot exceed 6 months**. **Quorum = 1/10** of the total members. Key devices: **Question Hour, Zero Hour, Adjournment/Calling Attention motions, No-Confidence motion (LS only)**.

**Bills:**
- **Ordinary bills** — either House; disagreement resolved by a **joint sitting** (Art 108).
- **Money bills (Art 110)** — only in **Lok Sabha** (on President's recommendation); RS can only **recommend** changes (returns within **14 days**), LS may accept/reject; **Speaker certifies** a money bill. **No joint sitting** for money bills.
- **Financial bills** and **Constitutional Amendment bills (Art 368)** — special procedures.

**Committees:** **Public Accounts Committee (PAC)**, **Estimates Committee**, **Committee on Public Undertakings (COPU)** (the three **financial committees**); **Departmentally Related Standing Committees (DRSCs)**; Business Advisory, Privileges, etc. The **PAC** examines the **CAG's** audit reports (chaired conventionally by an **Opposition** member; the Estimates Committee has **only Lok Sabha** members).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Parliament = President + LS + RS. **Money bill (Art 110)** → LS only, Speaker certifies, RS 14-day recommendatory, **no joint sitting**. Ordinary bill disagreement → **joint sitting (Art 108)**. RS: permanent, 1/3 retire every 2 yrs, VP chairs. Financial committees: PAC, Estimates, COPU.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Money bills** originate **only in the Lok Sabha** and have **no joint sitting**; the **Speaker** certifies them. The **Estimates Committee** has **only LS members**; the **PAC** examines **CAG** reports.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Which House can a money bill originate in, and who certifies it?* → Only the **Lok Sabha**; the **Speaker** certifies it.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- LS max 552; RS max 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated).
- Money bill: Art 110, LS only, no joint sitting.
- Joint sitting (Art 108) — ordinary bills only.
- Financial committees: PAC (CAG reports), Estimates (LS only), COPU.

## 🏰 Telangana History: The Kakatiyas — Political History

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **Kakatiyas (c. 1163-1323 CE)** were the great Telugu dynasty that unified much of the Telugu-speaking region with **Orugallu (Warangal)** as capital. They began as **feudatories of the Kalyani (Western) Chalukyas** and became independent under Prataparudra I.

**Key rulers:**
- **Beta Raja I / early chiefs** — founders (from Kazipet/Hanamkonda area).
- **Prola Raja II** — expanded power.
- **Rudradeva (Prataparudra I)** — declared independence (~1163); built the **Thousand Pillar Temple, Hanamkonda**; wrote **Niti Sara** (Sanskrit).
- **Mahadeva** — brief reign.
- **Ganapati Deva (1199-1262)** — the **greatest** Kakatiya; longest reign; unified Telugu lands; encouraged trade (**Motupalli port**; the **Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam** / charter protecting traders); his general **Recharla Rudra** built the **Ramappa temple (1213)**.
- **Rani Rudrama Devi (1262-1289)** — one of India's few ruling **queens**; ruled ably (took the male title *Rudradeva Maharaja*); the traveller **Marco Polo** is said to have praised her administration; repelled invasions.
- **Prataparudra II (1289-1323)** — the last; faced **Delhi Sultanate** invasions (**Malik Kafur** under Alauddin Khalji extracted tribute, incl. surrender of the **Koh-i-Noor** per tradition); finally defeated by **Ulugh Khan (Muhammad bin Tughlaq)** in **1323**, ending the dynasty; Warangal was annexed.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Kakatiyas: capital **Orugallu (Warangal)**; **Ganapati Deva** (greatest, Motupalli charter), **Rudrama Devi** (ruling queen, Marco Polo), **Prataparudra II** (last, defeated 1323 by Ulugh Khan). Rudradeva → Thousand Pillar Temple; Recharla Rudra → Ramappa (1213).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Ganapati Deva** = greatest/longest reign; **Rudrama Devi** = the famous queen (took a male title); **Prataparudra II** = last ruler (defeated **1323**). Kakatiyas started as **Kalyani Chalukya** feudatories.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Which Kakatiya ruler issued the Motupalli charter protecting sea traders?* → **Ganapati Deva** (Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam).

## 🇮🇳 Indian National Movement: Partition of Bengal, Swadeshi & the Extremists (Surat Split)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Partition of Bengal (1905).** Viceroy **Lord Curzon** partitioned Bengal (effective **16 October 1905**), ostensibly for administrative ease but really to **divide Hindus and Muslims** and weaken Bengali nationalism. It triggered massive protest.

**Swadeshi & Boycott Movement (1905-08).** The anti-partition agitation grew into the **Swadeshi (use Indian goods)** and **Boycott (of British goods)** movement — the first mass phase with **self-reliance** (swadeshi enterprises, national education — **Bengal National College** with Aurobindo; **Rakhi** by Tagore as unity symbol; *Vande Mataram* as a rallying song). It spread beyond Bengal (Tilak in Maharashtra, Lajpat Rai in Punjab, Chidambaram Pillai in the south).

**The Extremists (Lal-Bal-Pal):** **Lala Lajpat Rai, Bal Gangadhar Tilak ("Swaraj is my birthright"), Bipin Chandra Pal** — plus **Aurobindo Ghosh** — advocated **swaraj through self-reliance, passive resistance and mass agitation**, rejecting Moderate "mendicancy."

**Surat Split (1907).** At the **Surat session (1907)**, the Congress split between **Moderates** and **Extremists** over the pace/method (and the presidency); the Extremists were sidelined; the party reunited only at **Lucknow (1916)** (Lucknow Pact). The **British repression** and the **Morley-Minto Reforms (1909)** — which introduced **separate electorates for Muslims** — followed.

**Annulment:** Bengal partition was **annulled in 1911** (Delhi Durbar), and the capital shifted from Calcutta to **Delhi**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Partition of Bengal 1905 (Curzon)** → **Swadeshi & Boycott** (first mass phase, national education, Vande Mataram). **Extremists = Lal-Bal-Pal** (+ Aurobindo); Tilak's "**Swaraj is my birthright**." **Surat Split 1907**; partition **annulled 1911** (capital → Delhi).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Bengal was partitioned by **Curzon (1905)** and **annulled 1911**. The **Surat Split (1907)** was Moderates vs Extremists; **separate electorates** came via **Morley-Minto (1909)**. "Swaraj is my birthright" = **Tilak**.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Who partitioned Bengal and in which year was it annulled?* → **Lord Curzon (1905)**; annulled in **1911**.

### 🎯 Exam Pointers
- Partition of Bengal: Curzon, 16 Oct 1905; annulled 1911.
- Swadeshi/Boycott: national education, Vande Mataram.
- Extremists: Lal-Bal-Pal + Aurobindo; Tilak's slogan.
- Surat Split 1907; Morley-Minto 1909 (separate electorates).

## 🗺️ Telangana Geography: Area Focus — Karimnagar & Jagtial

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Karimnagar (Elgandal)** and **Jagtial** form a north-central belt on the **Godavari** system, historically the **Satavahana heartland** (Kotilingala) and a rich agricultural/granite region.

**Physiography & rivers:** the **Godavari** and tributaries (**Manair, Maner**) drain the area; the **Lower Manair Dam (LMD)** near Karimnagar and **Mid Manair** (part of Kaleshwaram) are key reservoirs; **Sri Ram Sagar (SRSP)** command extends here. Fertile black/red soils support **paddy** (the belt is a granary), maize, cotton.

**Economy:** famous for **granite** (Karimnagar black/tan granite — a major export); **Karimnagar silver filigree** (GI craft); **poultry** (a leading egg-producing belt); **Sircilla** (Rajanna Sircilla, carved out) is a **textile/powerloom** hub (also **Sircilla** known for the weavers' distress historically). Ramagundam (nearby, Peddapalli) has **NTPC** and Singareni coal.

**Heritage:** **Kotilingala** (early Satavahana site, Jagtial); **Elgandal Fort** and **Dharmapuri** (Narasimha temple on the Godavari) in Jagtial; **Vemulawada** (Rajanna Sircilla, carved from Karimnagar) — Raja Rajeshwara temple; **Kondagattu** Anjaneya temple (Jagtial).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Karimnagar-Jagtial: **Godavari** belt, **Kotilingala** (Satavahana), **granite** & **silver filigree (GI)**, **poultry**; **LMD/Mid Manair** reservoirs; **Sircilla** textiles; heritage — **Dharmapuri, Kondagattu, Elgandal, Vemulawada** (nearby).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Kotilingala** (early Satavahana coins) is in **Jagtial** (this belt). **Karimnagar** = granite + **silver filigree** + poultry. **Vemulawada** and **Sircilla** are in **Rajanna Sircilla** (carved from Karimnagar).

### 💡 Example
