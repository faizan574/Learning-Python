## 🏛️ Subject 1 — Polity: Preamble and its Keywords

### Deep Dive

The **Preamble** is the **introduction/preface** to the Constitution, based on the **Objectives Resolution** drafted by Nehru. It declares the **source of authority, the nature of the state, its objectives, and the date of adoption**.

**Text (paraphrased):** "**We, the People of India**, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a **Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic** and to secure to all its citizens — **Justice** (social, economic, political), **Liberty** (of thought, expression, belief, faith, worship), **Equality** (of status and opportunity), and to promote **Fraternity** assuring the **dignity of the individual** and the **unity and integrity of the Nation**…"

**Keywords decoded:**
- **Sovereign** — free of external control; **Socialist** & **Secular** & **Integrity** were **added by the `42nd Amendment (1976)`**.
- **Democratic** — government by the people (universal adult franchise); **Republic** — an **elected head of state** (no hereditary monarch).
- **Justice–Liberty–Equality–Fraternity** — inspired by the ideals of the **French Revolution**; Fraternity added the **dignity of the individual** and **unity & integrity** of the nation.

**Legal status.** In **Berubari (1960)** the SC held the Preamble was **not part** of the Constitution. In **Kesavananda Bharati (1973)** the Court reversed this — the Preamble **is part** of the Constitution and can be **amended** under Art 368, but **not** so as to destroy its **basic structure**. The Preamble is **non-justiciable** (not enforceable in court).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — The `42nd Amendment (1976)` added **Socialist, Secular, and Integrity**. **Kesavananda Bharati (1973)**: the Preamble **is part** of the Constitution and amendable within the **basic-structure** limit.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The Preamble is **non-justiciable** and is **not a source of power** (nor a limitation on it). "Socialist/Secular" were **not** in the original 1949 text — they came in **1976**.

💡 **Example:** The word **"Republic"** distinguishes India from constitutional monarchies (e.g., the UK) — the head of state (President) is **elected**, not hereditary; a favourite one-liner PYQ.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Order of keywords: **Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic**.
- 42nd Amendment (1976) added **Socialist, Secular, Integrity**.
- Berubari (1960): not part → Kesavananda (1973): part, amendable but basic structure protected.
- Preamble: non-justiciable; ideals of **Justice–Liberty–Equality–Fraternity**.

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Which amendment added "Secular" to the Preamble?* → the **42nd Amendment, 1976**.

**Praja Palana.** After the December 2023 change of government, the state launched **Praja Palana** — a **mass application drive** to collect citizens' applications for the **Six Guarantees**, held at village/ward level so eligible people could enrol for the promised welfare schemes. It served as the enrolment gateway for schemes like Mahalakshmi, Rythu Bharosa, Gruha Jyothi, etc. *(Verify latest application counts and coverage.)*

**Praja Vani.** A **grievance-redressal programme** — a fixed weekly day (commonly Mondays) when citizens submit petitions at the **Secretariat / collectorates / CMO**, meant to make governance more accessible and responsive. *(Verify current schedule and scope.)*

**Administrative changes.** The new government made several symbolic and structural changes:
- **Renamings** of schemes and institutions, and revival of the state's **Formation Day** observance.

## 🏰 Subject 3 — Telangana History: Mulki Rules & the 1969 Jai Telangana Agitation

### Deep Dive

**Mulki rules.** "**Mulki**" means a **local/native** of the erstwhile Hyderabad State. The rules — traceable to a **Nizam-era firman (1919)** — reserved government jobs for those with a defined **local domicile** (a residency requirement). After the 1956 merger, the **Gentlemen's Agreement** promised to protect Telangana's employment share through such safeguards.

**The 1969 agitation.** Grievances that **non-mulkis** (from the Andhra region) were holding jobs meant for Telangana locals, plus **diversion of surplus funds** and weak safeguards, sparked a mass movement in **1969** demanding **implementation of safeguards and/or a separate Telangana**:
- It began among **students and government employees** in early 1969 and spread across the region.
- **Marri Chenna Reddy** formed the **Telangana Praja Samithi (TPS)** to lead the movement.
- The agitation turned **violent**; a large number of people (often cited as around **`300`+**, *verify the exact figure*) died in police action.
- The demand was for enforcement of the Gentlemen's Agreement / Mulki rules and, increasingly, **statehood**.

**Aftermath.** The **Supreme Court upheld the validity of the Mulki rules (1972)**, which in turn triggered a counter-agitation in Andhra ("Jai Andhra," 1972-73). To resolve the deadlock, the **Six-Point Formula (1973)** was adopted (abolishing Mulki rules) and **Article 371-D** (with a **Presidential Order on zonal/local cadres**) was introduced via the **32nd Amendment (1973)**.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Mulki rules** = local-domicile job reservation (Nizam-era firman `1919`); the **1969 Jai Telangana agitation** was led by **Marri Chenna Reddy's Telangana Praja Samithi (TPS)**. SC upheld Mulki rules in **1972**; **Six-Point Formula & Art 371-D** followed in **1973**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **1969** movement was for **safeguards/statehood within** the framework, decades before the **2001-2014** statehood movement — don't merge them. **Mulki rules were later abolished** by the Six-Point Formula (1973), replaced by Art 371-D local-cadre protections.

💡 **Example:** The chain **Gentlemen's Agreement (1956) → violations → 1969 TPS agitation → 1972 SC verdict → Six-Point Formula/Art 371-D (1973)** is a classic sequence PYQ.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Mulki = local domicile; origin Nizam firman **1919**.
- 1969 agitation → **TPS** (Marri Chenna Reddy).
- SC upheld Mulki rules **1972**; Jai Andhra counter-stir 1972-73.
- **Six-Point Formula (1973)** + **Art 371-D** (32nd Amendment).

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Who led the Telangana Praja Samithi in 1969?* → **Marri Chenna Reddy**.

## 🇮🇳 Subject 4 — Indian National Movement: Subhas Bose & the INA; RIN Revolt

### Deep Dive

**Subhas Chandra Bose.** Elected **Congress President** at **Haripura (1938)** and again at **Tripuri (1939)** — but resigned after friction with Gandhi's group and founded the **Forward Bloc (1939)**. Placed under surveillance, he **escaped from India (1941)** to Germany, then travelled to **Southeast Asia (via submarine)**.

**The INA (Azad Hind Fauj).** First raised by **Mohan Singh** and **Rash Behari Bose** with Japanese support (**1942**) from Indian PoWs. **Subhas Bose took command in 1943**, revitalised it, and:
- Proclaimed the **Provisional Government of Free India (Azad Hind)** at **Singapore (Oct 1943)**.
- Gave the slogans **"Dilli Chalo"** and **"Jai Hind"**; addressed as **"Netaji."**
- Formed the **Rani of Jhansi Regiment** (women's unit, led by **Capt. Lakshmi Sahgal**).
- The INA fought alongside the Japanese towards **Imphal-Kohima (1944)** but was pushed back.

**INA Trials (1945-46).** Captured INA officers — **Shah Nawaz Khan, Prem Kumar Sahgal, Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon** — were tried at the **Red Fort**; the defence (including **Bhulabhai Desai, Nehru, Tej Bahadur Sapru**) and huge public sympathy forced the government to commute sentences.

**RIN Revolt (Feb 1946).** Ratings of the **Royal Indian Navy** mutinied in **Bombay** (starting on **HMIS Talwar**) over pay, food and racial discrimination; it spread to other ports before being called off. It signalled that even the **armed forces** were slipping from British control — hastening the decision to leave.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Bose: Congress President **1938 & 1939**, founded **Forward Bloc (1939)**; **Provisional Govt of Free India** at **Singapore (Oct 1943)**; INA trials at the **Red Fort (1945-46)**; **RIN revolt Feb 1946** (HMIS Talwar, Bombay).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — The **INA was first formed by Mohan Singh/Rash Behari Bose (1942)**; **Subhas Bose took over in 1943** (he didn't found it). Netaji's death is officially attributed to a **plane crash at Taipei (18 Aug 1945)** — *widely debated; state it as the official version.*

💡 **Example:** The **Red Fort INA trials** and the **RIN revolt** together are the classic "post-war mass upsurge" that made continued British rule untenable — a frequent PYQ pairing.

🎯 **Exam Pointers:**
- Bose: Haripura 1938, Tripuri 1939, Forward Bloc 1939.
- INA: Mohan Singh/Rash Behari Bose 1942 → Bose 1943; Azad Hind at Singapore Oct 1943.
- Rani of Jhansi Regiment — Lakshmi Sahgal.
- INA trials Red Fort 1945-46; RIN revolt Feb 1946 (Bombay).

✅ **Quick self-check:** *Where did Bose proclaim the Provisional Government of Free India?* → **Singapore (October 1943)**.

## 🗺️ Subject 5 — Telangana Geography: Irrigation Projects (Major, Medium & Lift Schemes)

### Deep Dive

Telangana, a largely **upland, drought-prone** state, depends heavily on **irrigation projects** to lift and store river water. Projects are classified by **command area**: **major** (`> 10,000 ha`), **medium** (`2,000–10,000 ha`), and **minor** (`< 2,000 ha`), *(verify current thresholds/definitions)*.

**Godavari-basin projects:**
- **Kaleshwaram Lift Irrigation Scheme (KLIS)** — a giant **multi-stage lift** scheme on the **Godavari**, with the **Medigadda (Laxmi), Annaram and Sundilla** barrages; designed to lift water across districts. *(Note: parts, e.g. the Medigadda barrage, faced structural issues — verify current status.)*
- **Sri Ram Sagar Project (SRSP)** at **Pochampad, Nizamabad** — a major Godavari project.
- **Nizam Sagar** (on the **Manjira**), **Singur** (Manjira), **Kadem, Sripada Yellampalli**.

**Krishna-basin projects:**
- **Nagarjuna Sagar** (on the **Krishna**, shared) — one of the largest masonry dams.
- **Priyadarshini Jurala (Jurala)** — the **first major project on the Krishna in Telangana** (Mahabubnagar).
- **Palamuru–Rangareddy Lift Irrigation Scheme** — lifts Krishna (Srisailam backwater) to the parched **Palamuru** region.
- **Srisailam** (hydro-electric + irrigation, shared).

**Lift irrigation** is the state's hallmark, moving water **uphill** to command areas above river level — energy-intensive but essential given the terrain.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **KLIS & SRSP** are on the **Godavari**; **Nagarjuna Sagar, Jurala & Palamuru-Rangareddy** are on the **Krishna**. **Jurala** = first major **Krishna** project in Telangana; KLIS uses the **Medigadda/Annaram/Sundilla** barrages.
