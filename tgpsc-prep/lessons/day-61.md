## 🏛️ Polity: Constitutional Bodies (EC, UPSC/TGPSC, CAG, AG, Commissions)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Constitutional bodies** derive their powers directly from the Constitution (as opposed to statutory bodies created by law).

**Election Commission (Art 324).** Independent body for conducting elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, President & Vice-President. A **multi-member body** (CEC + Election Commissioners); appointed by the President; CEC removed like a **Supreme Court judge** (impeachment-style); ECs removed on CEC's recommendation.

**UPSC (Art 315-323) / State PSC.** Recruits for All-India & central services (UPSC) and state services (**TGPSC** for Telangana). Chairman & members appointed by the President (Governor for SPSC); removed by the **President** only (even for SPSC), on grounds of misbehaviour after SC inquiry. Advisory functions; submits an annual report.

**CAG (Art 148).** The **Comptroller & Auditor General** — "guardian of the public purse"; audits all Union and State accounts; reports to the President/Governor (laid before the legislature); the **PAC** examines CAG reports. Appointed by the President; removed like an SC judge. **Dr. Ambedkar** called it one of the most important officers.

**Attorney General (Art 76).** The **highest law officer** of the Union; advises the government; appointed by the President (qualified to be an SC judge); holds office during pleasure. (State equivalent: **Advocate General, Art 165**.)

**Commissions for SC/ST/BC:**
- **National Commission for SCs (Art 338)**, **STs (Art 338A)**, **Backward Classes (Art 338B — added by 102nd Amendment, 2018)** — constitutional bodies safeguarding these groups.
- **Finance Commission (Art 280)** — quasi-permanent, every 5 years.

**Contrast — non-constitutional/statutory bodies:** NITI Aayog, NHRC, CIC, Lokpal, CVC (created by law/executive order, not the Constitution).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — Constitutional bodies: **EC (Art 324)**, **UPSC/SPSC (315)**, **CAG (148, guardian of public purse)**, **AG (76)**, **SC/ST/BC Commissions (338/338A/338B)**, **Finance Commission (280)**. Statutory (not constitutional): NITI Aayog, NHRC, CIC, Lokpal, CVC.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **CAG = Art 148** (audits, guardian of the purse; PAC examines its reports). **NBCC/BC Commission = Art 338B (102nd Amendment)**. **NITI Aayog, NHRC, CVC, Lokpal are NOT constitutional** (statutory/executive). SPSC members are removed only by the **President** (not Governor).

## 🎁 Telangana Schemes: Cheyutha Pensions & Rajiv Aarogyasri (Round 5)

### 📖 Deep Dive

The **Cheyutha** guarantee covers **social-security pensions** and **health**:

1. **Cheyutha / Aasara pensions** — monthly pensions for vulnerable groups: **old-age, widows, persons with disability (differently-abled), beedi workers, single women, HIV+ (ART) patients, weavers, toddy tappers, filaria patients**, etc. Manifesto raised amounts (commonly cited ~**₹4,000/month** for old-age/widow etc., higher for the disabled — verify latest sanctioned amounts and the qualifying **age**, both revised over time). Delivered through the earlier **Aasara** framework.

2. **Rajiv Aarogyasri** — the flagship **cashless health-insurance** scheme for eligible (BPL) families, covering hospitalisation and specified procedures at **empanelled hospitals**; coverage enhanced under successive governments (commonly cited up to **₹5 lakh/family/year**, with reports of moves to higher limits — verify latest cap and package list). Complements the central **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)**.

**Context:** Cheyutha addresses **income security in vulnerability** and **catastrophic-health-expenditure** protection — a "helping hand." Compare with central **NSAP** (pensions) and **Ayushman Bharat** (health).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Cheyutha** = monthly **Aasara pensions** (old-age/widow/PwD/beedi/weaver/toddy etc.; amounts revised — verify) + **Rajiv Aarogyasri** (cashless health cover, up to ~₹5 lakh, verify). Central counterparts: **NSAP** (pensions), **Ayushman Bharat** (health).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Pension amounts and the eligibility **age** have changed across governments — always "verify latest." **Aarogyasri** is a **state** health scheme (distinct from central Ayushman Bharat, though overlapping). Cheyutha = pensions + health (not housing/travel).

## 🏰 Telangana History: Socio-Cultural Awakening (Andhra Mahasabha, Library Movement, Press)

### 📖 Deep Dive

Under the Nizam, a **socio-cultural and political awakening** grew in the Telangana region (early-mid 20th century), asserting Telugu identity and reform against feudal/autocratic conditions.

**Library Movement (Grandhalaya Udyamam).** From the early 1900s, libraries became centres of Telugu literacy, awareness and mobilisation. The first Telugu library, **Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam (Hyderabad, 1901)**, and **Rajaraja Narendra Andhra Bhasha Nilayam (Hanamkonda, 1904)** were pioneers; **Komarraju Lakshmana Rao** was a key figure (Vignana Chandrika Mandali). Libraries spread Telugu books and nationalist ideas despite official suspicion.

**The Press.** **Golconda Patrika** (edited by **Suravaram Pratapa Reddy**) was a leading Telugu newspaper voicing Telangana concerns; **Meezan** and others also featured. The press countered the dominance of Urdu and raised social/political awareness.

**Andhra Mahasabha (1930).** The premier socio-political organisation of Telugu people in Hyderabad State; its **first session (1930) at Jogipet (Medak)** was presided over by **Suravaram Pratapa Reddy**. It initially raised **cultural/linguistic and civic demands** (Telugu in administration/education, civil rights) and progressively became **political**, later radicalising (a communist-influenced wing led it toward the **Telangana Armed Struggle**). Related: **Andhra Jana Sangham (1921/1922)** and **Andhra Maha Sabha** evolution; the **Andhra Saraswata Parishat**.

**Nizam Rashtra Andhra Jana Sangham → Andhra Mahasabha** channelled Telugu identity; alongside, the **Arya Samaj** and **Hindu-Muslim** reform currents and the **Vandemataram Movement (1938, Osmania University students)** marked the awakening.

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Library Movement**: Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam (1901), Komarraju Lakshmana Rao. **Golconda Patrika** (Suravaram Pratapa Reddy). **Andhra Mahasabha (1930, first session Jogipet, presided by Suravaram Pratapa Reddy)** — cultural→political; **Vandemataram Movement 1938** (Osmania).

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Andhra Mahasabha (1930)** first session at **Jogipet (Medak)**, Suravaram Pratapa Reddy presiding. **Golconda Patrika** = Suravaram Pratapa Reddy. The **Library Movement** (1901 first library) predated and fed the political awakening. **Vandemataram Movement (1938)** was at Osmania University.

### 💡 Example

*Q: Who presided over the first Andhra Mahasabha (1930) and edited Golconda Patrika?* → **Suravaram Pratapa Reddy**.

- Golconda Patrika — Suravaram Pratapa Reddy.

- Vandemataram Movement 1938 (Osmania University).

## 🇮🇳 Indian National Movement: Simon Commission, Nehru Report & Lahore Session (Round 4)

### 📖 Deep Dive

**Simon Commission (1927).** The Indian Statutory Commission, appointed to review the 1919 Act, was **all-white (7 British members, no Indian)** → boycotted nationwide with **"Simon Go Back."** Reached India **3 Feb 1928**. During a Lahore protest, **Lala Lajpat Rai** was injured in a lathi charge (Oct 1928) and died (17 Nov 1928).

**Nehru Report (1928).** The All-Parties Conference response, chaired by **Motilal Nehru**: proposed **Dominion Status**, **rejection of separate electorates**, a **bill of fundamental rights**, and linguistic provinces. Opposed by **Jinnah** (countered with his **Fourteen Points, 1929**) and by younger leaders (**Jawaharlal Nehru, Bose**) who wanted **complete independence**.

**Lahore Session (December 1929).** Presided by **Jawaharlal Nehru**; the Congress adopted **Purna Swaraj (complete independence)** as its goal, resolved that **26 January 1930** be the first **Independence Day (Purna Swaraj Day)**, and authorised **Civil Disobedience**. The tricolour was unfurled on the Ravi's banks (31 Dec 1929).

**Significance:** the shift from **Dominion Status (Nehru Report 1928)** to **Purna Swaraj (Lahore 1929)** marked the Congress's decisive turn to full independence, setting up the Civil Disobedience Movement (1930).

> 💎 **KEY FACT** — **Simon 1927** (all-white, "Simon Go Back", reached India 3 Feb 1928; Lajpat Rai injured). **Nehru Report 1928** (Motilal Nehru, Dominion Status, no separate electorates). **Lahore 1929** (J. Nehru president) → **Purna Swaraj**; first Independence Day **26 Jan 1930**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — **Nehru Report (1928)** = **Motilal** Nehru, **Dominion Status**; **Lahore (1929)** = **Purna Swaraj** (full independence). **Jinnah's Fourteen Points (1929)** answered the Nehru Report. Simon reached India **3 Feb 1928**.

## 🗺️ Telangana Geography: Telangana at a Glance (Round 4)

### 📖 Deep Dive

*(Round 4 revisits the foundational geography overview — verify latest official figures.)*

**Formation & location.** Telangana became India's **29th state on 2 June 2014**. It lies on the **Deccan Plateau** in **south-central India**, roughly between **~15.8°N–19.9°N** and **77°E–81.3°E** — a **landlocked** state.

**Extent & boundaries.** Area **~112,077 km²** (about 3.4% of India; ~11th-12th largest state — verify). Bordered by **Maharashtra** (N/NW), **Chhattisgarh** (NE), **Karnataka** (W), and **Andhra Pradesh** (S/E).

**Physiography.** Almost entirely **plateau** (part of the Deccan/Telangana plateau), gently sloping **south/south-east**; elevations ~**300-600 m**; granite-gneiss terrain with residual hills (Deccan traps in the NW). Two main river basins: **Godavari (~79%)** and **Krishna (~21%)** drain the state (both flow generally SE/E to the Bay of Bengal).

**Administrative.** Reorganised from 10 to **33 districts** (verify current count). Capital **Hyderabad**. Highest point often cited as **Doli Gutta (~965 m)** in the north-east (Kumram Bheem/near Chhattisgarh border — verify).

**Climate & vegetation** (recap): tropical semi-arid to sub-humid; SW-monsoon dominated (~80% rainfall); dry-deciduous teak forests (north). Three erstwhile "regions" concept: (the state's own regions/districts).

> 💎 **KEY FACT (verify)** — Telangana: **29th state, 2 Jun 2014**; **Deccan plateau**, landlocked; area **~112,077 km²**; borders **Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, AP**; drained by **Godavari (~79%) & Krishna (~21%)**; **33 districts** (verify); capital **Hyderabad**; highest point ~**Doli Gutta**.

> ⚠️ **TRAP ALERT** — Telangana is **landlocked** (no coast) and entirely **plateau**. **Godavari** drains the larger share (~79%) vs Krishna (~21%). It borders **four** states (Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh). Area **~1.12 lakh km²**.
